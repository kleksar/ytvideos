Ответ: Create a cohesive Paper Detour-style handmade paper diorama scene, vertical 9:16, showing the same two Triassic animals consistently across the series: Thrinaxodon as a small ochre-brown four-legged predator with visible teeth, and Broomistega as a young gray-green elongated amphibian with a tail. Keep both animals recognizable, naturalistic, and not anthropomorphized. Set every scene inside the same burrow layout, with the entrance at the upper left and the chamber at the lower right. Warm layered handmade paper textures, tactile cut-paper depth, soft cinematic shadows, clear readable silhouettes, one complete composition per frame. No letters, no captions, no blood, no gore.

Применение: сцены снаружи и крупные планы не обязаны показывать всю нору.
