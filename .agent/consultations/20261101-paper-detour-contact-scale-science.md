## Completed visible answer — substantive transcription

Scale covers eight decimal orders, 10^-2 to 10^-10 m. It is scientifically plausible as a sequence of representations, not nine literal photographs of the same point. Numbers represent horizontal field width, not finger separation.

| Field width | Plausible content |
|---|---|
| 1 cm | Contacting fingertip pads and contact line |
| 1 mm | A few fingerprint ridges, hundreds of micrometres wide |
| 100 µm | Part of a ridge, microrelief of apposed surfaces |
| 10 µm | Microrelief and fragments of flattened surface corneocytes |
| 1 µm | Small protrusions and depressions of a corneocyte surface |
| 100 nm | Nanoscale surface topography, investigated with AFM |
| 10 nm | Molecular constituents, fragments of protein/lipid structures |
| 1 nm | Small molecular groups and atomic arrangement, scientific model |
| 0.1 nm | Local electron density, conditional model |

Lipid structures with periods around 6 and 13 nm exist between skin cells; do not transplant internal structures into the external finger interface. Each step divides field width by ten. Subtracting a fixed centimetre fails below that scale. Optical microscopy can resolve details down to roughly 200 nm; field width and resolution are different. SEM/TEM/AFM and other methods extend observations. Atomic features are accessible in suitable samples, but this is not continuous footage of living fingers. Electron clouds represent probability/density, not hard shells; no planetary electron balls. There is no universal fixed contact gap. Contact depends on roughness, pressure and moisture; atomic interactions involve electromagnetism and quantum mechanics. Quantum is not a separate spatial realm.

Suggested sources: Frontiers DOI 10.3389/fmech.2022.1074393; PMC10591027; Open University A tour of the cell section 2.1; Purdue What Is An Atomic Orbital; University of Arizona The Normal Force.
