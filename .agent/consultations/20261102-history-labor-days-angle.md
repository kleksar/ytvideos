## Coordinator's independent check

Allen defines the welfare ratio as assumed annual earnings divided by the poverty-line basket cost, with annual earnings calculated at 250 daily wages. Therefore, dividing 250 by a ratio gives a derived number of daily-wage equivalents for the basket; it is not an observed number of working days. The cited ratios and the 3.2→7.1 g/day, 0.45→1.25 CPI reversal match Allen's tables and discussion. The fifteenth-century statement remains qualitative because Allen does not give this ledger a precise welfare ratio for that interval.
