### Limits and conclusion

English query text does not prove demand for English-language videos. Alternative terms such as `history of India` and `Indian history` can produce different results; educational and exam searches may not indicate demand for documentary storytelling; Google Trends search activity does not capture Home/Suggested viewing; combined channel country share does not establish interest in any historical topic.

The consultant also reported that Google Trends API access was limited to a group of alpha testers as of the check; this needs independent source verification before relying on it.

Status: global query ranking — insufficient data; country differences — insufficient data; 12-month growth — insufficient data; specific demand for English historical videos — not established. This is a measurement-access limitation, not evidence of no demand.
