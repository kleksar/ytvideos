In 1932, Australian soldiers waited beside a dam with machine guns. Their targets were emus.

The birds were moving through Western Australia’s wheat country, and farmers were struggling to protect their crops. What followed was a campaign of scattered flocks, a jammed gun and a truck that couldn’t keep up.
