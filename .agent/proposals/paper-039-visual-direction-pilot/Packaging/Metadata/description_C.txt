For farmers in Western Australia, a damaged wheat field could mean losing a year’s work. Then thousands of emus arrived.

In 1932, the government sent soldiers with machine guns to help. Their campaign became known as the Emu War, but the farmers’ struggle to protect their crops did not end with the army’s departure.
