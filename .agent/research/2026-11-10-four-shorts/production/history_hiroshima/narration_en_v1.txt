Above Hiroshima, an atomic bomb exploded about six hundred meters off the ground.

On August sixth, 1945, an American plane dropped it over the Japanese city. The bomb fell for about three quarters of a minute. Then came a blinding flash.

Heat and a blast wave struck the city. Fires began. People died immediately, and later from injuries, burns, and radiation exposure.
