The assassination of Austria-Hungary's heir did not lead to a world war that same day. A month of decisions lay between them.

In June 1914, Franz Ferdinand and his wife Sophie were killed in Sarajevo. With Germany's support, Austria-Hungary gave Serbia an ultimatum. Then it declared war.

Russia began to mobilize. Germany declared war on Russia and France and invaded Belgium. Britain entered the war too.
