Fingers wrinkle in water, but not simply because skin gets wet. The signal comes from inside your body.

When fingers stay in water, the nervous system causes blood vessels in their tips to narrow. The tissue beneath the skin changes, and the surface folds.

Why? In one experiment, people with wrinkled fingers moved wet marbles faster. But other similar experiments found no such advantage.
