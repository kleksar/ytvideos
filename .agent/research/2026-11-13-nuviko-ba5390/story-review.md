**10m (new)**: questions → two converging timelines → ordinary flight → window ejection → first restraint → loss of autopilot/comms → handoff and trapped legs → controlled diversion/landing → recovery and injuries → evidence at crash site → night-shift replacement → inherited wrong bolt sample → second dark storeroom → physical fit/missed cues → missing independent check → systemic lesson. Weakness: after captain's survival, second maintenance act is less urgent; if viewers only want rescue, interest likely falls at 'The next question was mechanical.'
