## Historical ambiguities and creative guardrails
- Published sources vary in initial headcounts, immediate wreck fatalities and final survivors due to embarkation/desertion/rescue definitions. Script uses no exact conflicting total; 'more than one hundred murdered' is broadly supported.
- One museum account says approximately seven weeks for rescue voyage; another describes 63 days. Script says 'many weeks' without falsely reconciling.
- The first-contact moment is recorded via Pelsaert's journal as Hayes running to him after landing nearby. Narration correctly states Hayes *reached Pelsaert first*, not that he necessarily boarded Sardam first.
- The attack on West Wallabi and two boats are sourced. The staged looks of all people, precise weather and reconstructed camera viewpoints are artist's visual choices, not original eyewitness footage.
- This is a dark historical crime story. Keep killing, sexual violence, torture and capital punishment non-graphic; do not erase that violence occurred or turn it into a spectacle.
