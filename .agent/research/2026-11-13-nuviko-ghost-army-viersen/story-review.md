## Story maps and viewer promises

**02m — direct Short:** Apparent division beside Rhine (specific visual contradiction) → two real divisions secretly leave → 1,100 impostors create rubber props + intercepted radio + sounds → real engineer equipment broadens illusion → actual divisions cross in other sector → captured German overlay has false 79th position and no 30th → limited conclusion. Payoff **before** replay. Cold entry is a concrete, comprehensible paradox; final “That map shows just how convincing the view from across the river could be” semantically introduces “A whole American division seemed to be waiting...” on replay. Author text read twice with both languages, seam plausible **author-only**, not independent; real picture/music/voice seam UNVERIFIED. The original r1 closure was a direct question; new r2 uses a declarative contextual bridge rather than another mandatory question.

**05m — documentary Long:** Opening questions: how hide two divisions and how test deception? → Rhine crossing requires detectable preparations → transfer out of old sectors creates a new concealment problem → secret unit assumes the false identity → props alone insufficient, so radio/sound and real bridge work reinforce them → impersonated traffic posts and civilian removal prevent close inspection of rubber dummies → night crossing yields comparatively light resistance but does not confirm belief → captured order-of-battle map provides new independent-of-performers evidence → limitation and payoff. Main hook develops **after** the necessary explanation of river and divisional movement, before the technical layers. Human line: impostors perform genuine security duties while residents are displaced; the real divisions must stay unseen.

**10m — documentary Long:** Same opening questions, greater strategic dilemma → XVI Corps actual crossing sector vs XIII Corps decoy sector → day/night concealment and precise 30th/79th movement → staging for aerial surveillance, sound and radio → impersonating checkpoints and enforcing restrictions around dummy equipment → documented unsuccessful house-search and real civilian exclusion show the cost of maintaining the illusion → synchronize both sectors until assault → US post-assault reports → German map reveal → scope of evidence/limits → Simpson commendation and unit's final mission → map distinguished from iconic inflatable-tank image. Human line: performers keep a dangerous real front operating behind a falsified one; civilians were also affected. Added factual beats referenced G1/G6, not invented micro-actions. The checkpoint episode is the likeliest interest dip.
