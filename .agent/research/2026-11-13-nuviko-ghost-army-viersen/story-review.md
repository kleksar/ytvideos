## Comparison and choice

r3 02m opened with rubber tank and closed by repeating map/view; r4 uses a sourced truck driver's mistaken conclusion, explains the actual encounter, then expands the same error to German intelligence. The tradeoff is losing some equipment-specific exposition. 05m preserved because the G6 security/civilian section adds a complete human consequence without the long preparation inventory. 10m removes repeated conclusions, adds friendly guard/driver/major reactions as separate evidence and a specific postwar award; no extra invented suspense. 02m → 05m buys strategic explanation, civilian impact and more of the broader army's contribution but loses the immediate human encounter. 05m → 10m adds friendly-side confusion and a later recognition event; p7–11 still slow the central human line and the award comes after the map payoff. 

**Recommendation: 02m Short, editorial only.** It is the most direct completed story for a general viewer: concrete mistake → reason → further documented intelligence mistake. 05m is the stronger conventional Long if context and civilian consequences are the viewer promise. 10m is a viable alternative but weaker pace.
