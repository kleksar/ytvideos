## Specific editorial checks / failure points

- **Hook** S001–S002: Shallow rig tilting → lake itself disappearing; impossible physical contradiction. Audience knows what is wrong before jargon.
- **Setup & false assumption** S003–S004: Salt mine established, crew thinks pilings failed, then sees rig vanish.
- **First human decision** S005–S006: Electrician notices floods & cuts power thrice as evacuation signal.
- **Escalation into rescue** S007–S008: Four miners beyond visibility picked up, everyone reaches lift in four trips.
- **Physical mechanism** S009: Water erodes soluble salt; plausible widening. Should not pretend federal investigator proved drill penetration.
- **Larger consequence** S010–S011: Whirlpool consumes vessels, shore subsides; not just restating rig sank.
- **New reversal** S012–S013: Canal normal outflow reverses, temporary waterfall refills lake.
- **Payoff & ethical limit** S014: Every worker survives, mine destroyed, lake transformed.
- **Residual uncertainty & replay** S015 → S001: Precise trigger not identified; return to visible first sign, without inventing a second disaster.

**First-viewer listening challenge:** Does S001–S002 make sense at zero context? Yes on paper (oil rig vs shallow water); S003 provides place/time quickly. Most likely drop-off at S008/009, where the rescue has already completed and fresh-water chemistry replaces human agency. Shorten or visually convert that transition if independent listeners cannot paraphrase why canal reversing matters. Do not invent a more immediate death threat or pretend an incorrectly positioned drill bit is a proven federal finding.
