## 2026-11-20 — added verified naval decision in the 05m variant

**Actions and chronology:** Governor James Douglas wanted the US soldiers removed from San Juan Island. Captain Geoffrey Hornby declined to attempt a marine landing that might trigger battle, and waited for Rear Admiral Lambert Baynes. On arrival in August 1859, Baynes cancelled the proposed marine landing, while both sides maintained their territorial claims and US reinforcement followed. This choice prevented an opposed landing in that phase; it did NOT settle the treaty dispute or mean peace was achieved without Scott's later negotiation.

**Evidence (re-read 2026-11-20):** National Park Service, [The Pig War](https://home.nps.gov/sajh/learn/historyculture/the-pig-war.htm) (Hornby refusing action while waiting for Baynes; reaction to Douglas); NPS [Griffin Bay](https://www.nps.gov/places/griffin-bay.htm) (governor's invasion instruction overruled by Baynes); NPS [Lambert Baynes](https://www.nps.gov/places/lambert-baynes.htm) (instruction to avoid collision).
