### Transferable observations from Wikipedia, not a detector

- Excessive grandeur or value judgments → replace with observable choice and consequence; preserve documented danger.
- Shallow summaries/transition filler → keep exact physical events, allow listener to infer urgency.
- Repetitive oppositions and formulaic 3-part pacing → vary sentence lengths according to actions and voice rhythm, not arbitrary word bans.
- Fake quotes/imagined private thoughts and slippery sourcing → verify claim ledger, identify reconstruction and true reported dialogue.
- An over-explained moral or mechanical loop → end on specific physical cause and meet a standalone cold-open; both languages must work.

### What changed here

- A explains rescue repeatedly and announces decisions as 'desperate'; B hears seawater, gas, drills, and sees a shirt on a pipe.
- S005 modern classroom/museum explainer replaced by period-appropriate aft-ballast action image brief; historical crew remains a reconstruction, not a documentary screenshot.
- Same 38 crew, valve, approximate depth, aft tanks, steel, 2 passed ships, third ship, white shirt, reported historical quote, rescue, snapped towline and second sinking.
