# Packaging brief — Nuviko History / USS S-5 (1920)

**Format:** English Short 9:16. These are proposals for a future approved film, not generated thumbnails or final release metadata.

## Primary — rescue signal

- **Title:** A White T-Shirt Saved 38 Trapped Sailors #Shorts
- **Thumbnail text:** 38 STILL INSIDE (compare also no-text variant; Nuviko has no blanket mandatory thumbnail text rule).
- **Hero visual:** photoreal 1920 steel submarine S-5 stern projecting only about five metres above a calm grey-blue Atlantic, thin copper pipe with tiny brilliant white cotton shirt, distant merchant steamer. Actual scale anchored by official Navy photo NH 41810. Eye goes white cloth -> dark steel -> ship; no sailors standing outside, no whole submarine vertically floating.
- **First proof of promise:** S001 white T-shirt/underwater trapped sailors. **Payoff:** S012 rescue of 38, S013 empty submarine sinks again. **Misinterpretation to avoid:** T-shirt literally floated the submarine, or all 38 were outside already.

## Alternative — twice sunk

- **Title:** This Submarine Sank Twice. All 38 Men Survived. #Shorts
- **Thumbnail text:** SANK TWICE
- **Composition:** USS Ohio partly visible from period-accurate low sea angle, snapped towline to a collapsing exposed stern at right, no people still inside the submarine. This angle reveals final fate, so the script must retain the interesting 'how'.

## Alternative — improbable escape

- **Title:** They Had to Drill Their Way Out of a Sunken Submarine #Shorts
- **Thumbnail text:** NO WAY OUT
- **Composition:** human hands on an early 1920 metal hand drill biting through a thick wet steel hull, daylight visible through a tiny circular hole beyond. Strong action, but the very first shot of the accepted narration is a shirt above waves, not drilling; check promise-to-first-frame mismatch before selection.

## Channel-specific staging

- Background, objects and historical people photoreal in natural muted color. The distinctive 2D traveler from the Nuviko visual profile appears once as a present-day explainer in S005; neither he nor his costume is part of the historical crew.
- No external paper frame, no flattened cartoon environment, no unverified fake archive; full-frame AI image per unique shot; S014 reuses S001 as established loop.
- Subtitles are separate native words/groups with Nuviko format, not part of source art; precise sizes/crops from current profile only.
- No guaranteed CTR, no derived hidden metrics; compare primary and alternatives qualitatively before final artwork.
- Description concept: In 1920, USS S-5 sank during a practice dive. Thirty-eight men trapped inside raised the stern, cut a hole through steel and waved a white T-shirt to attract a passing ship.
- Suggested hashtags: #Shorts #NavalHistory #Submarine #Survival
