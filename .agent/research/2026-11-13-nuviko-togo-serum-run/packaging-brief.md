# Packaging concept (not approved): Togo and the 1925 Nome serum relay

**Channel** Nuviko History / illustrated English Short 9:16. Proposed only; no rendered art, tested thumbnails, YouTube metadata, or publication.

## Candidate A — default

**Title:** `Togo Led the Hardest Miles of the 1925 Serum Run #Shorts`

**Thumbnail promise:** an old experienced husky crosses dangerous sea ice to help get medicine to sick children. **Visual:** photographic-looking close-up of one dark-brown/black-and-cream, pale-eyed 1925 Siberian husky on blue-white fractured Norton Sound ice in lead harness, authentic wood freight sled and bundled musher small but identifiable behind, precarious dark ocean crevasse beside paws, cold winter daylight. One dominant animal, one meaningful hazard, one distant destination direction. **Optional very short text:** `THE HARDEST MILES` (true, not implying only dog involved). Avoid generic blood-red fake danger, double dog heads and impossible ice fracture.

**First image/line agreement:** S001 depicts lead dog crossing sea ice for medicine. **Full payoff:** medicine actually reaches Nome and both Togo's longer journey and Balto's real final-team contribution are fairly named in S012–S015. **Main risk:** viewer may not know Togo or Balto. Make lead-dog situation itself understandable and emotional before names, but don't manufacture a dying child in foreground.

## Candidate B — counterintuitive last-stage recognition

**Title:** `Balto Became Famous. Another Dog Took the Hardest Route. #Shorts`

**Thumbnail promise:** recognition gap. **Visual:** 1925 Central Park Balto bronze statue in focused foreground, snowy Alaskan sea crossing behind as an artistic but spatially coherent visual contrast on an actual *physical* museum exhibition wall photo display (not a false photo from 1925): two clearly distinct subjects, last-team memorial and first hard journey. **Optional words:** `BEFORE BALTO`.

**First confirmation:** S001 Togo crosses ice, S013 statue arrives only when story earns it; risk of viewers expecting a pure Balto biography. More spoiler, perhaps more obvious attraction to Balto-known audiences.

## Candidate C — rendezvous failure

**Title:** `The Sled Team Almost Missed the Medicine That Would Save Nome #Shorts`

**Thumbnail promise:** two tiny dog-sled teams barely visible to each other across an Arctic whiteout, with one tiny securely wrapped package on the incoming team. **Visual:** historically accurate 1925 Shaktoolik snowy trail, visible enough to read two opposing sled team silhouettes on phone, intense but not deceptive. **Optional words:** `ALMOST MISSED`. It aligns with meaningful middle reversal at S008.

**First confirmation concern:** this problem only arrives at S007–S008, so risk of disconnect with S001 ice opening. Prefer candidate A until real content/card testing; don't pretend any A/B result exists.
