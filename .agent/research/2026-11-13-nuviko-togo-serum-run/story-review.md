## Revisions during factual/interest check, before scene work

- **Draft age bug fixed.** Draft originally said `twelve-year-old`, but NPS 1913-10-17 birth date and January 1925 timeline conflict with the conventional retrospective "12" label. Final replaces precise age with experienced/old dog; avoids arithmetic/documentary conflict without dulling the story.
- **Balto's exact place corrected.** Draft claimed `Balto led` the final team unconditionally. Smithsonian (2025-01-28, near line 83) says leadership at actual arrival is uncertain. Final says his **team** reached Nome and that Balto was **among its dogs**.
- **Real middle checked.** After a first quick pass, story risked reducing to "Togo longer distance, Balto more famous," a fact list. Changed-plan / almost-missed-exchange / outward-then-returning ice crossing retained, backed independently by AKC and Smithsonian.
