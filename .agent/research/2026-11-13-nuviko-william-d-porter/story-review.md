**10m Long:** opening questions about near-hit, failed warnings and enduring myth → concealed voyage, named escorts, documented boiler malfunction → practice establishes physical primer requirement → live weapon at 14:36 becomes main hook → two misleading messages and radio rule → 14:38 warning, evasive turn and miss → investigation, evidence disposal and consequences → contrasted documented facts with unsupported harbor collisions, depth-charge and mass-arrest legends → 1945 sinking with all 273 surviving, explicitly a different event.
