## Thumbnail concept
**Promise:** An operator disconnected both batteries yet the wire continued carrying messages.
**Paper thumbnail text:** `NO BATTERIES` (large, tactile, deliberate letters; title adds the 1859/telegraph context).
**Hero object:** period-correct brass telegraph key and disconnected battery lead foreground, visually unmistakable gap in power circuit; thin visibly connected telegraph wire leading to a second distant paper office. A restrained atmospheric/magnetic ribbon at distant skyline is conceptual, not literal lightning.
**Format:** portrait Short thumbnail with central-safe subject and required short prominent paper lettering, distinct from caption track; verify actual surface and phone crop after imagegen. Do not label as actual archival image.
**First promise confirmation:** S001 quote + telegraph key. **Payoff:** S012 induced current + S013 storm current as temporary power source. **False reading to avoid:** this was ordinary solar-panel electricity or a permanently batteryless telegraph.
**Composition QA after images exist:** dominant key and disconnected lead read at phone scale, dynamic angle, thick layered paper depth, title/thumbnail complement rather than repeat, easy first-glance relation, source/reference assets genuinely available.
