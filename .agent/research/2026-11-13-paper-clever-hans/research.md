# Research — Clever Hans: a horse that was reading the person asking the question

- **Research performed:** 2026-11-13; **public historical period:** Berlin, especially experiments in 1904 (publication in German 1907 / English translation 1911). This is *not* a contemporary 2026 news claim.
- **Target:** Paper Detour, English-language 9:16 Short for a new viewer with no background in psychology, horse training or Prussian history. Full Russian translation is an editorial reading copy.
- **Draft decision:** Choose a participatory puzzle, not the most frightening or expensive historical spectacle. The actual **answer** is that Hans was reacting to involuntary visible human movements; the **story** is the increasingly strict set of tests that showed it, then Pfungst playing the horse himself.
- **One-sentence compression:** A 1904 horse appeared to solve arithmetic even for strangers, but failed if the person asking did not know the answer or could not be seen, revealing human micro-movements that another human could read too.
- **Frame-level promise:** A paper-crafted horse taps an answer while a human examiner looks on. Exact truth payoff: after two independent tests, the person asking is the unwitting source of the answer.
- **Prohibition:** Never assert real paranormal mind reading, a conscious fraud committed by the master, mathematical horse intelligence, or that modern experiment controls emerged *solely* from this case.
- **Actual owner permissions:** Draft textual research/story packet only, no production/TTS/imagegen/render/upload/publication/merge.

## Three finalist briefs, before writing full narration

### A — Clever Hans (chosen)

- **Audience:** curiosity/history + animal behavior + psychological puzzles; English new viewer 16+ without assumed German history knowledge.
- **Why stop:** one familiar, vivid action gives an impossible outcome that viewers can test mentally themselves. **Exact first line** `What's three plus five? In 1904 Berlin, a horse seemed able to answer questions like that by tapping his hoof.` Image: horse tapping in a 1904 Berlin yard with a human watching.
- **Why stay:** independent commission did not see obvious fraud; remove trainer, horse still answers some others; change questioner's knowledge and answers collapse; block vision and fail; restore vision and improve; identify involuntary micro-jerk; psychologist demonstrates human replicates the same trick.
- **Payoff:** Hans wasn't doing math, he read human expectations, and the people signaling did not know they moved. The last sentence loops to the exact question spoken first, **after** revealing the mechanism.
- **Strongest objection:** the horse idea may be familiar, and two experiments might sound like repetition if the visual and phrasing do not make their different controls obvious. The 199-word text may be long for a Short: actual TTS and blind listener required. A fresh 0:54 summary of the same historical topic was published 2026-11-10, so **not a discovery claim**.
- **Source, beginning and outcome:** full original Pfungst experimental report, including 1904 committee reports, book chapters II (known/unknown answer, visible/blinders) and IV (human imitates horse); [2013 scientific revisit](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.4161/cib.27122). See precise matrix below.

### B — The LEGO octopus from 1997

- **Audience:** general English Shorts, toy nostalgia, families, true weird finds, ecology.
- **Why stop / exact opening:** `A teenager found a tiny black LEGO octopus on a beach. That toy had been missing for twenty-seven years.` Image: rare black octopus in sand on handmade paper beach.
- **Why stay:** 1997 cargo storm sends 62 containers overboard, one with almost five million LEGO pieces, many sea themed; a boy and family search two years collecting hundreds; only 4,200 octopuses among millions; he finds one in April 2024; rare second octopus emerges shortly after and the pieces show long-term plastic persistence.
- **Payoff:** the tiny octopus is confirmed from this real cargo event, not imagined pirate treasure; unusual romance of a find becomes a tangible consequence of cargo pollution.
- **Strongest objection:** after the 27-year lost-object premise, much of the middle can devolve into context/rarity numbers; a 40–55s short may be better than forcing 70–90s. Some toys sank vs floated; never claim octopus spent precisely 27 years **floating** or was lost on a sunken ship.
- **Source beginning and outcome:** [Marine Conservation Society/Tracey Williams 2022](https://www.mcsuk.org/insights/adrift-the-legacy-of-lego-lost-at-sea/), [Guardian reporting Apr 27 2024](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2024/apr/27/teenager-finds-holy-grail-lego-octopus-from-1997-spill-off-cornwall-coast), [Smithsonian background 2022](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/whimsical-legos-are-still-washing-ashore-decades-after-they-were-lost-at-sea-180979580/).

### C — Operation Paul Bunyan

- **Audience:** bizarre real geopolitics, military/historical tension without prior Korean War context.
- **Why stop / exact opening:** `The US sent bombers and an aircraft carrier to cut down a tree.` Image: lone poplar tree between watchtowers beneath aircraft silhouettes.
- **Why stay:** tree blocked UN guard sightline; August 18 1976 pruning party attacked and two officers murdered; response planned during dangerous standoff; August 21 a force with engineers, ROK troops, helicopters, B-52 bombers and offshore carrier secures cutting; the tree falls, no further fighting during operation.
- **Payoff:** absurd scale is explained by a lethal border confrontation; a 42-minute logging operation avoided further battle and left a memorial.
- **Strongest objection:** if the hook treats two real deaths as a joke about gardening, audience may feel deceived/offended. Politically complicated; requires unbiased contextualization. Near another Nuviko naval/military story #17 in cross-channel slate, though events unrelated.
- **Source beginning and outcome:** [US Army 35th anniversary](https://www.army.mil/article/63742/jsa_battalion_honors_fallen_officers_on_35th_anniversary), [US Army 2ID current](https://www.army.mil/2id), [UN Command 50th anniversary dated Aug 18 2026](https://www.unc.mil/Media/News-Stories-Press-Release/Article/4577082/50th-commemoration-of-the-murder-of-us-army-officers-at-the-joint-security-area/). ## Winner vs strongest rival

**Strongest rival = LEGO** for immediate recognizable visual, transnational audience and less psychological abstraction. **Chosen Hans** because the middle contains *at least three real independent information changes*: removing trainer fails to find fraud; eliminating questioner's knowledge kills correctness; eliminating visual cues kills correctness; the human replication explains that the clue wasn't intentional. Each step changes the audience's hypothesis and leads to a new decision. LEGO's discovery is more emotionally concrete, but context after the first octopus is less inherently suspenseful without manufacture. The second rival Paul Bunyan has higher stakes yet a more serious dramatic contract; Paper's visual charm risks mismatched tone. **Unresolved Hans weakness:** 190 words and a visually abstract movement must be done with physically plausible close-ups; if fresh first-listener says 'clear, but no reason to finish', reject rather than doubling down.

## Verified historic claims, evidence, uncertainty

Date of this source review: **2026-11-13**. `Original book` means **Oskar Pfungst**, experiments from 1904, book first published German 1907, translated English 1911: https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/33936/pg33936-images.html (Project Gutenberg 2010 digital edition). Do not call this a new 2026 event.

| Claim | Source and chapter / location | Status and uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| Hans could tap a number ostensibly answering arithmetic in Berlin; 1904 investigation | Pfungst Introduction, Chapter I, Appendices reprinting signed 1904 committee report; [2013 review](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.4161/cib.27122) | Verified broad fact. `3+5=8` is a **simple illustrative sum**, not a claim an identical literal question appears in a dated 1904 record. |
| A panel found no intentional sleight of hand; independence from trainer initially plausible | Pfungst reports and Introduction; Chapter II, `Experimental Conditions` | Qualified: Hans gave responses with a subset of other questioners, with unequal reliability; do **not** claim everybody could elicit 100% correct answers or commission certified arithmetic. |
| When examiner didn't know correct result, performance deteriorated markedly | Pfungst Chapter II, `Experiments and Observations`, at printed pp 36–41; success **90–100% with knowledge**, at most **10% without knowledge** across the listed series | Direct controlled experimental evidence. Script uses qualitative `started making mistakes`, not made-up figures. |
| When large blinders blocked examiner, correct responses nearly vanished; seeing examiner restored success | Pfungst Chapter II, blinder series printed p 44–45; large-blinder 102 tests, results **6% not seen, 89% seen**; [2013 review](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.4161/cib.27122) | Direct experimental evidence. Repeated smaller-blinder tests were ambiguous; script explicitly says **large** blinders, not generic fabric blindfold or eyes fully covered. |
| Lean forward, slight upward head jerk at expected number, signals horse to stop | Pfungst Chapter II p 47+, chapter IV p 122–125 documented head-jerk `¼ to 1½ millimeters`, average ~1 mm | Direct physical observations; do not show huge nod, flashing light, intentional wink, spoken count cues or any telepathy. |
| Questioners usually weren't knowingly signaling | Pfungst Chapter IV, pp 103–104, 25 volunteers unaware purpose; only two didn't exhibit typical movements. | Direct reports by observer; not a claim every person on earth moves. Hans learned the cues, not arithmetic. |
| Pfungst played role of horse, tapped with hand, read humans' chosen numbers | Pfungst Chapter IV pp 102–105, `the rôle of the horse`, 25 persons, tapping hand, no conscious knowledge of exact signs | Documented second-level reveal, beyond horse demonstration, makes a payoff after the first revelation. |
| Story outcome: no intentional trick detected, but examiners' knowledge leaked by micro-cues | Pfungst Chapters II–IV and 2013 review | Supported, while `modern blind trials all trace to Hans` is **not** asserted. |

## Ethical visual / rights / production boundaries

- Style is handmade full-frame opaque paper/card diorama, visible fibers, folded edges, crisp handmade subjects, physically consistent light and shadows, **not layered parallax outputs** or photo people. The visual of a Berlin yard is a historical **reconstruction**, not a photograph of Hans or participants.
