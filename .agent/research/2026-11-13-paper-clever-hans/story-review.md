## Exact staged hypotheses, defects to target

- **Hook:** Accessible to any viewer without a name or a museum date. S001 instantly proves what kind of surprise is promised. Potential issue: it may look like a trained horse doing a normal circus trick, not a mystery. The owner not present on S003 is an evidence-based push against this initial guess.
- **Second challenge:** S004–S006 require a clean visual separation: `who knows the result` and `who can be seen`. They are **two different variables** in Pfungst's original controls, not the same 'hide the answer' reveal twice.
- **First answer:** S008–S010 show exact minimal involuntary gesture. The outcome is explained in simple physical terms rather than vaguely `he read minds`.
- **Second payoff:** S011–S012 is a separate actual Pfungst laboratory replication with himself tapping a hand, and adds a surprising result AFTER the animal explanation. It makes the narration more than one fact stretched to 65 seconds.
- **Syntactic loop:** Ending provides the asker as hidden source, opening supplies their literal question. Honest semantic repeat after completed causal explanation; no fake 'watch part 2' or concealment.
- **Durational risk:** **199 English words**, 13 paragraphs. A 13×5s (~65s) **provisional** image grid does not establish actual speech duration. If actual normal-speed narration is longer, compress wordage or add genuinely necessary distinct plans; do not stretch one still shot or silently accelerate voice beyond profile. The plot may be better at 55–60s; voice test remains future authorized production.
- **Exact first-hand accuracy caveat:** Chapter II says success rates 90–100% with examiner knowledge and at most 10% unknown; in big blinder tests 89% seen, 6% unseen, but different examiners had unequal ability. The text wisely says `could answer other people's questions`, not any stranger always. Scene diagrams must not show glowing telepathic waves as real.
