**02m → 05m adds:** separate character with responsible duty (Thornton), verified uncertainty before explosion, original 1970 reporters as witnesses, and the next real-world disposal decision in 1979. **05m → 10m adds:** official risk from visitors, alternatives and consulted expertise, Thornton's disputed judgment after blast, film preservation, the 1990 bulletin-board revival, original 16mm versus degraded copies, and 2020 correction to the species. **Recommendation: 05m Long (conditional).** The decisive human action and irreversible consequences are clear without a large archival afterword. The revised 02m is viable as a Short candidate if a fresh blind reviewer accepts the two-cycle seam; it must not inherit the old storyboard, review, or approvals. The 10m archive angle is fully written and sourced, but a separate interest-led approval should justify its weaker closing third. 05m needs TTS-duration confirmation and may come in under five minutes; do not pad or change voice speed to fit the label.
