## Comparison of complete scripts and recommendation

**02m → 05m adds:** separate character with responsible duty (Thornton), verified uncertainty before explosion, original 1970 reporters as witnesses, and the next real-world disposal decision in 1979. The beach cleanup becomes an accountable causal story, not merely a spectacular incident. The 02m answer is complete and the revised textual loop is author-checked, but it still requires independent two-cycle listening before Short production.

**05m → 10m adds:** official risk from visitors, alternatives and consulted expertise, Thornton's disputed judgment after blast, film preservation, the 1990 bulletin-board revival, original 16mm versus degraded copies, and 2020 correction to the species. These are fact-supported and answer the 10m opening's third question. The price: once the burial and 1979 followup are resolved, the long film moves into media history; a viewer drawn only by the whale/car may stop at “The event might have stayed a local example...”. This is a **predicted editorial attention risk, not measured retention**.

**Recommendation: 05m Long (conditional).** The decisive human action and irreversible consequences are clear without a large archival afterword. The revised 02m is viable as a Short candidate if a fresh blind reviewer accepts the two-cycle seam; it must not inherit the old storyboard, review, or approvals. The 10m archive angle is fully written and sourced, but a separate interest-led approval should justify its weaker closing third. 05m needs TTS-duration confirmation and may come in under five minutes; do not pad or change voice speed to fit the label.

**Probable exact point of attention loss:** 02m from “They worried the surf would uncover it” through the scavenger explanation before the explosion; 05m from the shift to Thornton's retrospective assessment after burial if spoken too slowly; 10m from the move out of the 1979 disposal resolution into the tape/BBS chronology.
