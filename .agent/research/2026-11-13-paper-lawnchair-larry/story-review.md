## Literal final TWO → first TWO narration paragraphs (EN, one uninterrupted speech stream)

But he gave the lawn chair to a neighborhood kid. Years later, that kid donated it to the Smithsonian. It's part of a museum collection now. But imagine what the passing pilots saw that day: Sixteen thousand feet above California, a man was floating through the sky in a lawn chair. No engine. No wings. Just forty-two giant weather balloons filled with helium.
