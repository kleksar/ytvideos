## Three final applications, each independent

**A. Pepsi / Frontier — selected.** Audience: general English viewers interested in consumer promotions and real legal oddities, with 1990s context explained without prior brand knowledge. Exact opening: “A Harrier lands beside a high school. The Pepsi commercial puts a price on it: seven million points.” **Start:** fighter price tag unlike an ordinary prize. **Stay:** purchase rule → fifteen originals and $700,008.50 check → catalog missing jet → returned check → court and appeal. **Payoff:** judicial loss and Frontier's separately documented 7m miles announcement in February 2026. **Objection:** Netflix made a full 2022 documentary of the original case, so many may know the result; new coda is distinctive but not a proven audience outlier.

**B. Crash at Crush.** Audience: train enthusiasts and absurd historical spectacles. Exact proposed opening: “Thirty thousand people came to watch two locomotives crash into each other.” **Start:** deliberate head-on impact with crowd. **Stay:** event was intentionally staged, engineering predictions fail and boilers explode. **Payoff:** marker records two deaths and multiple injuries. **Objection:** the first collision essentially contains the full surprising answer; tragic consequences need care; earlier PR #47 compared this as an alternative. Primary Texas historical marker 1976 https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/Details/5309005315; secondary historical https://texasescapes.com/TexasRailroads/Crash-at-Crush.htm (access limited to retrieved summaries).

**C. Boston molasses flood.** Audience: urban-history and engineering-disaster viewers. Exact opening: “A huge tank burst, and molasses flooded the streets of Boston.” **Start:** physically surprising liquid disaster. **Stay:** reports of leaks, brown paint hiding flaws, rupture and engineering findings. **Payoff:** catastrophic deaths and why the tank failed. **Objection:** at least 21 fatalities; treating this as comedy would be exploitative. Municipal archive https://content.boston.gov/news/100-years-ago-today-molasses-crashes-through-bostons-north-end (2019), Smithsonian Nov 1983/reprint Jan 2019 https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/without-warning-molasses-january-surged-over-boston-180971251/.

**Why Pepsi over Crash:** several later changes to actual expectation (can BUY points, real check, refusal, court, 2026 civilian-miles coda), broader present-day reward-points familiarity, and no overlap with existing disaster/heist rescue drafts. No pseudo-precise score. “None” was allowed but Pepsi passes authorial interest screen with remaining reviewer risk.

## Verified claim, evidence, date, uncertainty (opened on 2026-11-13)

1. **Ad and rewards:** U.S. District Court *Leonard v. Pepsico*, decided Aug 5 1999, full description of 1995 Harrier shown beside school, “HARRIER FIGHTER 7,000,000 PEPSI POINTS”, actual catalog shirts and jackets. Primary **court's description**, not raw 1995 commercial bytes: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp2/88/116/2579076/ .
2. **Rules:** same court decision §I.A, supplement points ten cents each subject to minimum fifteen original packaging points; no Harrier catalog item. Did NOT mean legal ownership of a Harrier, which was disputed.
3. **Money and mailed attempt:** same decision §I.B; on/about Mar 27 1996 sent **15 original points** plus **$700,008.50** check, wrote “1 Harrier Jet” himself; raised roughly $700,000 through acquaintances. Do not claim he drank seven million cans or bank transfer completed.
4. **Refusal:** same legal opinion reproduces Pepsi fulfillment-house reply dated around May 7 1996, returning check and saying ad was humorous. This is a company statement; no verified fraud conviction.
5. **Judgment 1999:** court held advertisement not a valid offer and unreasonable to interpret literally; ruling based on multiple factors, not simply military legal logistics. 
6. **Appeal Apr 17 2000:** U.S. Second Circuit affirmed district court https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/210/88/483989/ . No jet was supplied.
7. **Feb 5 2026:** Frontier's primary marketing release https://news.flyfrontier.com/a-big-game-moment-comes-full-circle-as-john-leonard-finally-gets-redemption--30-years-after-he-collected-enough-reward-points-for-a-jet/ reports the replacement of the original seven-million-point joke with **7 million Frontier miles**. Separately People interviewed Leonard and confirmed campaign https://people.com/man-redeem-7m-soda-points-for-jet-in-2026-super-bowl-ad-sort-of-exclusive-11899862 (Feb 5 2026). Attribution in EN/RU is **“Frontier said” / “Frontier объявила”**; award-program terms and present account balance UNVERIFIED; no claim of guaranteed lifetime air travel.
8. **Observational demand, separate from predictions:** Netflix official media listing of four-part documentary released **Nov 17 2022**, https://media.netflix.com/en/only-on-netflix/81446626 ; 2026 airline promotion and independently reported interview. English historical documentary is neither a Short nor a controlled cohort. Public comparable YouTube Shorts views and account usual outcomes UNVERIFIED, no implied CTR/retention.
