## Comparison / recommendation

**02→05:** the viewer acquires the two-roof logistics, why an arrow was used, actual officer testimony, and the sequence from security to prosecutor. These change the *how*, not just the word count. The 02m is strongest for a cold Short but lacks enough time for those mechanisms.

**05→10:** the viewer also acquires the building's early public reception, fake entry/insider, rigging dependencies, hospital and photographs, a real Central Park performance three weeks later, and Petit's 2024 correction of the longest-lived myths. These are sourced, but the midpoint before the first walk and the second resolution after the park may cost attention.

**Recommendation: 05m Long editorial candidate** for a complete, causally varied story with one clean main climax and a surprising legal payoff. 02m may be the stronger *production choice* if owner prioritizes a Short; no claim that 05m empirically retains better. Recommendation is not approval, and none of the three may automatically be produced.
