# Nuviko History — The Last Warning at Halifax (1917)

**Logline:** When an explosives ship burned beside a Halifax railway yard, dispatcher Vincent Coleman returned to warn incoming trains. He died in the blast; other trains were halted and the railway sent six relief trains after a further call. The familiar claim about one particular train carrying 300 people is disputed.
