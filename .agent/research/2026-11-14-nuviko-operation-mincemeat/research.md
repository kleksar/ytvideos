# Operation Mincemeat (1943) — research and factual boundaries

**Status:** EDITORIAL_CANDIDATE_UNREGISTERED. Topic explicitly selected by owner for **Nuviko History Long**; no open-topic search funnel. Public historical research checked 2026-11-14. This document is not a production run or independent specialist review.

## Viewer promise and thesis
Central question: **How do you convince enemy spies to steal a document your side wrote specifically for them?** The story follows a causal chain: obvious Sicily invasion → corpse-based deception → constructed mundane identity and two letters → uncertain handoff via neutral Spain → paper inspection and decoded German reaction → actual Operation Husky → historical and human aftermath. Full resolution is a lie accepted because German intelligence believed the information had been obtained against Britain's wishes. This is not a claim that one dead man alone won Sicily.

## Creative, ethical and rights limits
- The actual body and its later identification must be treated respectfully; **no graphic corpse shots**, no claim of informed consent, no gruesome fictional autopsy scene.
- Reconstruction of private conversations, psychic motives, phone calls or document text not in checked sources is forbidden. We may show a scene as labelled AI illustration but not call it original archival footage.
- Exact photo of “Pam”, identity card and pocket litter exists in collections. Production must verify licenses and actual references or AI-recreate distinct illustrations without implying that a fake image is a historical scan.
- Do not claim footage of Allied reconnaissance or German HQ comes from archives when it is AI reconstruction.
- The narration's dramatic ordering is the storytelling structure; do not insert invented events between verified ones to fill target minutes.
