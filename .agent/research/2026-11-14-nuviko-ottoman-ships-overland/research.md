# Fact ledger — Ottoman fleet overland, April 1453

## Scope and viewer contract
The owner chose the subject directly. This is not a fresh topic-search contest, and does not claim measured audience demand. One verified action: smaller Ottoman warships were hauled across the ridge around Genoese Galata into the Golden Horn, bypassing the defensive chain. The short asks *how a fleet could get inside a chained harbour without breaking the chain*. A viewer should understand the blockade, prepared route, resulting defensive pressure, failed counterattack, and limits of this tactic's causal contribution.

## Audience and overlap
International viewer can grasp an iron boom, an uphill ship and a new exposed waterfront without extensive prior knowledge. It differs from open draft narratives of aviation disasters, shipwreck rescues and St Nazaire demolition: logistical geography is the reveal. The original earlier chat draft claimed a sudden reaction after April 20 and defenders “discovered” the move; those simplifications were corrected.
