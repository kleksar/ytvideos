## Delta comparison and recommendation

**Before:** one Short EN+RU, original research and preliminary 14-scene storyboard, no alternative lengths. **After:** identical original as 02m plus complete 05m and 10m EN/RU, shared expanded source ledger, separate metrics/maps and explicit invalidation of 02m-only storyboard for Long.

**2 → 5:** the actual gain is a connected reason for the raid, the two mutually supporting attacks, Chant's wounded demolition effort, damaged evacuation and Beattie's captivity. It makes the suspense of the missed fuse more consequential. 02m risks a swipe after the camouflage explanation because the personal fate is compressed to one line. 05m risks losing some viewers in the two-part plan just before the searchlights; shortening that section would be the next targeted revision, not more hooks.

**5 → 10:** real additional value includes the dock mechanics, U-boat's wrong-course report, compromised diversion, Durrant's fate, Newman escaping overland and the later torpedo panic. The **specific weaker stretch** is between the pump-house demolition and the hidden charge: several interleaved human paths dilute the central search-and-bomb suspense. After the Campbeltown explosion the torpedo/civilian consequence is substantial but a separate coda. This is not proof of poor retention, only editorial risk.

**Recommend 05m Long.** It delivers the double tactical/evacuation reversal with enough human meaning and fewer competing subplots. 02m remains a credible alternative if an actual reviewer finds the Long too slow; 10m is a complete candidate but not preferred simply because it is longer. Recommendation ≠ owner selection, approval or permission to produce media.
