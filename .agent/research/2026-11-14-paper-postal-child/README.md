# Paper Detour — May Pierstorff, child transported as mail (1913–1915)

Five-year-old May travelled to her grandparents on a mail train with 53 cents in postage (the attachment location is disputed), accompanied by a railway postal relative. Other documented child deliveries before and after a 1914 prohibition expose how a new rule could fail to stop local practice. Famous mailbag photographs were staged.
