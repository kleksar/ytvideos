## Selection actually considered, before detailed narration

Initial bounded scan of **eight distinct events**, not falsely represented as eight complete fact dossiers: (1) 1976 Chowchilla kidnapping; (2) 2018 Tham Luang/Thai cave rescue; (3) 1952 SS Pendleton rescue; (4) 1944 Port Chicago explosion and 2024 exoneration of 50 Black sailors; (5) 1986 Lake Nyos lethal CO2 release; (6) 1961 Goldsboro nuclear bomb near miss; (7) 1983 Gimli Glider; (8) 1972 Andes Flight 571. Gimli is duplicated by open #86; Andes by #107. Port Chicago would require more source work to make an otherwise legal-and-policy storyline emotionally complete; Goldsboro's "narrow miss" risks revealing its whole payoff in the opening; Lake Nyos's science is strong but second half often becomes a lecture on pipes. These judgments are concepts, not invented YouTube reviews.

## Fact ledger — claims, URL, actual evidence, uncertainty

All pages actually read on **2026-11-15**, pages dated as stated; documentary synthesis is not a recovered verbatim call recording.

1. **Municipality's compiled contemporary local-newspaper account**: https://www.cityofchowchilla.org/300/1976-Bus-Kidnapping — bus with 26 children aged 5–14, Ed Ray, stopped 1976-07-15 returning from swim, concealed under brush in riverbed; captives spent about **11h in two vans**, about **16h below the quarry**, all 27 survived. Quarry owner/suspects and draft **$5m** demand, arrest days and 1977 guilty pleas, 2012/2015/2022 parole. It is a retrospective official compilation, not a minute-by-minute primary audio/video recording.
2. **A&E 50th-anniversary reported timeline**: https://www.aetv.com/articles/chowchilla-school-bus-kidnapping-1976 — published **2026-07-13**, updated **2026-07-16**. Armed roadblock, painted/covered van windows, buried **moving van (NOT school bus)**, air pipes, food, water, mattresses, ceiling sag, heavy **cover and two batteries**, higher wooden box, 14-year-old Michael Marshall's work, dig up, complete escape. It specifically warns survivors disagree over who initiated escape. Reports failed police calls due to families/media occupying phone lines; links underlying retrospective https://www.sfgate.com/bayarea/article/s-f-s-worst-crime-scene-survivors-of-2673166.php. **No original call recordings/logs verified**.
3. **CBS 48 Hours interviews and contemporaneous child recollections**: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/chowchilla-bus-kidnapping-jodi-heffington-survivor-lifelong-fight-to-keep-captors-behind-bars/ — published **2023-03-18**, named survivor interviews; masked gunmen, stolen bus, painted van windows, lengthy vehicle transfer and ~16h underground, decades-later traumatic effects. Survivor first-person memories are marked as testimony; **no fictional exact speech, panic dialogue or unique escape leader inserted**.
4. **CBS follow-up**: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/chowchilla-bus-kidnapping-survivor-never-before-seen-interview/ — published 2023; describes survivor memories and circumstances of reunion. Details like transportation to a jail facility were **excluded** as not serving the chosen central question.
5. **Alternative Thai**: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2018-07-15/thai-cave-rescue-australian-diver-craig-challen-boys-sedation/9995830 — published **2018-07-15**, direct rescuer interview on sedation, full-face-mask panic risk and 2018 rescue.
6. **Alternative Pendleton**: https://www.history.uscg.mil/Research/THE-LONG-BLUE-LINE/Article/3757656/bernard-bernie-webber-and-the-greatest-smallboat-rescue-in-coast-guard-history/ — U.S. Coast Guard historical narrative, published 2024-03-20; broken tanker, 4 rescuers, compass failure, **32 survivors** brought back and one fatal loss.

Material uncertainty to preserve: leadership of hatch effort contested; moving van distinct from school bus; distances and 11/16h rounded; telephone-line failure is retrospective reporting with no direct call records, not grounds to invent a recorded call; no real survivor photo or exact face authorized, and source photos cannot be copied without licenses. Any visual reconstruction of a phone call is symbolic, not documentary footage. We specifically avoided exact depth claim, unconfirmed conversations and emotion invented for minors.
