## Pitches before script (same evaluation standard)

### A — FedEx 705 (selected)
- **Exact opening:** “A wounded pilot banked a FedEx cargo jet until it was almost upside down. He did it on purpose.”
- **Why stop:** A trained pilot intentionally places an aircraft in an extreme attitude; a human threat inside the cockpit explains the choice but raises how the crew can survive both.
- **Question after first line:** Who forced the pilot to do that, and how could he still bring the plane back?
- **Development:** trusted colleague aboard -> hammer attack -> speargun confrontation -> copilot impaired on right side -> deliberately extreme bank interrupts fight -> attacker struggles again -> injured captain lands while others restrain attacker.
- **Payoff:** safe landing, all crew survive; long-term career consequences; conviction affirmed.
- **Strongest objection:** prior #34/#86 and other aviation stories crowd the Nuviko queue; competing English documentaries already cover this event. One 2025 comparable is BELOW the publisher's ordinary recent result.

### B — Chowchilla school-bus kidnapping (strongest alternate)
- **Proposed opening:** “Twenty-six children and their driver disappeared from a school bus. The kidnappers hid them in a box buried underground.”
- **Question:** How can the trapped children get air and escape without outside help?
- **Development:** abduction -> long drive -> subterranean confinement -> escape via stacked mattresses and digging -> survivors' long-term trauma.
- **Payoff:** all survived, perpetrators arrested; survival doesn't erase aftermath.
- **Strongest objection:** prior suggested angle scored weaker for sustained development, and handling a real crime against children in a fast visual Short risks exploitative storytelling. A recent, more directly contemporary YouTube 19-minute retelling underperformed its own publisher's reported 7-day cohort (~0.4x). No second strong same-format independent YouTube baseline verified.
- **Sources:** https://www.aetv.com/articles/chowchilla-school-bus-kidnapping-1976 ; https://cnnpressroom.blogs.cnn.com/2023/10/31/cnn-films-chilling-crime-documentary-chowchilla-to-premiere-on-sunday-december-3-at-9pm-et-pt/amp/ ; https://www.explodingviews.com/pl/video/BwwCfttX0Ak .

### C — Tunnel 57 (Berlin, 1964)
- **Proposed opening:** “A group of students spent months digging a tunnel under the Berlin Wall. The escape route lasted only two nights.”
- **Question:** Can people cross before border guards find the exit?
- **Development:** excavation -> route under border -> 57 escape -> detection, shooting and closure.
- **Payoff:** 57 reach West Berlin; a border guard was killed and the tunnel was sealed.
- **Strongest objection:** two specific indexed videos have very low public view counts; some compelling details require an updated first-hand German source and careful attribution.
- **Sources:** https://berlinwallmap.info/fr/tunnel-57 ; https://www.ardmediathek.de/video/der-tag-in-berlin-und-brandenburg/joachim-neumann-tunnelbauer-ingenieur-und-fluchthelfer/rbb/Y3JpZDovL3JiYl8yY2E2MDBiMS1jOGE5LTQ3OGUtODkyYi0yMWEwOGY0ZjA1MGRfcHVibGljYXRpb24 ; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wlx-NZLw0Iw ; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zJrOdk0HU2g .

**Comparison.** Chowchilla carries greater social stakes but its strongest story functions are more difficult to portray without speculation and trauma exploitation in ~90 seconds. FedEx has a concentrated, sourced chain with a real second obstacle AFTER the aircraft maneuver (landing while the struggle continued). Tunnel 57 provides a distinct genre, but its public video reception is a weaker signal and the fatal confrontation needs careful detail. An optional failure remains: if an independent reviewer finds the aircraft genre repetitive or the loop merely thematic, reject rather than force a score.

## Documentary claim → source → boundary

All below were read 2026-11-15 as webpage body, not video reproductions. Key support:

- **Flight date, crew, Calloway's position and company hearing.** U.S. Court of Appeals, United States v Calloway, decided 1997-06-20; https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-6th-circuit/1058021.html , paragraphs starting “Mr. Calloway worked…” and “One of the FedEx flights…”. The hearing was scheduled next day; narrative does NOT assert he had already been dismissed.
- **Hammer attack, speargun, emergency return, all three injured, confiscated weapons, life sentence.** Same appellate opinion, factual section and disposition. Judicial description of trial evidence; conviction for flight-crew interference separately vacated as lesser included offense, so narration names **attempted aircraft piracy** only.
- **Extreme bank (~135°), partially impaired Tucker and purposeful flight maneuver, continued fight, longer runway, commercial careers.** Eyewitness captain David Sanders speaking at a conference in a 2011 State Aviation Journal account: https://stateaviationjournal.com/index.php/news/life-changer-horrific-story-fedex-flight-705 . Approximate bank angle from his account, not independently reviewed flight-recorder chart. We say “roughly”; avoid a barrel-roll claim of a complete 360°. The number of fight minutes varies across retellings; no exact duration in narration.
- **Contemporary corroboration:** Reuters dated 1994-04-08 in Washington Post https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1994/04/08/jet-lands-safely-after-attack-on-crew/d6481a01-a4ee-4e63-9c23-29504c5f9ee3/ confirms attack and safe landing, with initially uncertain motive.
- **FBI archival file exists:** https://vault.fbi.gov/fedex-flight-705-incident-on-april-7-1994 ; 186-page PDF collection **not reviewed as pages** in this run; do not imply it was read.

**Limits:** 135/140° discrepancy in later accounts, so use only captain's approximate 135°. Some articles present an elaborate insurance/suicide plan as certain; in narration we deliberately avoid imputing a definitive motive beyond the verified scheduled hearing. No quoted dialogue or invented panic/intent. No proof of the literal layout of every moment in cockpit, and scene artwork is reconstructed. “All three could not return to commercial flying” does not mean none ever recreationally flew again. The current public views of analogous videos are not a measurement of our viewers' choices or retention.

## Promise / packaging evidence

Opening image: **DC-10 in extreme bank, conceptual photorealistic historical reconstruction**, not a fake news still of exact flight. First line acknowledges the pilot did it intentionally; do not hide the answer. The viewer stays to learn why a crew member attacked and how wounded pilots kept the cargo jet airborne. Full payoff at safe Memphis landing, survivors, career loss and court outcome, all before textual return to the first image. No invented catastrophe or claim that the aircraft actually flew fully inverted.
