Thumbnail promise: two opposing human positions, a U.S. captain's empty personnel folder facing a Japanese submarine commander's witness stand; “HE DEFENDED HIM?” only if readable and grounded in courtroom/letter evidence. Unlike the film, not a present-day photo of either man. Early first proof: the first 2 paragraphs explicitly state a court appearance, contested accusation, and a 56-year question; within opening movement the viewer learns the true 1945 loss and stakes. Payoff: actual Navy personnel-record amendment in 2001, not fictional retrial or pardon. A/B thumbnail tests and image generation NOT authorized by this task.
