## Three competing short pitches

**A — Sampoong (selected).** Target English daily-life/survival/general-history viewer. **Exact hook:** "On June 29, 1995, a luxury department store in Seoul stayed open even as cracks spread across its upper floor." **Stop:** managers knowingly leave shoppers inside, a relatable safety breach. **Open question:** how did weak design persist and could anyone be alive under the rubble? **What changes after hook:** construction alterations; fifth-floor restaurants and added 35% dead load; cracks/closure without evacuation; collapse and precarious search; Park's 16-day rescue. **Payoff:** intelligible structural failure, accountability and astonishing real rescue. **Main objection:** subject widely told; tragedy should not be exploitative; weak audience match across languages/formats. **One-sentence story:** A luxury Seoul store remained open despite warnings, collapsed and killed 502, yet a worker emerged alive 16 days later. **Pre-script author scores:** interest 9, hook 9, development 8, payoff 8, novelty 6, visual 8, speech UNVERIFIED, public evidence 6.

**B — Vajont (strongest alternative).** English human decision/engineering audience. **Hook:** "The dam was still standing after the wave destroyed the town below it." **Stop:** intact dam but town destroyed. **Question:** why didn't holding dam wall prevent catastrophe? **Change:** warnings about unstable slope, journalist Tina Merlin's warnings, landslide, displaced lake, fatalities, inquiry. **Payoff:** distinguish rockslide displacement from dam breach; documented warnings and harm. **Main objection:** after immediate mechanism reveal, several beats could reduce to warnings chronology; difficult to make standalone emotional payoff without detailed firsthand voices. **Single sentence:** A known unstable slope fell into Vajont reservoir, overtopping a dam that remained intact and killing about 1,910 people. **Pre-script:** interest 8, hook 9, development 7, payoff 8, novelty 6, visual 9, speech/evidence UNVERIFIED. Evidence: https://www.attimidistoria.it/en/ (museum 1,910 death toll), https://jms.imde.ac.cn/en/article/doi/10.1007/s11629-021-7098-3 (mechanics), https://www.rainews.it/tgr/veneto/video/2023/09/belluno-longarone-tina-merlin-giornalista-quella-del-vajont-44af6cf2-024e-4547-b20e-05e507f6d4af.html (reporter warnings and trial).

**C — Radium Girls.** English workplace injustice/history audience. **Hook:** "To paint the next watch dial, the worker put a radioactive brush into her mouth." **Stop:** intimate dangerous instruction; how can employer claim safe? **Question:** who proved cause and did workers get justice? **Change:** illnesses → clinical evidence → lawyer sought → lawsuit → June 1928 settlement without admission of liability → work-safety measures. **Payoff:** compensation and workplace reforms, not full legal admission. **Objection:** very bleak victims' fates and widely known occupational tragedy, risk of falsely calling settlement jury verdict. **Single sentence:** Women painting luminous dials ingested radium, fell ill and fought a case that changed workplace safeguards without the company accepting liability. **Pre-script:** interest 8, hook 8, development 8, payoff 7, novelty 6, visual 7, speech/evidence UNVERIFIED. Evidence: https://blog.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/the-radium-girls/ and https://www.nist.gov/blogs/taking-measure/new-jerseys-radium-girls-and-nist-trained-scientist-who-came-their-aid

- Choi Myong-sok, a 21-year-old shoe worker, found alive July 9 after more than nine days; he reported rainwater and eating cardboard; his father and uncle helped search: contemporary [Associated Press](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/1995/jul/09/store-worker-found-alive-in-rubble-after-9-days/) and [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1995/07/09/man-found-alive-in-rubble-9-days-after-store-collapse/6aebcdd7-f88a-4de7-b1aa-97027838f5ef/) (1995-07-09). - Park Seung-hyun, age 19, rescued July 15 after sixteen elapsed days, thirsty, asking for the date: contemporary [Associated Press](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/1995/jul/16/survivor-surprised-by-stamina-woman-buried-for-16/), [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1995/07/14/Another-survivor-found-in-Seoul/8672805694400/), [Reuters / El País](https://elpais.com/diario/1995/07/16/internacional/805845606_850215.html).
