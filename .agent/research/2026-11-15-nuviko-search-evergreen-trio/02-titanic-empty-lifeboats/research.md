## Why watch
A common factual question has **two** independent answers: early boats left partly filled, but filling all 20 still would not hold everyone. This changes the likely expectation after the first fact, so the remaining script has an actual development and payoff.

Hook: "Why did Titanic's lifeboats leave with empty seats while people were still on board?" Question: Why would anyone leave seats empty, and would filling them have solved the disaster? Shift: limited confidence in danger → failed drill/uneven loading → insufficient *total* boat capacity. Payoff: both organizational and structural failure, not one simple villain.

- 20 boats, rated 1,178; no proper muster; some crew unaware of assigned boat — British Wreck Commissioner's report, Boats: https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/BOTRepBoats.php (read 2026-11-15).
- Lack of consistent loading instructions; boats left only partly filled — 1912 US Senate inquiry report (direct source, historical): https://www.senate.gov/about/resources/pdf/TitanicReport.pdf (search extract only; PDF not independently inspected here). Prefer British report for counts. Counts of individuals aboard vary slightly between hearing documents; script conservatively says more than two thousand.
