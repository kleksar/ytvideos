### Claim → event → reason → consequence → next question → evidence

1. **11:40 collision → boats not yet ready:** court reconstructs orders around 00:05 uncover, 00:20 swing out, 00:30 women/children, approx 00:45 No.7 lowered; explicitly notes **all post-collision eyewitness times unreliable**. Leads to why a passenger might hesitate; report's chronology and Lightoller's steam-noise testimony.
2. **First full-size boat listed 27/65, No.6 28/65, small No.1 12/40 → not a single reason:** official report identifies refusal to board, around 65-foot descent and officers' fear of buckling, later shown unfounded. **Important uncertainty:** report says it used lowest available occupancy figures; individual testimony (e.g. Hichens on No.6) differed, so don't call numbers a modern definitive manifest. Counts attributed to 1912 report, not to a perfect census.
3. **No muster → confused crew assignments → slower loading:** printed boat assignments existed, some men had not read them; pre-sailing inspection of two boats with crews did occur, **not** a ship-wide emergency drill. Means no claim that lifeboat equipment was never tested. Report "There had been no proper boat drill nor a muster", and paragraph on Southampton inspector Clarke.
4. **Deck labor moves between tasks → locally uneven loading:** Poingdestre reports around 40 women/children loaded into No.12 and he then assisted No.14 before returning. Don't turn this into documented general motive or claim that he counted every passenger.
5. **Lower-gangway proposal → missing completion:** report: captain ordered some partly loaded boats to row below side doors to receive additional passengers; doors **never opened**. This is a confirmed failed action, not proof of how many could have boarded.
6. **Late collapsibles A/B → paper capacity differs from deployable capacity:** A/B positioned on officers' quarters roof; difficult to bring down as list developed; floated off and used as rafts, not launched normally. Report "As regards the collapsible boats". Avoid claiming they were totally useless: survivors used them.
7. **After sinking, potential return → uncertain counterfactual:** commissioner criticized failure of *some* boats, especially No.1, to attempt rescuing people, but acknowledged plausible safety fears. Don't invent number of lives recoverable, officer orders, or cowardly motive.
8. **20 boats = 14 × 65 + 2 × 40 + 4 × 47 = 1,178 versus >2,000 aboard → structural shortage:** official finding and report. Even perfect simultaneous loading cannot hold everybody; not a claim that repeated ferrying was logically impossible under all imaginary conditions, merely under actual sinking schedule. Recommendations include capacity/drills; later international reform referenced in Senate historical summary https://www.senate.gov/about/powers-procedures/investigations/titanic.htm.

**Human line:** stewards waking passengers; officers supervising noisy deck; Poingdestre moving between boats; final consideration of people left in water. No reconstructed thoughts, dialogue, cinematic eyewitness quotations or fabricated individual passengers.
