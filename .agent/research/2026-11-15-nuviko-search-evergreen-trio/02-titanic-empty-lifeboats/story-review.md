## 02m story map — original strong concise text preserved

**Questions / promise:** “Why did Titanic's lifeboats leave with empty seats… And even if every seat had been filled…?” → answer both empty departures and global shortage. **First concrete event/hook:** first launches with far fewer people than rated; uncertainty on deck while passengers reach stations. **Development:** no drill → unequal loading → boats depart → capacity of only 1,178 for >2,000. **Payoff:** “even a perfectly organized evacuation could not have carried everyone in those boats at once.” **Consequence:** lifeboat rules/preparation improved. **Probable attention dip:** general paragraph about calm sea/deceptive safety, less specific than numbered boat case. And if…” **Immersion before main hook:** collision, separate passenger/crew preparations. **Main hook:** No.7 27/65, No.6 inquiry estimate 28/65, No.1 12/40. **Human/operational line:** stewards waking cabins → Lightoller signalling through steam noise → sailor alternating between No.12 and another boat → cautious decisions. **Change:** proposed lower gangway-door loading did not happen; empty places lost after departure. **Climax:** 20 boats for 1,178 versus over 2,000; even a perfect launch cannot carry all. **Payoff/consequence:** both organizational and structural failures, later rules; no invented precise saved count. **Probable attention dip:** dockside setup between the boat counts and first named officer; retaining a real shipboard action partly offsets it.

## Comparison and recommendation

**2 → 5:** 02m answers the question but not *how* empty seats became irrevocable. 05m adds the concrete failed side-door loading plan and sequential deck work, which materially changes understanding. **5 → 10:** 10m adds distinct facts about actual boat preparation, witness uncertainty, No.12, A/B and possible rescue attempts; valuable for an archival audience but attention may weaken after the gangway doors fail. These are additive narratives, not trimmed or padded duplicates. **Recommend 05m Long, provisionally**: highest causal density around a recoverable chance that disappears; 02m remains a defensible quick explanation, 10m a deeper documentary alternative. Recommendation ≠ production authorization.
