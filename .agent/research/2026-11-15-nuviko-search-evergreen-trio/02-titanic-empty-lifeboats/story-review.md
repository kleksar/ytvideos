## Comparison and recommendation

**2 → 5:** 02m answers the question but not *how* empty seats became irrevocable. 05m adds the concrete failed side-door loading plan and sequential deck work, which materially changes understanding. **5 → 10:** 10m now connects Lowe and Poingdestre's deck work with the later transfer and rescue; the empty seats change meaning rather than being recapped. P13–18 may slow attention before the door plan, although P25–28 adds a real new outcome. **Recommend 05m Long, provisionally** for the search question: shortest clear explanation with the failed door plan and capacity answer; 10m is stronger for viewers seeking the rescue narrative, and 02m remains a complete compact answer. Recommendation ≠ production authorization.
