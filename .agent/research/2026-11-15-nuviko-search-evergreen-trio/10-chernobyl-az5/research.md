## Viewer-value test and comparison
First two exact script questions promise a physical explanation of why an emergency shutdown could cause a brief positive effect. Development actually changes understanding five times: test was a cooling power supply question → power loss/xenon and rods withdrawal → positive steam coefficient → rod graphite displacement on AZ-5 → post-accident knowledge of design flaw. Payoff: not a magical button or nuclear bomb, but safety hardware in an unsafe configuration. ## Fact / evidence ledger: directly read HTML 2026-11-15
- Chernobyl NPP official historical account: https://www2.chnpp.gov.ua/en/42-about/accident-of-1986/528-2012-02-01-08-01-38528 — reactor danger from low margin plus design and positive steam coefficient; rods insertion ~18 s; existing operators did not receive clear margin indication. Distinguishes physical cause from party blame.
- World Nuclear Association, updated **2026-04-15**, https://world-nuclear.org/information-library/appendices/chernobyl-accident-appendix-1-sequence-of-events — electrical-coastdown test, postponed rampdown due grid, ~30 MWt fall, ~200 MWt stabilization, xenon, rods withdrawn, first positive reactivity on movement of graphite displacement pieces, AZ-5 at around 01:23:40, and precise warning that **why AZ-5 was pressed then has not been established**. This is an industry organization, not an independent eyewitness; source quotes INSAG-7 critically.
- OECD Nuclear Energy Agency, https://www.oecd-nea.org/jcms/pl_28271/chernobyl-chapter-i-the-site-and-accident-sequence — coolant/water absorption physics, positive void coefficient at particular conditions, test aim and timeline. Its historical write-up can preserve older interpretations, so cross-check with updated WNA and Chernobyl plant account.
- IAEA 1986 early report https://inis.iaea.org/records/8e700-gms57 — 1986 account emphasizes rule violations, not final word. Contrasted with later INSAG-7 as summarized by later sources; full PDF and technical appendix not directly inspected. Do not invent contents or precise sequence of explosions.

Caveat: phrase 'graphite-tipped rods' is searchable shorthand, but the physical story is a **graphite displacer connected to an absorber**, not a fake sensational 'graphite power button'. Other contributing factors and disputed last-second chronology are explicitly retained; no false nuclear-detonation narrative. Post-accident people must not be assigned anonymous thoughts or invented quotes.
