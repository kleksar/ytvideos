# Shackleton — 28 members of Endurance survived

**Nuviko History EN 16:9 Long.** A 1915 crushed ship leaves 28 stranded. Six cross roughly 800 miles on a seven-metre lifeboat to call for help for 22 on an Antarctic island. They must cross South Georgia's mountains first; three later relief vessels fail because of sea ice. Chilean tug Yelcho finally saves all 22. The wider expedition did suffer deaths: the claim is scoped to Endurance's own 28.
