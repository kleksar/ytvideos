# Shackleton / Endurance — 28 men and four rescue attempts

**Nuviko History · EN narrated illustrated Long 16:9.** When Endurance sank, 28 sailors were left without a ship. Six later crossed roughly 800 miles to South Georgia so a vessel could collect the other 22 from Elephant Island. Three relief voyages failed; the fourth, Chilean tug Yelcho, reached them on 30 August 1916. The 28 were *Endurance*'s men, not all members of the larger expedition.
