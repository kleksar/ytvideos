# Tenerife editorial packaging ideas — not production assets

**Single viewer promise:** Two jumbo jets were speaking to air traffic control. Why did the last *audible* runway question fail to stop the unauthorized takeoff?

- **Working title:** Two 747s. One Runway. A Warning That Was Heard.
- **Alternate:** Tenerife 1977: The Question That Didn't Stop Takeoff
- **Visual promise:** one fact-checked runway/taxiway map with both jets in dense fog, not invented photographic evidence. A split cockpit radio channel may show one jammed exchange followed by one later, *audible* question. Text concept: `RUNWAY NOT CLEAR`.
- **Payoff:** route clearance was not takeoff clearance; interference hid one exchange, but the later engineer's check was heard and dismissed. The record does not disclose internal motives.
