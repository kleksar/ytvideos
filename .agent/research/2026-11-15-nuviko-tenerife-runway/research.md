### Three finalists tested with the same questions

| Candidate | Why stop? (opening action / question) | Why remain? (verified causal development) | What is paid off? | Strongest objection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tenerife — selected** | Two Boeing 747s collide **on the ground**. | **Chile miners** | After 17 days rescuers discover 33 miners alive, but they're 700 metres below ground. How extract them? | Discovery → thin supply borehole → engineering/rescue capsule → individual ascents after 69 days | Rescue engineering and all 33 alive | Rescue payoff similar to existing Tham Luang #113; later drama can become repetitive shaft-drilling |

**Decision:** Tenerife wins among these three *as an editorial hypothesis*, not because raw views are comparable or because its tragedy is larger: the audience learns a surprising difference between two kinds of radio clearance, follows a documented sequence of changes in what each party believes, and reaches a concrete operational payoff. United 232 has a stronger human heroism arc but a more demanding aerodynamic explanation. Chile has the happier emotional finale but overlaps #113 structurally. Tenerife's greatest weak point is its saturated topic: packaging must promise the *mechanism of the missed warning*, not a generic “worst crash”.

**Recommended working title:** **Two 747s, One Runway: How Tenerife Happened**. **Exact opening in EN master:** “How did two Boeing 747s collide on the same runway while both crews were in contact with air traffic control? Why did one of them begin to take off without permission? And why did a warning from the other aircraft fail to stop it?” **Immersion:** the Gran Canaria bombing → diversion → blocked parallel taxiway → two aircraft taxiing in fog. **Main hook:** the KLM first officer's recorded warning that there was still no ATC clearance. About 173 English spoken words **precede** this hook (word count, not measured audio time). After it, Pan Am's exit geometry and the fatal radio sequence supply successive concrete answers, without re-telling the diversion.

**Full payoff:** the audience can distinguish ATC route versus takeoff clearance, muffled warnings versus subsequent audible warning/question, why the takeoff went on, 61 survivors/583 dead, and subsequent radio/crew-training changes.
