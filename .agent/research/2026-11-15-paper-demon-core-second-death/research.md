## 2026-11-19: 05m causal repair — first safety response vs later remote control

The earlier 05m omitted a documented intermediate institutional event: activity at the more isolated Pajarito Site **began in April 1946**, between the two deaths, but remote **critical** operations first appeared later (1948). This distinguishes *a genuine earlier response* from the *still unresolved reliance on close manual work*. It is not proof that moving sites caused, or could have prevented, Slotin's accident. Sources:

- [LANL-hosted journal abstract, Loaiza & Gehman, *Annals of Nuclear Energy* 2006](https://laro.lanl.gov/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/End-of-an-era-for-the/9916364480503761): experiments at Pajarito began April 1946; first kiva 1947; first remote critical work about 18 months later. The script gives 1948, without conflating early subcritical measurements with remote critical operations.
- [Nuclear Museum, Harry Daghlian profile](https://ahf.nuclearmuseum.org/ahf/profile/harry-daghlian/): returned alone after dinner against safety protocols; warning counter, dropped block, age 24, September 15 death; **stricter measures followed**. The fact of a safety-rule violation in 1945 does not imply intentional or reckless motives in 1946.
- [National Park Service, Slotin Building](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/slotin-building.htm) and [NPS oral history account](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/preserving-world-war-ii-era-atomic-weapons-research-and-development-sites.htm): Slotin had Trinity experience, trained successor Alvin Graves, stopped the reaction, experienced sour taste and burning hand, died May 30. No invented dialogue, subjective thoughts, or witness counts.
- [Nuclear Museum accident history](https://ahf.nuclearmuseum.org/ahf/history/atomic-accidents/): the second fatal event drove remote criticality work; other people were exposed, but not to identical doses. This cannot support any future no-accidents-ever promise.
- [Nuclear Museum archival correction](https://ahf.nuclearmuseum.org/death-louis-slotin/): original plutonium later melted and recast; Bikini-detonation legend excluded.

**Fact boundary:** The phrase “after Slotin” describes a policy/method shift, not an instantly operational remote laboratory. Never say the first death produced *no* safety action. No nuclear detonation, no technical reproduction instructions or inferred motive. All claims here checked as historical source text; no independent human source audit or live audience test.
