## Comparison of *the finished texts*

**2 → 5:** 02m compresses Daghlian, Slotin and prohibition into a causal line. Revised 05m gives a **specific earlier response**: stricter rules *and* Pajarito work already underway in April 1946. It then names successor Alvin Graves, demonstrates why hand operation was still risky, and follows the actual 1947–48 remote transition. These are new causal answers rather than elaborated adjectives. Potential 02m dropout: the first abstract scientific explanation, before a named actor.

**5 → 10:** 10m adds Daghlian's career, medical dose uncertainty and the same-room/date coincidence, but the revised 05m now includes the most important Pajarito timeline without the long exposition. In 10m the sequence **after “Slotin died on May 30”** diverts into coincidence and repeated lessons before the remote facility arrives; that remains the likely editorial loss point, not measured retention. Revised 05m may still lose viewers while explaining the initial measurement need, before Slotin appears. The original 34-paragraph master similarly spends time in early technical setup.

**Recommendation: revised 05m Long**, because it now shows *two distinct, verifiable safety responses* (tightened rules and developing a separate site vs later prohibition of manual operation and actual remote work). It uses Daghlian's solo violation and Slotin teaching Graves as human causes, without inventing intent. 02m could prove more watchable; this has not been measured. 10m remains a retained competing Long, not a failed/rejected topic. No version is approved for production.
