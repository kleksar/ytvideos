# Packaging hypotheses — Paper Detour Long

All concepts are text-only proposals, no AI images, no production title or uploaded metadata. The chosen exact narration is the sole factual boundary.

**C1 / PD-HOOK-01** — Title: **Pepsi Announced a Winning Number. Then Refused to Pay.** Thumbnail: tactile paper bottle cap marked “349” in huge foreground, television showing same number at rear, a smaller security-code strip separating cap and court ledger. Big paper lettering **“349?”**. Viewer promise: how matching the broadcast number failed to meet payout rules. First confirmation S001–S003, payoff S013 and S025. Avoid implying every 349 cap was legitimately entitled to one million pesos.

**C2 / PD-CHOICE-01** — Title: **When One Pepsi Number Sparked Years of Lawsuits**. Thumbnail: paper cap on a claims desk, on one side a million-peso poster, on other a 500-peso note, two opposed groups but no invented historical portraits. Big paper lettering **“₱1M OR ₱500?”**. Viewer promise: offer, public dispute and legal resolution. First confirmation S002/S012; payoff S017/S025. Avoid presenting the offer as accepted by everyone.

**C3 / PD-THRESHOLD-01** — Title: **The Hidden Code Behind Pepsi's 349 Disaster**. Thumbnail: physical enlarged cross-section of cap, upper part “349”, underside shows small separate code with a court file behind, physically connected perspective. Big paper lettering **“THE OTHER CODE”**. Viewer promise: the missing condition in what people saw and what courts enforced. First confirmation S003; full payoff S025. Avoid displaying a fabricated actual winning security code; illustrative symbols only.

Provisional editorial preference **C1** because its first frame matches the actual narration and the contradiction is legible. C2 and C3 are distinct editorial hypotheses, not three finished thumbnail outputs or an A/B performance claim. Each would require approved AI artwork, exact title/card QA and release-stage receipts after script acceptance.
