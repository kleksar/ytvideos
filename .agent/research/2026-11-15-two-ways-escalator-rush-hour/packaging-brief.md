### Thumbnail promise
Same escalator, different commuter behaviour: one walking lane versus packed two-sided standing. The readable question is **MORE PEOPLE?**, not "STOP WALKING" or "YOU'RE DOING IT WRONG". Make the left/right models visually distinct even without numerical overlays. Follow verified channel brand and separately generated lettering rules at production time.

### Early proof and payoff
First visual proof: side-by-side crowded queue, walking lane with visible gaps. Editorial-model payoff: in 2 minutes at Holborn-reported throughput used as assumed boarding capacities, 230 vs 302 hypothetical boardings out of 400 waiting. Real observation: about 30% higher capacity on a tall, crowded escalator, not a universal prescription. Honest qualification: no congested queue means walking may personally save time, and the experiment did not become a permanent London-wide rule.
