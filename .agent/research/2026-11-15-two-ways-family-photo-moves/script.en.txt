Six family photos, five relatives. Do I follow the list, or rearrange the order?

In my simple model, everyone starts outside the frame. I count each move in or out until everyone leaves again. Which would you choose?

First, I follow the list. Mom and Dad pose, leave for the kids' picture, then return for the whole family. Later, I have to call them back again. Twenty-four moves.

I reset the same six photos and rearrange them so people can stay in place. Strangely, Grandma goes first, not the biggest group. The parents join her, then I bring in the kids. All six photos, just twelve moves.
