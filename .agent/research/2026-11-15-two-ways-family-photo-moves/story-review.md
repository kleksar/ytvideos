## Completed comparisons — where added time earns its place

- **Original ~132-word root → 02m (302 EN):** preserves the original same-six-photos premise and natural next-list loop, but adds a specific human consequence: B's total minimum still makes Grandma return. A new C14 trade-off prevents a simplistic “always optimize total moves” takeaway. The short loses urgency if too many poses must be visually decoded before the 12/14 payoff.
- **02m → 05m (711 EN):** gain a full visible cause for A24, the crucial A5→A6 surprise, per-person fairness, and precise C14 sequence. The value depends on showing clear membership cards; likely attention risk is the fourth movement-count paragraph if the edit only displays another counter.
- **05m → 10m (1399 EN):** get why 12 is a verified minimum, that eight tied orderings exist, and why the added G2 restriction changes the optimum to 14. The main loss-of-interest risk starts after B12, when the exposition explains 720 permutations before a new human consequence appears, and resurfaces after C14 in practical caveats. This extra mathematical confidence does not by itself justify ten minutes for a broad entertainment audience.

**Recommendation: 05m as best *editorial* explanation of this one story**, not an approval and **not a production choice**. It develops the human-centered G4→G2 dilemma and avoids ten minutes of calculation. **Major blocker:** Two Ways Lab `channel.json` has no Long canvas/fps or completed Long production recipe, so 05m and 10m are not producible with current ChannelSpec. Do not alter shared rules or enable Long to bypass the blocker.
