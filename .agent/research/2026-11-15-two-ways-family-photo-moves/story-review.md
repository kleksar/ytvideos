## Completed comparisons — where added time earns its place

- **Original ~132-word root → 02m (329 EN):** preserves the original same-six-photos premise and natural next-list loop, but adds a specific human consequence: B's total minimum still makes Grandma return. A new C14 trade-off prevents a simplistic “always optimize total moves” takeaway. The short loses urgency if too many poses must be visually decoded before the 12/14 payoff.
- **02m → 05m (718 EN):** gain a full visible cause for A24, the crucial A5→A6 surprise, per-person fairness, and precise C14 sequence. The value depends on showing clear membership cards; likely attention risk is the fourth movement-count paragraph if the edit only displays another counter.
- **05m → 10m (1367 EN):** adds the invariant G4 across all eight global optima and D14, a second equally efficient G2 arrangement that transfers the extra movements from children to parents. Sixteen G2 optima divide equally. The first A/B ledger P6–12 remains repetitive if the viewer already understands the tally; the 720-order proof at P14 may delay the human payoff. New D14 materially earns some added time, but not necessarily ten minutes for a broad audience.

**Recommendation: 05m as best *editorial* explanation of this one story**, not an approval and **not a production choice**. It develops the human-centered G4→G2 dilemma more densely. The new D14 choice is valuable, but the ten-minute ledger risks slowing the story before the extra payoff. **Major blocker:** Two Ways Lab `channel.json` has no Long canvas/fps or completed Long production recipe, so 05m and 10m are not producible with current ChannelSpec.
