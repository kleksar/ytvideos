## Primary evidence, read during this research

**Canadian Board of Inquiry — Final Report (1985), hosted by FAA:**
https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/2023-10/Final%20Report%20of%20the%20Board%20of%20Inquiry.pdf

Extracted sections (PDF page-numbering and visual figure confirmation limited: source tool screenshot failed on large PDF; no image was claimed reviewed):
- Overview, report text around lines 317–329: 23 July 1983, Flight 143, Montreal/Ottawa/Edmonton, 61 passengers and 8 crew, no fatalities/significant physical injuries.
- Technical and operations evidence around 608–664: unreliable fuel quantity indications, manual sticks, `1.77 lb/litre` used as `kg/litre` instead of ~`0.8 kg/litre`, Ottawa repetition, both engines and power loss; navigation to Gimli, sideslip, landing gear and drag-race area.
- Computation around lines 872–907: 7,682 litres measured before refuelling; ~13,600 **pounds** misread as kilograms; around 5,000 litres added rather than roughly 20,000. A more precise spreadsheet/result requires the whole appendix, not a claim of personally measuring fuel.
- Board's systemic interpretation around lines 728–749: “metric mix-up” alone is too simple; maintenance, dispatch permission, training, communication and procedures contributed.
