# Research — Palomares 1966, the fisherman, Alvin and unfinished cleanup
Observation date: 2026-11-16. Audience hypothesis: English-speaking viewers who respond to a concrete Cold War mystery and a consequential human clue, not a speculative "almost nuclear war" story. All external queries omit private project data.

## Selection before writing

Ten distinct subjects screened against recent and older story PRs: (1) Palomares 1966, (2) Damascus Titan II 1980, (3) Nome serum relay 1925, (4) Hyatt Regency walkway collapse 1981, (5) General Slocum fire 1904, (6) Port Chicago explosion 1944, (7) Tangshan earthquake 1976, (8) MS Estonia disaster 1994, (9) 1956 Grand Canyon air collision, (10) 1961 Goldsboro lost bombs. **Three real competing pitches (not three variants of one theme)**

1. **Palomares / selected:** Opening: "How could a fisherman identify a hydrogen bomb that the United States military could not find?" First escalation: the parachute witness changes a land hunt into a seabed search; Alvin finds the weapon but the lifting attempt sends it farther down; payoff: CURV gets tangled and is raised with the bomb; second payoff: cleanup on land remains an unresolved practical question six decades later. **Why stop:** lost H-bomb and fisherman's odd role; **why stay:** first discovery fails then machinery becomes part of rescue; **gain:** distinguish weapon recovery from long-term contamination. Strongest objection: Cold War-nuke content is familiar, and public view-count signals are inconsistent.
2. **Damascus 1980 / strongest alternative:** A socket falling in a missile silo starts a fuel leak, leads to explosion and human casualties; physical escalation and concrete technical chain. Official PBS American Experience production covers the incident at feature length. A new five-minute retelling may offer less original value. PBS sources: https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/command-and-control-scene-breakdown-socket/ and https://www.pbs.org/video/american-experience-command-and-control/ . **Why stop:** a tiny dropped tool; **why stay:** expanding consequences; **gain:** understand how maintenance risk propagated; **objection:** strong existing documentary with its central beats covered. Not an owner rejection.
3. **Nome serum run 1925:** Real dog relay delivered antitoxin, but popular memory centers Balto while Togo ran a different difficult stretch. NPS https://home.nps.gov/people/togo.htm ; Smithsonian https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/this-heroic-dog-raced-across-the-frozen-alaskan-wilderness-to-deliver-life-saving-medicine-but-his-contributions-were-long-overlooked-180985905/ . **Why stop:** famous hero vs overlooked participant; **why stay:** dangerous relay and logistics; **gain:** accurate division of work; **objection:** familiar film/story territory and easy to flatten into one twist.

**Comparison:** Damascus arguably has the sharper instant hook; Palomares wins on a second, verified turn after the hook and a 2026 consequence that is neither the original explosion nor another quick moral. Nome has the widest family association but its revealing twist is already common. Winner remains a **testable editorial hypothesis**, not evidence of superior CTR or retention. If an independent blind reader says Palomares loses interest after the lift, reject or shorten rather than extend the radiation section.

## Primary and corroborating evidence, exact scope

- U.S. Naval Undersea Museum, Mary Ryan, **2016-04-01**, https://navalunderseamuseum.org/petrel-helmet/ . Sections from "On the morning of January 17" through "the morning of April 7": aircraft B-52/KC-135 collide, seven deaths, four weapons; three on land; fisherman Francisco Simó Ortz points to parachute sea area; Navy fleet and Alvin; first found March 17 about 2,550 ft, lost during attempt, found again April 2 at 2,900 ft; CURV entangled; decision to lift together; aboard USS Petrel April 7. This is museum interpretation of material evidence. Its legal claim that a salvor "owned" a bomb is **not** used; salvage-rights law is more complicated.
- Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, **2021-11-19**, https://www.whoi.edu/oceanus/feature/little-alvin-and-the-lost-h-bomb/ . Francisco first believed he saw a parachuting crewman, but all crew accounted; underwater terrain and poor visibility; early research-sub limitations; failed hook; Alvin 34 dives, over 220 hours; CURV tangle and lifting; technical navigation lessons. Attribution: WHOI interviews/recollection, not contemporaneous transcript; no invented pilot/worker dialogue.
- EFE Spanish news agency, **2026-01-17**, https://efe.com/andalucia/2026-01-17/palomares-60-anos-de-diplomacia-radiactiva-y-50-000-m%C2%B3-de-tierra-sin-mover/ . Two conventional charges released radioactive material, **not nuclear detonation**; 50,000 m³ *reported* contaminated soil pending a solution six decades later, activism and land-use controversy; political context. Figure is a cited current estimate, not a direct measurement here.
- EFE interview with CIEMAT director Yolanda Benito, **2026-01-19**, https://efe.com/andalucia/2026-01-19/el-ciemat-admite-que-el-plan-de-limpieza-de-palomares-de-2010-esta-obsoleto/ . Older rehabilitation plan partly obsolete; no authorized domestic waste repository; agency estimates <0.1 mSv/year public dose and states no condition attributable directly to residual contamination has been detected; concern remains about potential long-term hazard. Do not say the village is wholly unsafe or claim verified cancer increases.
- RTVE TV documentary metadata, **2026-02-10/17**: https://www.rtve.es/play/videos/palomares/episodio-1/16930887/ and https://www.rtve.es/play/videos/palomares/episodio-3/16937183/ . Descriptions corroborate aircraft/cold war and publicly photographed 1966 ministerial swim; **only pages/synopses inspected, not footage**. 51–56 minute Spanish programme, not comparable directly with our 5-minute English illustrated film.
- Contemporary WHOI and museum sources agree on broad sequence. Depth/date differences in reports are *not* spoken as precise claims beyond March/April 7; no off-the-record conversations, emotions or motivations imagined.
