## Audience promise / search-facing positioning

Premise: **a wreck in Scotland carried 264,000 whisky bottles, locals 'rescued' them, customs sent some islanders to prison and finally destroyed the remains of the ship**. Searchable historical anchor: `SS Politician 1941`, `Whisky Galore true story`, `Eriskay shipwreck`. Avoid claiming an unverified current YouTube search trend.

Candidates for chosen 05m:
- **Title A**: `Why Britain Blew Up a Ship Full of Whisky` — explosive climax as promise; caveat: vessel was already wrecked and not all bottles remained when destroyed.
- **Title B**: `The Shipwreck That Turned Whisky Salvage Into a Crime` — more exact legal contrast, less immediate shock.
- **Title C**: `264,000 Whisky Bottles. One Shipwreck. Then the Customs Raids.` — quantity / conflict, accurate original manifest from Scottish Maritime Museum.

Recommended A provisionally because first scene actually delivers a wrecked cargo ship and answers the deliberately blown-up wreck question. Not a CTR claim or user approval.
