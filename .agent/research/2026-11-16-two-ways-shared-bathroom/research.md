## Three equivalent pitches

| Candidate | Precise EN opening + first visible state | Why stop / what changes after hook | Complete payoff | Strongest objection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bathroom** | “I left our only bathroom empty for seven minutes. And somehow, we both got ready three minutes faster.” Two figures, one bathroom, 07:37 → 08:00 deadline | Familiar lateness; in A the bathroom is continuously used but shared departure is 08:02. In B bathroom empty 7 minutes and departure 07:59; later 3-minute return penalty reverses winner | Utilization of a room and completion time of two people are different metrics; 3m saved in a declared model, not universally | Fixed task minutes and zero return penalty are invented; one idea may feel mathematical instead of emotional; ten minutes too long |
| **Shopping route** | “I picked the nearest item every time. So why did I walk farther than if I'd followed the aisles?” Overhead grocery layout | Greedy shortest-next hop can double back; aisle order may win by full-route distance | Full route and ending position, not each next step, determine the winner | Without surveyed geometry this is simply a contrived route; visual floorplan required |
| **Suitcase** | “I packed the heaviest items first and still couldn't close the suitcase. Would larger items first help?” Open bag and item silhouettes | A shape constraint interacts with a mass budget; item placement order changes admissible set | Winner conditional on exactly declared items/packing method | Solving real 3D fit has high factual/visual complexity; too many hidden assumptions for Short |

**Why choose bathroom:** The first scene is an immediately known pressure (late to leave), subsequent change isn't just more data but an *opposite interpretation* of an empty bathroom, and the payoff has a meaningful 08:00 deadline. Strongest runner-up is route planning, which may outperform bathroom on visual curiosity if its actual layout is validated. This winner still risks an instructional feel and a weak 10m expansion. No guarantee of audience interest.

## Deterministic toy model and event ledger (the factual core)

Start 07:37 = t0. Deadline 08:00 = t23. No walking/setup/interruptions in base model. One bathroom, separate bedrooms. All jobs non-preemptible. Both people start available at t0. Each must shower before dressing, teeth can be brushed before or after dressing; shoes last. Bathroom admits one person. All other work independent. All timings chosen for the story, not measured.

| Actor | Shower in bathroom | Brush in bathroom | Dress in bedroom | Shoes outside | Sum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Narrator | 4m | 3m | 10m | 1m | **18m** |
| Sam | 5m | 2m | 10m | 1m | **18m** |
| Total | **9m** | **5m** | **20m** | **2m** | **36 person-minutes** |

**A — contiguous bathroom visit (narrator first):**
- narrator shower t0–4, teeth t4–7, dress t7–17, shoes t17–18 → ready t18 / 07:55;
- Sam shower t7–12, teeth t12–14, dress t14–24, shoes t24–25 → ready t25 / 08:02;
- bathroom busy t0–14 without gaps, departure t25, **2 minutes late**.

**B — shower, dress, return to brush:**
- narrator shower t0–4, dress t4–14, brush t14–17, shoes t17–18 → ready t18 / 07:55;
- Sam shower t4–9, dress t9–19, brush t19–21, shoes t21–22 → ready t22 / 07:59;
- bathroom idle t9–14 (5m) and t17–19 (2m) = **7m idle**; occupied total **14m** in both A and B, departure **3m earlier**.

**Sensitivity with return setup `h` minutes/person** (setup occurs inside bathroom before second brushing visit, A incurs none): at h=1: B t23; h=2: B t24; h=3: narrator returns t14–20, Sam t20–25, shoes t25–26 → B t26, A t25 wins by 1. Exact formula for 2≤h: shared B finish `20+2h`; at h=2, 24; at 2.5, 25 (tie). No claim about real-world reentry cost. Swapping order in A does not change shared t25 with these symmetric bedroom lengths. If only narrator departure matters, both ready at t18, tie.

## Script promise, beats and proof boundaries

Promise: **Can deliberately leaving the only bathroom vacant get us out before 08:00?** First visual proof: one bathroom door, two clocks, one crossed-out deadline (illustration, not generated asset). Main plot: A full resource use yet late → B appears to waste room time → overlapping dress completes early → assumption flip at reentry cost. Payoff exact: **A 08:02, B 07:59** in base; no universal result. 02m ends on exact response to “what changed?” and returns grammatically to cold start; visual/audio seam without MP4 UNVERIFIED.
