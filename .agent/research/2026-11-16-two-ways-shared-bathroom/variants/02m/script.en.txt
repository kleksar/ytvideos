Seven minutes of empty bathroom, even though we're in a hurry.

Yet I'm ready at eighteen minutes, Sam at twenty-two. We leave at seven fifty-nine.

Same jobs. Same fourteen minutes of actual bathroom work. Three minutes saved because our dressing overlaps with the other person's shower.
