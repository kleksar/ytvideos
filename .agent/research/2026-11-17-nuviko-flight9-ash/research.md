## Promise → actions → payoff

- Audience: English-speaking viewer interested in real survival decisions, without needing to know flight codes or jet mechanics.
- Opening (05m exact): “Why did a four-engine Boeing 747 suddenly become a glider over Indonesia? How did its engines return? And why did the crew still have to invent a way to land almost blind?”
- First evidence: night over Indonesia, unusual glow and smell, a clean radar.
- Primary hook: four engines fail; crew has to spend height and consider ditching.
- Causal turn: leaving ash + cooler engines permits restart.
- Second complication: ash-abraded cockpit windows and nonfunctioning ILS vertical guidance.
- Full payoff: altitude/distance callouts and safe landing. Viewer learns why a moisture radar cannot exclude an ash hazard and why restart was not the end.
- Strong objection: existing compelling 40-minute documentary means original Nuviko contribution must be shorter and more tightly structured; demand is only an editorial hypothesis.
