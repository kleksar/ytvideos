## Promise -> first seconds -> payoff

- **Verifiable promise**: the guitarist found an empty bridge, made the distress call, and joined an evacuation staffed by entertainers and professional rescuers.
- **Suggested primary title:** *The Cruise Ship That Needed Its Guitarist to Call Mayday*. Alternative: *When the Bridge Was Empty: The Oceanos Rescue*. Third: *A Sinking Ship, a Guitarist, and the Rescue of Everyone Onboard*. All are editorial proposals, not measured CTR winners.
- **Suggested cover text:** `NO ONE ON THE BRIDGE` (not “Captain Fled”, “571 Lives Saved By One Man”, or “They Had 10 Minutes”). Cover concept: a realistically scaled empty 1991 bridge/radio and dark ocean through windows, tiny inset of the same ship listing. An optionally present 2D Nuviko traveler must remain an **outside narrator**, not a made-up crewman. No 3D traveler, no imitation archive screenshot, no exact visual claim unsupported by reference.
- **05m opening narration, exact:** “Why was a guitarist sending a distress call from the bridge of a cruise ship? Why could the ship's lifeboats not finish the evacuation? And how did every person aboard survive?” The opening questions come *before* storm immersion; main hook is later flood/lifeboat breakdown.
- **First confirming story steps:** 1991 ship leaves South African port in storm → lights and engines fail → the entertainer investigates and finds flooding → lifeboats can no longer be safely lowered.
- **New tension after initial hook:** empty bridge, inability to give coordinates, merchant vessels' limited rescue capacity, waits for helicopter divers.
- **Full payoff:** everybody survives; ship sinks ~45 min after the last person is rescued. Entertainers **did not** execute the whole rescue: navy divers, pilots, vessels, surviving crew and passengers all mattered.
- **Viewer choice title/cover consistency:** no literal claim that every officer fled or that the captain's motives were proven; legal inquiry and contested witness testimony belong in research, not an invented moral verdict.
