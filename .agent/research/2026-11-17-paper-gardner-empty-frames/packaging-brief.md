# Packaging — Paper Detour, Gardner empty frames (proposed 05m Long)

**Target and first viewer question:** global English audience; no museum knowledge assumed. The viewer learns immediately that two people impersonated police, gained access to an art museum and removed famous artworks. The unanswered promise is **why the frames deliberately remain empty**, not the identity of the thieves.

## Honest title approaches

A. **The $500M Art Heist That Left Frames Empty for 36 Years** — strongest: known disappearance, ongoing consequence, no fake solved mystery.
B. **Two Fake Cops Stole 13 Artworks. The Frames Are Still Empty.** — action and payoff image, slightly long.
C. **The Museum That Refused to Fill Its Empty Frames** — curator's response foregrounded; less immediate theft scale.

Avoid “finally solved”, “the thief revealed”, “13 stolen paintings” (many are drawings/objects), and “the cameras caught the thieves” (no robber-night video available).

## Paper thumbnail intent, not image assets

One full handmade-card gallery scene: two **truly empty** gilded frames, tangible cut-paper depth and museum wall, small silhouetted uniformed figures by a distant doorway, clearly stylized rather than identified suspects. Large physically layered letter shapes: **STILL EMPTY**. 16:9 Long; keep major empty-frame shape legible under 320×180 preview and duration overlay. This is a creative proposal; no actual image, archive photo, QR, PNG or provenance bytes claimed. Brand requires text, paper fibers, contact shadows and no decorative external frame.

## Opening-to-payoff trace of chosen 05m

- **First line:** “Why would thieves leave the most visible evidence of a museum robbery hanging on the walls?”
