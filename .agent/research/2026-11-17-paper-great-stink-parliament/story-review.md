- **Promise:** Parliament passed a sewer law in eighteen days because of smell; how did it help fight cholera?

- **After hook:** flush toilets route sewage into river → sun exposes waste at Parliament → chemically treated curtains fail → 18-day legislation → Bazalgette construction until 1875 → foul-air theory versus contaminated water.
