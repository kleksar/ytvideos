## Three competing verified pitches

**Hubble** — stop: a telescope passes tests but fails to see in space. Exact opening: “How can a telescope pass its tests, reach space and produce blurry pictures?” After hook: the mirror flaw appears in two cameras; the allegedly reliable testing device was wrong; WFPC2 fixes its own beam; COSTAR costs a photometer slot to correct other instruments; distorted solar panel complicates astronauts' work; sharp star and 1995 Hubble Deep Field. Payoff: original mirror stays flawed but corrected optical paths enable new science. Biggest objection: widely covered by established channels, high legacy views not evidence of current audience demand. **Selected:** unique contradiction and at least five different verified causal moves.

**Philippe Petit** — stop: wire between two New York skyscrapers, police below. Exact opening: “How did a group of artists pull a steel cable between two guarded skyscraper roofs?” After hook: disguises → arrow carries line → cables progressively crossed → multiple passes and bows → surrender. Payoff: artist descended safely and trespass charges dropped on public performance condition. Strong objection: famous, film-dramatized outcome; 10m requires heavy reuse-risk historical media. [History overview](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/August-7/philippe-petit-tightrope-walk-twin-towers-world-trade-center-1974); [production actions](https://www.history.com/articles/philippe-petit-twin-towers-high-wire-walk).

**Leonov** — stop: first spacewalker outside an airlock cannot easily reenter. Exact opening: “Could the first spacewalker fit back inside after his suit inflated?” After hook: suit expands, pressure release, reentry, separate landing problems. Payoff: lived through milestone and landed away from recovery area. Strong objection: [Smithsonian's reading of newly released contemporary report](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/air-space-magazine/turns-out-alexei-leonovs-first-spacewalk-wasnt-quite-dramatic-we-thought-180974522/) challenges later head-first/desperate versions; exact drama requires careful firsthand calibration. [ESA](https://www.esa.int/articles/about-us/directorates/human-and-robotic-exploration/alexei-leonov-the-first-spacewalker-passes-away), [Smithsonian](https://airandspace.si.edu/exhibition/outside-spacecraft/floating.html).

**Strongest alternative: Philippe Petit** wins single-frame shock and recognizability, but Hubble yields a less obvious correctable error, second repair route and 1995 Deep Field as second payoff. Petit is a *loser in this comparison*, not an owner-rejected concept. Hubble's limiting risk is subject saturation; reject if independent reviewer finds this mechanism not compelling. ## Safeguards

Do not imply mirror was damaged during flight, completely useless before repair, or directly repaired in orbit. WFPC2 corrected its own instrument; COSTAR corrected the other three by occupying the High Speed Photometer slot. ESA solar-array problem was independent, not a cause of spherical aberration. Two-minute version omits secondary orbital servicing detail rather than misleadingly adding action. Deep Field happened in 1995, not in first post-repair photographs. No invented quotes, private motives or exact audience numbers.
