## Honest audience promise
A private fishing lake was held by a weakened dam upstream of Johnstown. There were attempted repairs and warnings, then a 1889 flood that killed **2,209 officially**, with debris/fire at a rail bridge. Why could owners of the lake escape **legal liability**? The angle is decisions and accountability, not simply a huge wave.

- **The Town Below the Rich Men's Lake** — emotional, but needs subtitle/title context to avoid wealth-as-proof.
- **The Dam Failed. The Warning Came First.** — weaker legal payoff but clearer sequence.
