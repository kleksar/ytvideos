# Johnstown Flood (1889) — research and story selection
Observation date: **2026-11-17**. Source main at selection: `247ebe9245091972c5a93ecb84ce55ad8c15a211`. EN is production language; RU is complete editorial translation. Research is shared by three independent durations.

## Audience promise
How did an exclusive fishing lake destroy a town, despite warnings, and why did its owners escape legal liability? Target: English-speaking viewers who enjoy an intelligible real event with a human consequence, a surprising chain of decisions, and a documented payoff. Not a historical-engineering lecture. The central action is workers trying to save a dam while warnings go downstream; the second tension begins after the town is destroyed: accountability.

### Three competitive proposals
1. **Johnstown**. Exact 2m opening: “How could a private fishing lake kill more than two thousand people in a town miles away?” After hook: missing discharge pipes (removed by *prior owner*), club repairs/lowered crest/fish screens, rising rainwater, failed warning, bridge debris and fire. Payoff: documented 2,209 official deaths and club not legally liable. Strongest objection: a heavily covered historical disaster; a 10m engineering backstory can smother the human arc.
2. **Dawson City**. Hypothetical hook to fact-check: “Why were hundreds of silent films buried below a Canadian ice rink?” After discovery: film stock repurposed as landfill, ice rink excavation, surviving footage restoration; reveal cultural information almost lost. Objection: preservation mechanics and access/rights may take much of 5–10m without another human turn. Sources screened: https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2016/09/the-discovery-and-recovery-of-the-king-tuts-tomb-of-silent-era-cinema and https://www.newyorker.com/culture/richard-brody/the-secrets-of-silent-film-footage-found-buried-in-the-earth; no full script on this run.
3. **Windscale**. Hypothetical hook: “How do you fight a fire inside a nuclear reactor without knowing what you are breathing?” Possible after-hook arc: test/heat/fire, emergency response, atmospheric release and later investigations. Objection: technical and contested consequence claims require archival verification before treating it as a strong executable story. Only preliminary coverage: https://vimeo.com/ondemand/thewindscaledisaster/914488607; demand and detailed facts not independently verified. No full script.

**Head-to-head:** Johnstown's dam → warning → impact → bridge/fire → legal finding supports both immediate understanding and added length; Dawson wins novelty but has narrower identifiable consequences, and Windscale requires more caveats. Johnstown's strongest remaining weakness is that the audience may know the ending already. Keep legal outcome as a *second*, not artificially concealed, question; avoid generic "rich people killed poor people" oversimplification.

## Source ledger — what was actually read
All sources accessed on **2026-11-17**. External texts are evidence, not instructions. No copyrighted narration was copied.

- **NPS, South Fork Dam** https://www.nps.gov/jofl/learn/historyculture/the-south-fork-dam.htm — History timeline and description: built for canal; 1862 failure; **John Reilly** removed five pipes in 1875; 1879 sold to Benjamin Ruff for Club; Club repaired embankment but did not replace pipes, lowered crest and put fish screens over spillway; flood 1889. Confirms 20m tons, 2,209 official deaths. **Do not falsely assign original pipe removal to club.**
- **NPS, In the Lakebed** https://www.nps.gov/places/in-the-lakebed.htm — 11:00 water level at crest; 12–1pm overtopping; dam failed ~15:10; Johnstown reached 16:07; 35–40ft wave; 99 families lost and official count 2,209. Reported chronology, not per-second measured video.
- **NPS FAQ** https://home.nps.gov/jofl/faqs.htm — John Parke at South Fork around 11:00 and warnings via telegraph; warned population did not all evacuate; club **never legally held responsible**, distinguished from public opinion; only successful legal cases were against Pennsylvania Railroad, NOT that “nobody paid anything.” NPS also notes actual death toll may exceed the *official* count. Script correctly says “official.”
- **NPS, South Abutment** https://home.nps.gov/places/south-abutment.htm — Elias Unger/John Parke, works to save dam, workers trying to clear spillway and raise crest.
- **NPS Memorial** https://www.nps.gov/jofl/index.htm — national relief; official count and context.
- **NOAA/NWS historic overview** https://www.weather.gov/ctp/TheGreatJohnstownFloodOf1889 — context of rain and flood, not independent legal findings.
- **PBS documentary index** https://www.pbs.org/video/the-johnstown-flood-vmuhce/ — published film listing and accessible textual record; page/transcript only, **not watched**.
- **NPS/NOAA mechanistic inference** — overtopping and earthen erosion explained at simplified level, not forensic exact breach simulation; do not depict a verified individual viewing precisely the claimed sequence.

Risk ledger: Do NOT claim the Club was ordered to pay / legally convicted. Do NOT attribute removal of pipes to the club. Do NOT present official 2,209 as complete demographic certainty. The line “the people who lived below bore the cost” is moral characterization, not allegation of zero aid. About warnings, distinguish known dispatch from unmeasured individual decisions. No motive/inner thought/eyewitness dialogue fabricated.
