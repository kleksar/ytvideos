## Viewer contract

- Audience: English-language Paper Detour viewers, no prior knowledge of Alaska geography.
- Promise: **a fishing boat travelled above treetops**; what happened to the three boats and what 1,720ft really measured.
- Mood: documented danger, plain human actions, quiet scale revelation, not disaster spectacle.
- Stronger than a pure "world's tallest wave" headline because it explicitly distinguishes **local water run-up** from a towering moving ocean wall.
- Search relevance: Lituya Bay 1958, Alaska megatsunami, fishing boats, tallest tsunami runup. These are descriptive search phrases, NOT proven high-traffic keywords.
- Avoid titles saying the boat rode a 1,720-foot-tall vertical wave or implying all fishermen survived.

## Three truthful English title candidates

A. **A Fishing Boat Rode Over a Forest — Lituya Bay, 1958** (central action, human hook).
B. **Three Fishing Boats Faced the Biggest Tsunami Run-Up Ever Measured** (both survival and loss).
C. **The 1,720-Foot Tsunami Number Everyone Gets Wrong** (explanation-focused, higher risk of feeling like an engineering lecture).

**Recommended A**; title test not conducted. Paper title still needs actual human/mobile review.
