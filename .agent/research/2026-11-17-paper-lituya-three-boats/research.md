### Three competitive proposals, equally assessed

**1. Lituya Bay 1958 — selected**
- Audience: English viewers who understand a fishing boat and a forest without specialist geological background.
- Stop: "How could a fishing boat travel above the trees?"
- Stay: Three boats in one bay, two distinct survival mechanisms, a missing third crew; the record height looks impossible until the measurement is explained.
- Payoff: Ulrich + son escape in Edrie; the Swansons abandon wrecked Badger and are rescued; Wagners on Sunmore die; 524 m is local **run-up**, not a moving 524 m-tall wall.
- Actual factual coverage: Alaska Earthquake Center 2018; Don J. Miller/USGS 1960 eyewitness accounts in Geology.com; NASA 2020 satellite forest comparison. Full ten-minute details also include the departing mountaineers and previous wave scar evidence.
- Strongest objection: several 2026 documentaries already deliver the 1,720-foot record plus the same boats; short claim of novelty would be dishonest. The angle competes through clarity and concrete survival choices, not subject exclusivity.

**2. Alcatraz 1962 — strongest runner-up**
- Stop: Guards find realistic dummy heads in beds while three men are missing.
- Stay: Holes widened in cells, improvised raft, evidence recovered in the bay; the investigation changes what can and cannot be concluded.
- Payoff: FBI records an escape with a still-uncertain final fate, rather than declaring the escapees survived or drowned.
- Source opened: [FBI official Alcatraz Escape](https://www.fbi.gov/history/cases-and-criminals/alcatraz-escape), sections "The Escapees", "Missing", "Gathering the Clues" (observed 2026-11-17). Provides names Frank Morris, John and Clarence Anglin, dummy heads, recovered raft pieces.
- Serious objection: the central audience question "Did they escape alive?" cannot be conclusively resolved. Other tellings already exploit the unresolved ending. More character-driven and visual, but weaker verified payoff.

**3. Johnstown Flood 1889**
- Stop: A reservoir above a town fails after a succession of structural compromises.
- Stay: Removed outlet pipes, lowered dam crest, blocked spillway, rising rainwater, sudden breach.
- Payoff: Why the dam became vulnerable and the devastating consequences: the US National Park Service reports 2,209 deaths; breached around 3:10 p.m., waters reached Johnstown at 4:07 p.m.
- Source opened: [US NPS South Fork Dam](https://www.nps.gov/jofl/learn/historyculture/the-south-fork-dam.htm), history/timeline (observed 2026-11-17).
- Serious objection: much heavier tragedy and responsibility than the warm visual identity of Paper; compelling but risks spending ten minutes on dam geometry and number lists. Public YouTube baselines for this specific angle were not validated within this run.

**Why Lituya over Alcatraz:** both have an immediate tangible visual and changing decisions. Lituya has a documented, complete human payoff *and* a genuine conceptual reveal (run-up vs crest); Alcatraz's best viewer question remains unresolved. Lituya is stronger on verified resolution but worse on recent subject competition. Selection is editorial, not a predicted CTR or retention score.
