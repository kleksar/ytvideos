# Packaging brief — editorial, not production approval

**Working title:** “The Elevator Is Faster. So Why Did My Stairs Win?” / «Лифт быстрее. Почему лестница победила?»
**Other honest candidates:** “Stairs vs Elevator: The 39-Second Flip” / “One Wait Changed the Winner.” Do not suggest the elevator *always* loses.

**Viewer promise:** same fourth-floor destination, two real choices, but a **hypothetical transparent model** with only wait changing between comparisons. A 64s vs B 73s then B 37s. A high-contrast side-by-side of a lobby staircase and closed elevator doors, clock and fourth-floor landing, with A/B iconography from channel profile. Not a photographed or performed race.

**First evidence in narration:** elevator is absent at lobby; show declared full-trip timer starting at same entrance. First payoff: stairs beat B by 9s despite B riding quickly; second payoff reverses when wait falls from 48 to 12 seconds. The 39-second threshold is a model conclusion, not an observed elevator statistic.
