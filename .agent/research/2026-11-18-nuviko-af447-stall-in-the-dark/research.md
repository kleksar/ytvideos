## Three finalists — equal editorial application
**AF447 — selected, English Nuviko.** Why stop: why working engines could not prevent falling, and the contradictory stall alarm. Opening: "How could an airliner lose nearly eleven kilometres of altitude while its engines kept running?" After hook: Pitot obstruction → autopilot off → alternate law → nose-up controls → stall → warning suppressed with invalid measured speed → deep recovery/search. Payoff: measured speed glitch was temporary; the failure to identify the stall made the accident fatal, and nose direction is not flight path. Main objection: one of the most frequently covered aviation events, extremely strong incumbent competition; public view counts cannot establish a new channel's retention. Verified basis: [BEA investigation](https://bea.aero/les-enquetes/evenements-notifies/detail/accident-de-lairbus-a330-203-immatricule-f-gzcp-et-exploite-par-air-france-survenu-le-01-06-2009-dans-locean-atlantique/), [BEA-based detailed analysis](https://v1prep.com/crash-investigations/air-france-flight-447.html).
**Lac-Mégantic — strongest alternative.** Why stop: stopped unattended train rolls downhill and destroys town. Opening: "The crew went to bed. The train kept moving." After hook: inadequate hand-brake test, locomotive fire/engine shutoff, loss of air pressure, runaway, derailing downtown. Payoff: multiple interacting safeguards, 47 deaths, not an unexplained runaway. Strong objection: main TSB full report blocked 403 in this search environment; accessible summaries do not support every detail needed for ten-minute narration; recent English-format evidence weaker. Source index [TSB report](https://www.tsb.gc.ca/eng/rapports-reports/rail/2013/r13d0054/r13d0054.html) — **full page inaccessible, fact-check beyond observed snippet UNVERIFIED**; documentary [MagellanTV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lblbBXRM-UE).
**Eschede 1998 — strong third.** Why stop: one broken ring enters a passenger train from underneath at about 200 km/h. Opening: "A passenger watched a piece of the train's wheel rise through the floor." After hook: wheel fracture → floor intrusion → impact on track switch → derailment and bridge collapse → rescue/legal aftermath. Payoff: accident chain and its nontrivial court outcome, 101 died. Main objection: English YouTube demand baseline thin, major reports in German; legal outcome must not be simplified to a definitive verdict of culpability. Read [NDR report, updated 2026-06-03](https://www.ndr.de/geschichte/schauplaetze/Zugunglueck-in-Eschede-1998-Katastrophe-mit-101-Toten%2Ceschede18.html), [WELT 2002 conductor litigation reporting](https://www.welt.de/print-welt/article413086/Schaffner-konnte-ICE-Unglueck-nicht-verhindern.html).

**Decision**: AF447 beats Lac-Mégantic for English-language independently readable documentary evidence and a second, surprising mechanism after the autopilot loss; Lac-Mégantic has unusually strong everyday significance and would win on proximity to daily life, but ten-minute causal coverage could not pass the same source standard today. Eschede has a more concrete visible object but less defensible English audience signal. The film should not present animation as genuine footage or make the crew into simplistic villains. No certainty of audience interest, CTR or retention is implied.
