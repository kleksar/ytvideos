## Three genuinely competitive pitches

### Columbia — choice for this run
**Exact proposed opening:** “Why did seven astronauts come home in a shuttle that had already been fatally damaged?” After hook: Jan16 81.9-second tank foam impact → Jan17 launch-footage review → request for better orbital photos does not yield them → Jan23 no-concern message to crew → Feb1 heat enters wing and orbiter breaks apart → full-scale foam impact test → board's organizational findings. **Payoff:** seeing the impact was not evidence that the wing was safe. Objection: known fatal ending and high existing coverage; avoid overselling an unobserved visible hole or assured rescue.

### AF447 — strongest alternative
**Opening:** “The jet's speed readings became unreliable. Why did the pilots continue to raise its nose?” Later developments: sensors and autopilot changes → crew response → stall → wreckage and recorder search → reconstructed cause. **Payoff:** how a chain of misinterpreted indications prevented recovery. **Official basis:** [French BEA investigation](https://bea.aero/les-enquetes/evenements-notifies/detail/accident-de-lairbus-a330-203-immatricule-f-gzcp-et-exploite-par-air-france-survenu-le-01-06-2009-dans-locean-atlantique/). Objection: heavy aviation jargon and similar category to existing flight-disaster pitches. Still viable.

### Bhopal — non-aviation competitor
**Opening:** “People woke to burning eyes, but the factory's most dangerous gas was already across the city.” Developments: production and storage → night release → flight and response → health and accountability. **Payoff:** concrete preventable hazards, not merely an explosion montage. Background: [Bhopal Medical Appeal](https://www.bhopal.org/continuing-disaster/the-bhopal-gas-disaster/) and [Ohio State Origins retrospective](https://origins.osu.edu/index.php/watch/bhopal-chemical-gas-disaster). Objection: contested deaths and causation; cannot use unverified exact totals or collapse distinct testimonies into one.

**Why Columbia wins here:** the 16-day delay between a visible strike and catastrophic failure creates an unusual, graspable causal question, followed by decisive laboratory evidence and a serious organizational answer. AF447 might outperform for specialist pilot audiences and remains a credible future pick. None has measured Nuviko demand.

## Story mechanics and promised delivery

- **Stop:** small foam fragment hitting shuttle at 81.9s.
- **Stay:** launch-footage review knows *where* but not *what*; uncertain imagery and previous safety assumptions; crew reassurance; reentry failure.
- **Payoff:** Sandia/board foam test and finding that risk never became verified safety case.
- **Most credible objection:** Columbia coverage may be saturated; avoid familiar catastrophe chronology by centering *unverified wing integrity*.
- **Recommended first-frame concept:** filmic shuttle launch with actual-looking detached insulation, no fabricated contemporaneous hole image. Nuviko host optional, external narrator.
- **Strongest extra 10m value:** hypothetical Atlantis rescue model, explicitly conditional, plus later return-to-flight inspection. Not promised by shorter titles.
