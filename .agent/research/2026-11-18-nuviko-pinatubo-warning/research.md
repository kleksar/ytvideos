## Three evidence-based final proposals

**A. Mount Pinatubo — selected; evergreen experiment, demand not proven.**
- Audience: English-speaking general history / disaster viewers who can grasp a warning decision without knowing volcanology.
- Stop line (02m): “Why would you leave your home when the mountain behind it had been quiet for centuries?”
- Main opening for 5m: two concrete questions about persuading evacuees and the eruption/typhoon collision.
- Development: small April steam blasts → monitoring, tremors, falling gas release, dome → alerts and evacuation before main eruption → June 12 explosions → June 15 giant event plus typhoon → roof collapse even outside hottest-flow zones → lingering lahars.
- Payoff: warnings arrived early enough for evacuation; USGS *estimated* 5,000–20,000 lives saved, while others died and displacement continued. The estimate is not a counted outcome.
- Strongest objection: meteorology and volcano mechanics can become an explanatory report after the eruption; the 10m text actually shows this risk, and archival footage may outperform an illustrated retelling.

**B. Estonia 1994 — strongest alternative, not owner-rejected.**
- Stop: “A ferry's front door failed in a storm. Why did investigators reopen the case decades later?”
- Development: bow visor/ramp failure and rapid car-deck flooding, 852 deaths → renewed inquiry after holes seen in wreck footage → physical modeling and seabed examination.
- Payoff: late-2025 investigation found the new holes consistent with contact with seabed, no collision/explosion evidence, and identified systemic shortcomings—not a miracle or unproven murder.
- Sources read: https://news.err.ee/1609886623/official-investigation-finds-no-new-causes-in-1994-estonia-ferry-sinking (Dec 2025), https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/dec/16/estonia-ferry-disaster-in-1994-caused-by-bow-failure-report-confirms (16 Dec 2025).
- Strong objection: structurally complex, hundreds of deaths and competing theories invite false certainty; the revived inquiry may be fresher than Pinatubo but full independent source/YouTube baseline was not completed in this run. Not a weak decoy.

**C. Nevado del Ruiz, Colombia, 1985.**
- Stop: “A volcano's eruption was not the deadliest part; the river valleys became the disaster.”
- Development: November 13 summit eruption melted ice → lahars traveled along valleys, far from summit → towns including Armero hit within hours.
- Payoff: more than 25,000 killed, real warning and dissemination failures. No invented personal recollections.
- Sources read: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/keeping-watch-over-colombias-slumbering-volcanoes (27 Feb 2015); https://www.usgs.gov/publications/preventing-volcanic-catastrophe-us-international-volcano-disaster-assistance-program (1993).
- Strong objection: grim conclusion and less distinct viewing promise for a general-interest channel than the successful evacuation-plus-typhoon contrast.

**Decision A over B:** B has greater renewed inquiry news value and a clearer late verdict; A has a more visible series of decisions, a distinct physical reversal from wet ash and an honest positive *and* negative payoff. B remains the strongest counterexample to the choice. Selection confidence **moderate for causality; low for likely popularity**. There is no measured ranking of actual viewer demand.

## Causal beats shared; version-specific composition

1. April small steam blasts and earthquakes motivate monitoring; no definite big eruption hour (P1).
2. June shift of earthquakes, decline in SO2 with possible sealing, appearance of lava dome; evidence **strengthens**, not merely accumulates (P2/P3).
3. Expand evacuation before climactic phase (P2); costs to people acknowledged without inventing named personal anecdotes.
4. June 12 eruptions; June 15 peak and Typhoon Yunya multiply weight of wet ash; some deaths outside flow paths (P1/P6).
5. Evacuations limited immediate loss of life; 5k–20k **counterfactual modeled range**, not headcount; disaster still severe (P4/P5).
6. Long-term lahars/displacement and atmospheric aerosol effects extend reach after blast (P4/P8/P9).

Per format: 02m starts with direct hypothetical personal choice and ends with a literal grammatical return to it, after payoff. 05m opens with two questions and adds measurable decision reversals. 10m runs through extended uncertainty/cost/system effects but self-review notes repetition; it is complete, not declared equally compelling.
