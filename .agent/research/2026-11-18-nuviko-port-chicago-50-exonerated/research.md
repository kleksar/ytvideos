### Three competing story pitches; same evidence standard

**Port Chicago:** “Why did the Navy convict men who refused to load ammunition after a blast killed 320 people?” After hook: unequal dangerous loading → unexplained blast → survivors ordered back → 258 refuse → 208 return / 50 face mutiny → convictions before the explosion inquiry is finished → long struggle and **2024 exoneration of remaining 256**. Payoff: the Navy's own court-martial errors, not an invented ignition cause, were formally reversed. **Strong objection:** low public YouTube interest evidence and risk of narrating racial injustice as simplistic one-person villainy; must be measured in a future Nuviko test. The 2/5/10 variants add real phases without fake quotes.

**Lac-Mégantic:** proposed first: “How did a parked train start rolling after firefighters had already put out its engine fire?” After hook: flawed brake test → locomotive fire → power shut off → braking air leaks → train rolls downhill → derailment/fire → systemic safety failures. Payoff: apparently unrelated firefighting and an inadequate handbrake test combined; 47 deaths. Source: https://www.tsb.gc.ca/eng/rapports-reports/rail/2013/r13d0054/r13d0054-r-es.html , section Key Issues/Findings; accessed 2026-11-18. **Strong objection:** diagram-heavy mechanism with very high care needed not to blame responders.

**Air France 447:** proposed first: “How could a passenger plane stall in the Atlantic after its speed readings stopped agreeing?” After hook: icing of probes → conflicting airspeed and loss of automatic assistance → stall → accident record and safety learning. Payoff: understanding why instrument failure alone was not the full causal chain. Source https://bea.aero/en/investigation-reports/notified-events/detail/accident-to-the-airbus-a330-203-registered-f-gzcp-and-operated-by-air-france-occured-on-06-01-2009-in-the-atlantic-ocean , closed investigation summary, 228 persons; accessed 2026-11-18. **Strong objection:** several recent Nuviko air-disaster topics in queue reduce novelty, and cockpit assertions require strict BEA fact-checking.

**Selection verdict:** Port Chicago has the strongest *second-plot reversal*: surviving the blast creates a new threat in the form of a military trial; the last event is an actual dated legal reversal, not a weak moral. Lac-Mégantic is stronger on mechanical causal trace and could have higher click interest; it remains available, not rejected. Port Chicago's broad demand remains UNVERIFIED despite institutional/historical coverage.

## Core factual evidence and limits

Read on 2026-11-18. Source bodies are primary institution/official or a secondary interview; exact claims traced below. Quotes are not copied into narration.

1. **U.S. Department of Navy, 17 July 2024 official release**: https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/Press-Releases/display-pressreleases/Article/3841792/the-secretary-of-the-navy-exonerates-256-defendants-from-1944-port-chicago-gene/ . Opening and investigation findings: **320 killed**, approximately **400 injured**, white officers given hardship leave while Black enlisted sailors were ordered back; **258 originally convicted, 256 remaining exonerated on 17 July 2024**. Found **joint trials despite conflicting interests**, **no meaningful right to counsel**, and decisions **before Navy court-of-inquiry report** including **19 substantive loading-safety recommendations**. The other two convictions had previously been set aside. This is a finding on trials; does not identify ignition source. Read via indexed source body/search on date; direct open returned HTTP 403.
2. **National Park Service, Mutiny Trial**: https://www.nps.gov/poch/learn/historyculture/the-mutiny-trial.htm , “Background”, “Aftermath”, “Trial Proceedings”: 17 July 1944, 320 dead, **202 Black enlisted sailors** dead; exact cause never established, inadequate training, 9 Aug **258 refuse**, **50 mutiny**; 1944 trial Sept 14-Oct 24, defense led by **Gerald Veltmann** with **Thurgood Marshall/NAACP public advocacy**, hard-labor sentences; 1999 Freddie Meeks pardon; 2024 reversal. This page sometimes calls hazardous practices the “direct cause” while also acknowledging **ignition cause unknown**; script preserves that distinction. Not all 258 were charged with mutiny.
3. **Naval History and Heritage Command, Port Chicago Naval Magazine Explosion**: https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/nhhc/browse-by-topic/wars-conflicts-and-operations/world-war-ii/1944/port-chicago.html ; item identifies two ships E.A. Cause of blast unknown; unsafe system documented but not proved immediate spark. Do **not** portray Thurgood Marshall as defense attorney in the 1944 court; Gerald Veltmann was. Do **not** pretend all 256 participated in one mass mutiny case. The 1948 desegregation was broader, Port Chicago a contributing pressure, not independently sufficient cause. Written texts distinguish these.

## Promise, hook, payoff, map and rights

**Audience promise:** sailors who survived an unexplained ammunition explosion were prosecuted for refusing to load again; an exact Navy decision decades later reverses those judgments. **First event:** segregation and inadequate loading training in a wartime port. **First picture concept:** historical recreation of pier loaded with ships and wartime ammunition, no fake “authentic footage” label. **Hook is not merely blast size:** the new order after the blast changes the danger. **02m exact first line:** “Why did the U.S. Navy put fifty sailors on trial for mutiny after hundreds of their shipmates died?” **05m exact first line:** “What does it take for a sailor's refusal to become a charge of mutiny?” **10m exact first line:** “How does a sailor's refusal to handle ammunition become a wartime mutiny charge?”

**Causal chain with evidentiary limits:** unequal 1944 loading assignments/training → July 17 massive blast (trigger unknown) → surviving sailors ordered back under disputed conditions → Aug 9 refusal 258 → 208 return / 50 remain → mutiny convictions + separate summary courts → trial before complete inquiry → civil-rights criticism, reduced sentences → 1999 single pardon → Navy reviews fundamental legal defects → **July 17 2024 final 256 cleared**. This preserves the second, more surprising narrative after the initial catastrophe.

**Payoff:** legal verdict reversed; initial ignition still unresolved. This is more meaningful than a “we learnt lessons” ending. English/Russian make the same important distinction. The recommendation may favor 5m, not necessarily 10m: long text risks turning into a general history of military desegregation.
