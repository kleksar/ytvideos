## Promise → first proof → reversal → full answer

- **Promise:** the crew reached the child deep underground but still could not pull her out. Why?
- **First spoken questions:** “Why would rescuers drill a tunnel below a trapped child instead of straight toward her? Why could they hear her voice for two days without being able to touch her?”
- **First event:** a toddler slips into an eight-inch well in a normal Midland yard; a microphone fits but no adult can descend; a wide second shaft is drilled deeper beside her.
- **Reversal:** “Now they could reach toward her. And still, they couldn't bring her out.” Her right leg is stuck, the connecting passage still too small. Do **not** depict rescue as already completed upon first breakthrough.
- **Payoff:** carefully enlarging the passage lets O'Donnell free Jessica, Forbes lift her, and physicians treat her afterward. The 58-hour outcome is fully stated, not reserved for clickbait.
