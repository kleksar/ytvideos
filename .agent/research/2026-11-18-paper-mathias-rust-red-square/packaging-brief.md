## Truthful user-facing promise

Small German Cessna was detected by Soviet radar and encountered a MiG but still arrived near the Kremlin. Reveal *how the identification chain failed*, and the surprise that the famous landing was on a **road bridge**, not the open Red Square paving. The story neither claims a stealth miracle nor suggests Rust alone ended the Cold War.

Title concepts for later verified tests:
1. **A Soviet MiG Saw His Plane. He Still Reached the Kremlin**
2. **The Teen Pilot Who Reached the Kremlin — With Soviet Jets Watching**
3. **The Plane Soviet Radar Saw but Couldn't Stop**

Thumbnail A: one 3D handcrafted full-frame paper/card miniature, giant gray MiG-23 scaled against a tiny white Cessna in Soviet sky, distinctive thick folded-paper wings, physical shadows/visible fibrous cut edges, Moscow landmark behind, large slightly irregular card letters **THEY SAW HIM**. The scale contrast is immediately legible on a mobile screen; no misleading bullet strike, no photograph masquerading as archival evidence.
Thumbnail B: folded-card Cessna descending over the **Bolshoy Moskvoretsky roadway**, cars and actual bridge wires, St. Basil's beyond in a plausible sightline, lettering **HE LANDED HERE**. Use natural light and restrained neutral color to keep contrast readable.
First proof: start with two questions and paper plane overhead Moscow; follow the Stockholm-route deviation and *real* Soviet interception. Main payoff: multiple sightings were reclassified as authorized aircraft, and the landing caused dismissals.
