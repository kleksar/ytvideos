## Three competing pitches (before full text)

**1 — Rust, chosen.** Cold opening: "A Soviet fighter jet pulled alongside a rented Cessna, then flew away." An eighteen-year-old from West Germany crosses Soviet airspace and reaches Moscow in 1987. Stop: a visible fighter and a civilian plane in a place protected by missiles. Stay: Finnish controllers search for a crash, Soviet units track and investigate, several controllers incorrectly classify the same target, the pilot lands on a *road bridge*, and the government removes defense leaders. Payoff: the plane was repeatedly detected, not invisible; the failures were in interpretation and handoffs. Main objection: familiar evergreen story; comparable public English videos do not establish large current demand.

**2 — 1932 Emu War, strongest broad-familiarity alternative.** Cold opening: "Australian soldiers brought machine guns, but the birds kept eating the wheat." Stop: absurd encounter; stay: dispersed emu flocks, failed motorized chase, subsequent return with changed approach. Payoff: it was a pest-control operation, not a literal war with a victorious bird army. Main objection: much of the appeal is contained in a meme headline, risking a repetitive 5/10m treatment. Real background: ABC Australia, 2022-12-10, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2022-12-10/great-emu-war-90-years-on-army-wheatbelt-battle-history/101752238 . Current comparable YouTube response remains UNVERIFIED.

**3 — London Great Smog, strongest social-consequence alternative.** Cold opening: "In London, people stepped outside and could barely see the next doorway." Stay: coal burning, weather inversion, traffic disruption, deaths not immediately counted, policy change. Payoff: the true human cost exceeded early estimates, not just a strange fog. Main objection: multi-day tragedy needs nuanced mortality estimates and lacks one decisive action; overcompressing it to 2m could trivialize lives. Real English comparables: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5N1SpLMa5o and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vkx-2mT1-q4 . No player/transcript viewed.

**Why Rust beats the others:** it has both a self-contained 2m reveal (MiG saw it) and a longer chain with escalating distinct mistaken identifications leading to a physical bridge landing. Emu War has a catchy premise but a weaker second act; Smog has deeper societal value but its disaster toll and resolution are more diffuse. **Counterargument:** Rust's public view counts are mixed/low, and attractive history alone is not proven demand for this English paper channel. This selection is a candid experimental evergreen hypothesis, not demonstrated high CTR or search traffic.

## Fact ledger — opened source pages, not just search snippets

- **Smithsonian Air & Space**, Tom LeCompte, July 2005, https://www.smithsonianmag.com/air-space-magazine/the-notorious-flight-of-mathias-rust-7101888/ : passages from "On May 13, 1987" through "At about 12:21" document Reykjavik practice / Helsinki flight plan; paragraphs "At the Tampere air traffic control facility" through "Soviet controllers continued" confirm Finland search, alert and radar; paragraphs "Now nearly two hours into his flight" through "The MiG accelerated" confirm actual visual MiG-23 contact; passages "At 3 p.m." through "In the handoff report" explain training, code reset, rescue helicopters and lost origin; passages "As he circled" through "Rust pulled up in front of the church" specify touchdown **on bridge**, taxi past car and wires, St Basil's; passages "On June 23" onward confirm sentencing, August 1988 release and removal of defense leaders. This article relies on Rust's later recollections, Soviet investigators and retrospective analysis; it is not a flight's primary cockpit log.
- **NDR** 2017-05-28 https://www.ndr.de/geschichte/chronologie/Mai-1987-Wie-einer-ueber-Kreml-flog,derkremlflieger101.html : age **18**, bridge, attributed peace claim and failed Gorbachev meeting; other interpretations of politics not proof one flight ended Cold War.
- **NDR** 2012-05-27 https://www.ndr.de/geschichte/chronologie/Mathias-Rust-Mit-der-Cessna-172-am-Roten-Platz,rustchronologie100.html : bridge landing 1987-05-28, four-year sentence 1987-09-04, release 1988-08-03 after approximately fourteen months.
- **German Broadcasting Archive** https://www.dra.de/de/entdecken/ueber-den-wolken/eine-cessna-auf-dem-roten-platz-mathias-rust-in-moskau/ : explicitly states age eighteen, loaned aircraft and that his stated motive is disputed due to inconsistent later accounts. Thus scripts do **not** present motive as objective verified fact.
- **WELT** 2017-05-27 https://www.welt.de/geschichte/article164975633/Mathias-Rust-So-narrte-ein-18-jaehriger-Deutscher-die-sowjetische-Flugabwehr.html : age eighteen and bridge, early historical summary.
- Contrary details: NDR various dates quote landing 18:43 or 19:30, and some retrospectives wrongly round Rust to 19. Since birth was June 1, 1968 and flight May 28, 1987, age was eighteen. Narration deliberately does not assert a precise hour. In 1987 he used the road bridge, **not** a direct runway touchdown on the plaza. His motive and some accounts of radar operators are reconstructed, not certain direct dialogue.

## Causal story map and staging boundaries

1. Visible MiG/Cessna contradiction → which warning failed? Source: Smithsonian MiG paragraphs.
2. Stockholm flight plan → Finland notices wrong heading / false crash search → Soviet radar alert.
3. Soviet first visual inspection and later close MiG-23 → still no effective landing order.
4. Training-fighter region, friendly-code error, helicopter and district handoff → track becomes misleading; exact reasons rely on later reconstructions.
5. Pilots reaches Moscow, land on road bridge amid wires/cars, taxi to St Basil's → encounter with pedestrians, arrest.
6. Sentence and removals → original question resolved: human handoff failure, not magical radar stealth.

**2m** compresses 1–6 but still gives complete result. **5m** shows cause and choice, with clear contrasts. **10m** adds verified preparation, KAL007 decision constraints, specifics of codes and wires, later Cessna fate; these have standalone factual value but a denser middle.
