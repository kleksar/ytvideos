## Exact story maps, comparison and gaps

**02m:** first line wrong-hole contradiction → 18 at work → one crew escapes/nine trapped → air pocket → narrow drill & taps → larger broken drill → new shaft → nine survivors → wrong map → thematic loop back to first wrong hole. The complete human payoff precedes the loop; title remains understandable on cold start. Textual loop read-through by author, not AV verification. Last EN: “The hole that trapped them began with a wall the map had placed somewhere else.” → first EN “A hole in the wrong place flooded a coal mine.” RU repeats the corresponding cause rather than withholding the ending. No measured AV seam, `UNVERIFIED`.

**05m:** questions → compact setting and anticipated safety gap → breakthrough exposes actual risk → nine trapped and build refuge → narrow signal but too small → pumping/air and broken large bit → voice confirms all nine → one-by-one capsule rescue → legal map discovery and qualification. Payoff: nine men alive; no perfect map was available.

**10m:** three opening questions → contrast of mines/plan → historical collapse in working face → bifurcated crews → refuge choices → first narrow contact → pressure/pumps → larger drill break, backup rig → contact and nine lifts → map provenance, museum record still incomplete → safety-policy consequences. Extra segments are independently fact-based, but the last third lacks a comparable escalation.

**Incremental value 2→5:** gives identifiable refuge behaviors, landowner drilling logistics, separate narrow and large drill functions, bad bit, first two-way confirmation, pain symptoms, and archival map caveat. These materially explain how they survived and why mining plan failed.

**Incremental value 5→10:** develops air pressure, the stop in drilling as experienced below, second rescue hole, survey provenance and mapping reforms. However the middle repeats urgency and the aftermath becomes legal/administrative rather than surprising. On current text, **5m is preferred**. Ten minutes is not better by default, and no media for other lengths should be prepared.
