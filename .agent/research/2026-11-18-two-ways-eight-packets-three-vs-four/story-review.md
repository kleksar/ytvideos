### 02m EN — author self-review (confidence medium on structure, low on demand)

1. **Interest 8:** The problem “I've got eight sealed packets of identical screws” makes the hidden underfill and minimum-test question easy to grasp. Strong relevance for viewers who like choice puzzles; ordinary shopping use is speculative.
2. **Opening 9:** Eight packets, one known missing screw and a two-pan balance appear before “Would you choose A ... or B ...?”, so A/B decision is not rigged after the reveal.
3. **Development 8:** A's four-to-two-to-one suspect narrowing contrasts with B's balanced first test, which unexpectedly clears six. True change of inference after the hook.
4. **Payoff 9:** “Two weighings, whatever the result. B wins by one weighing” gives a guaranteed answer and explains the third possible scale outcome; not an invented time saving.
5. **Freshness 6:** Eight counterfeit coins is an established puzzle; sealed screw packets are an illustrative setting, not a new discovery.
6. **Visual 8:** A cyan 4/4 and B amber 3/3+2 can be represented in one stable tabletop; no image generated or verified.
7. **Spoken 8:** “Leaving packets untouched isn't skipping information” is natural verbal reasoning, although multiple counts in a 2m film may challenge real comprehension.
8. **Viewer demand UNVERIFIED:** Two historic YouTube puzzles with different creators/formats offer hints, not audience retention or new-channel demand.
**Main weakness:** length >35–50s Short and known riddle. The revised ending “For the next round of this model, I reset the table” invites a repeat of the literal cold opening, but independent twice-through seam and actual AV seam UNVERIFIED; text alone does not create a verified loop.

## Comparison of finished texts and recommendation

**02m** delivers meaningful choice, two observable strategies, guaranteed result, mechanism and caveat without excess branches. Most concise full story. **Recommend 02m editorial script** even though **NOT production-ready** on current channel Short.

**05m** adds guaranteed behavior for each possible first weighing, verifies the same lighter packet remains across the reset, and explains that one ideal weighing cannot distinguish eight candidates. This meaningfully corrects the possible objection that B is just lucky. But a viewer who grasped 3:3+2 may no longer wait through all branches.

**10m** adds index-specific examples, the ninth and tenth candidate threshold, number of placements (14 versus 8), and possible failures with real bag mass/digital scale. These are logical additions, not fabricated real events, yet after its midway payoff the final third is at high risk of feeling like an educational appendix. REWORK rather than stretching voice or claiming a ten-minute entertainment payoff.

**Decision:** editorial strength 02m > 05m > 10m for this A/B channel. We cannot claim expected views or retention from these ratings. Long output has no authorized production recipe. No fabricated “winner” under an altered set of assumptions.
