## Audience promise and truthful framing

The shopper has three planned gifts and two exclusive discount offers under **explicit example conditions**: 25% off all three vs the lowest-price gift free when buying three. Show the same basket on both sides and reset to the original prices before changing the item prices. The surprise is a winner flip at the same $60 pre-sale total, not an allegation about a named retailer.

**Candidate titles for editorial testing (not measured CTR):**

1. **25% OFF vs FREE Gift: Which Deal Wins?** — promise matches intro and the $45 vs $48 opening case.
2. **Same $60 Cart. Two Deals. Opposite Winners.** — more intriguing causal promise, requires showing price redistribution clearly.
3. **When a FREE Gift Costs You More** — only if visual makes clear it means *more than the alternative promotion*, not a higher price than without discounts.

**Primary recommendation:** “Same $60 Cart. Two Deals. Opposite Winners.” It accurately foreshadows the actual $24/$24/$12 vs $20/$20/$20 contrast. All offers hypothetical. Do not put an actual store logo, invented current savings, or falsified verified receipts in visual treatments.

## First action → hook → reveal → payoff

- **Preview frame:** two equally sized receipt outlines from one three-gift cart; cyan A “25% OFF” and amber B “3 for 2,” with prices hidden until narration names them. Full 2D illustration per Two Ways Lab; real approved reference inputs would be needed in a later supported production task. No game HUD, Paper fibers or stock photorealism.
- **First spoken 05m promise:** “I have three gifts to buy, and two sale signs to choose from.” By the next lines the viewer knows both choices and that identical $60 totals can give opposite winners.
- **First meaningful action:** gifts visibly priced $24/$24/$12, whole cart resets between simulations; A computes $45, B computes $48. **Pause for A/B choice before the first number is revealed.**
- **Turning point:** three $20 gifts, still $60, B computes $40 vs A's $45. No new promotions or concealed changes; only price distribution is adjusted and announced.
- **Main 05m payoff:** tie at $25/$20/$15 and comparison of cheapest eligible item to 25% of total. Separate new scenario: do not buy a third item you didn't need just to qualify.
- **Human close:** choose actual planned purchases, read eligibility, calculate what the cashier would take, avoid buying unnecessary extra items. No guaranteed savings across stores.
