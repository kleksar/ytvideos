## Three competitive finalists

**Aberfan:** Opening question: “Why did a Welsh village have to help pay for the removal of the very coal waste that had buried its school?” First image: school beneath the spoil tips, not graphic victims. After hook: saturated Tip 7 → wrecked school → Nansi shielding five pupils → inquiry and other tips → disaster fund charged for clearing → 1997 admission. Payoff: responsibility and delayed nominal repayment; the story changes from immediate survival to institutional recovery. Strong objection: new BBC October 19, 2026 documentary *Aberfan: The Village That Fought Back* has already covered this exact aftermath. Viewer interest for a brief illustrated English Nuviko Long **not proven**. Do not make an overly simplistic allegation of £150k paid directly by individual families.

**Piper Alpha:** Opening: How could maintenance on one platform leave 167 dead? First visible action: offshore platform, equipment and permit handoff. Development: maintenance/permit communication, cascading fires, crew isolation, inquiry and changed offshore practices. Payoff: fatal coordination failures amid attempted response. Strong objection: technical systems easily become lecture, 2025 BBC documentary covers survivors; 10m source work substantial. [UK Parliament inquiry response](https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1990-11-12/debates/180108e7-71f9-48af-bbcc-e06ce1c640ba/PiperAlpha), [Step Change in Safety](https://www.stepchangeinsafety.com/workgroups/major-accident-hazard/remembering-piper-alpha/), [RTS on BBC film](https://rts.org.uk/article/behind-scenes-disaster-sea-piper-alpha-story).

**Operation Fortitude:** Opening: How do you persuade an enemy that the real invasion is still a decoy after the landings begin? First visible action: false radio positions and staged forces. Development: FUSAG/Patton deception and the Pas-de-Calais expectation after Normandy, later intelligence interpretation. Payoff: deception maintained beyond D-Day. Strong objection: evidence of German decisions must not become invented single-cause “saved D-Day” claim; another Ghost Army PR makes some image/angle overlap. [English Heritage history](https://production.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/dover-castle/history-and-stories/d-day-deception/), [National WWII Museum](https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/podcasts).

**Why selected:** Aberfan has both an immediate concrete human action (Nansi Williams shielding five children) and a verified *second conflict with a real decision and specific reversal* (1968 contribution, 1997 ministerial refund). Compared with the closest alternative Piper Alpha, the viewer need not memorise platform systems; compared with Fortitude, the final government's admission is direct rather than a war-history counterfactual. Strongest objection remains the **recent competing BBC documentary**, not dismissed.

## Source matrix: claims read, provenance and limitations

1. **British Geological Survey**, [Aberfan 1966 landslide case study](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/case-studies/aberfan-1966-landslide-case-study/) (accessed Nov 19, 2026): seven tips from 1916; only Tip 7 active, movement before 07:00; 107,000m³ mass hits disused canal and breaks water main, crosses embankment; destroys 18 houses, Pantglas Junior and part of secondary; end ~09:15; last living child rescued ~11:00; 144 dead, 116 children. BGS reconstruction, not direct live footage.
2. **National Library of Wales**, [Aberfan archival introduction](https://www.library.wales/discover-learn/digital-exhibitions/archives/aberfan-disaster-1966) (accessed Nov 19, 2026): October 21 school/groundwater explanation, rescue and no later live survivors; archive identifies fund, letters and community records. Archive landing page is not a review of unseen image/audio files.
3. **Glamorgan Archives**, [Aberfan Tribunal](https://glamarchives.gov.uk/blood/Aberfan/) (accessed Nov 19, 2026): government commission Oct 26, 76 days of hearings, overall Board blame; public documentary finding. No invented individual conviction.
4. **Tribunal text transcription**, [Report discussion of earlier local warnings](https://dmm.org.uk/ukreport/553-52.htm) (search excerpt observed Nov 19, 2026; full open timed out): letters and 1963–64 discussions are corroborated by [ITV's overview and tribunal quotation](https://www.itv.com/news/wales/2021-10-21/how-the-aberfan-disaster-is-still-being-remembered-55-years-on). Do not turn a genuine pre-disaster concern into a fabricated precise evacuation order or imply officials predicted the exact 1966 hour.
5. **ITV Wales**, [Nansi Williams account, July 3, 2026](https://www.itv.com/news/wales/2026-07-03/man-calls-for-recognition-of-godmother-who-died-saving-pupils-in-aberfan-tragedy) and [BBC interview with survivor Paul Harries, Oct 15 2026](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw1m49yve2n4o) (BBC article access via mirrored search text, direct BBC access not established): five children survived shielding; Nansi died; in 2026 Paul described learning of event from parents. ## Promise and causal chain

Promise: “A village paid from its relief fund for the removal of the danger that caused the disaster — who made that decision and when was the money returned?” Audience: English-language general-history viewers with no prior knowledge of Aberfan, Welsh coal policy or the NCB.

Cause/effect: tipping on wet spring → saturated waste travels into school → rescue shows human consequences → inquiry names organisation responsible → other tips persist → community demands removal → £150k fund contribution in 1968 → Parliament nominally repays in 1997 → modern inspection framework. Do not imply a direct invoice was sent to individual parents; do not conflate refund with compensation for lives.
