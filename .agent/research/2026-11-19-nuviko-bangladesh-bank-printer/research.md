# Research — Bangladesh Bank: the silent printer and the $81 million

Observed 2026-11-19. **Editorial research only; no official ytv run.** Nuviko History, EN illustrated documentary. One story, three independently composed 02m/05m/10m Long proposals. No viewer performance inference from public counts.

## Audience / reason to watch

English-speaking general viewers do not need prior knowledge of SWIFT or Bangladesh. The simple familiar problem: *someone can take money by impersonating the account owner*. The vivid first object, a bank printer that stopped producing statements, leads to a meaningful contradiction: nearly $1 billion of fraudulent orders was mostly blocked, yet $81m escaped and legal recovery remained unresolved a decade later. The core dramatic arc is **compromised sender → valid-looking orders → partly working payment controls → printer/different weekends → Sri Lanka reversal versus four Philippine payments → casinos and recovery struggle → still-active investigation**. No scene of a criminal's private thoughts or keystrokes is asserted.

## Three credible finalist pitches

**A — Bangladesh Bank, the printer and the four payments.** First: “How do you steal eighty-one million dollars from a bank without opening its vault?” Development: $951m requested, most held, $101m processed; printing fails; Sri Lanka typo reversal does not save four Philippine transfers; SWIFT/New York Fed distinction; 2025 confiscation order but not verified cash return; 98th probe delay in 2026. Payoff: *an authenticated instruction can still originate from a compromised bank*, and reversing cross-border funds is radically harder than sending them. Objection: technical-looking title and already-existing big documentaries; 2025/26 legal follow-up can sound bureaucratic unless connected to the initial theft.

**B — WannaCry, the domain that slowed a ransomware outbreak.** First: a hospital network is under ransomware attack and a researcher registers a suspicious domain. Development: hundreds of thousands of vulnerable systems, infection check calls out to a domain, registration changes observed propagation. Payoff: the kill switch slows one part of the outbreak, but is not a global cure and vulnerabilities remain. Objection: the twist is a single technical mechanism, with a well-known existing explanation; ten substantive minutes would demand more confirmed lived consequences. Primary: [NHS Digital 2017 alert](https://digital.nhs.uk/cyber-alerts/2017/cc-1411), [WIRED 2017 reporting](https://www.wired.com/2017/05/accidental-kill-switch-slowed-fridays-massive-ransomware-attack/).

**C — Operation Entebbe, the aircraft and the rescue.** First: a hijacked aircraft with hostages on the ground, a rescue force planning a very distant air operation. Development: selection of captives, planning constraints, surprise landing, rescue and casualties. Payoff: rescue succeeded, but not without loss of life, including hostages and an Israeli soldier. Objection: enormously strong visual/physical tension but higher risk of oversimplified context and ideological sensitivity, and saturated film/TV retellings. Primary: [Israeli Ministry of Defense archive](https://archives.mod.gov.il/sites/English/Exhibitions/Pages/Operation-Entebbe.aspx).

**Comparison:** C arguably has the strongest instantly understandable physical stakes. A has a clearer unique second act and present-day consequence in evidence dated 2025/2026, plus an object-based opening (silent printer) that converts an abstract transfer into an action. B has a highly shareable technical aha but little additional narrative after the reveal. Winner A, with *experimental* audience demand, not measured superiority. Strongest rejected-for-now alternative: **Entebbe**, not owner-rejected. The risk to A is becoming a financial chronology rather than a human action; versions must return to what employees could see and what each held/moved payment did.

## Factual guardrails and production boundary

No bank vault physically opened. No hack of the Federal Reserve Bank's own computers is established. SWIFT provides messaging, not bank reserves. The $20m spelling recovery is *one* payment; the rejected ~$850m is a separate set; the $81m is the Philippine flow. DOJ 2018 allegation must be attributed. 2025 order ≠ money recovered. Present-day legal procedures are kept short relative to the human bank-office chronology.

Nuviko's illustrated reconstruction: depict generic terminals, printed reports, bank interiors, map locations only as separately grounded geography; do not show invented identities, exact intercepted message text or archival photos as if they were generated evidence. 2D traveler is external explainer, not a participant; actual character PNGs/reference bytes unavailable here. No image/TTS/MP4 generated, no media or rights claim.
