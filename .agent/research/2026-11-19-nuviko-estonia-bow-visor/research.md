## Three *competing* fully specified finalist pitches

### A — MS Estonia, **selected**

**Stop:** “A passenger ferry's enormous bow could tear away while the bridge's lock light stayed green.” Specific physical contradiction from [JAIC 1997 chapter 13.4](https://onse.fi/estonia/chapt13_2.html), not invented eyewitness dialogue.

**Stay:** late-night metal bangs → inspection not detecting a fault → visor attachment failure under waves → ramp pulled open → wide vehicle deck floods and ship lists → narrow passenger evacuation window → later ship-side opening produces a second genuine investigative question.

**Payoff:** [joint 2025 safety authority assessment](https://www.turvallisuustutkinta.fi/en/news/news-archive/mv-estonia-preliminary-assessment-damage-on-starboard-side-originates-fromcontact-with-seabed/) attributes starboard damage to contact with seabed rock, and reaffirms the bow-failure flooding sequence; uninspected structural weaknesses and certification anomalies explain why the disaster was not a single bulb or one crew decision.

**Strongest objection:** fatal passenger disaster could become a dry technical lecture or exploit grief. 10-minute editorial variation reveals its own pacing problem; verified 2025 official conclusion is *not* evidence that all private investigators agree. No claim of novel 2026 discovery.

### B — Sewol, **strongest human alternative**

**Stop:** passengers, mostly students, were told to stay while a ferry was sinking. **Stay:** instructions and evacuation, captain and rescue decisions, then later scrutiny. **Payoff:** why ordinary compliance could make a human disaster worse, and why accountability continued for families. **Evidence:** [International Documentary Association overview](https://documentary.org/online-feature/absence-examines-sewol-ferry-disaster) and [Aeon on the 29-minute documentary](https://aeon.co/videos/they-told-us-to-stay-put-the-south-korean-ferry-disaster-that-sank-lives-and-trust), [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10464687). **Strongest objection:** exceptional human resonance but archival documentary style, language, historical context and representation of students are poorly matched to a speculative Nuviko reconstruction without additional primary evidence and ethical treatment; the available public view signal of a documentary cannot predict an illustrated channel's retention. Not rejected for future consideration.

### C — Eschede, **strongest clean-mechanics alternative**

**Stop:** a fractured piece of a wheel set off a chain that destroyed a high-speed train. **Stay:** wheel tire comes free, strikes the underfloor and track components, switches divert wagons, bridge struck. **Payoff:** why a small crack destroyed a full train and bridge, killing 101. **Evidence:** [Eschede municipality's technical narrative](https://www.eschede.de/seite/419030/ice-unglueck), [NDR historical article](https://www.ndr.de/geschichte/schauplaetze/Zugunglueck-Eschede-Wie-ein-Radreifen-zur-Katastrophe-fuehrte,eschede4.html). **Strongest objection:** a compelling visual chain, but public searchable competing clips are mostly unattributed German-TV/National Geographic reuploads; do not borrow copyrighted footage or call their views a baseline. Estonia offers a two-stage physical and investigative payoff. This finalist remains viable.

**Comparative judgment:** Sewol beats Estonia in direct human decisions; Eschede is cleaner mechanically. Estonia beats neither by automatic “rarity”; it wins this editorial round on the *combined* precise lock-sensor misconception, passenger consequence and independently documented 2025 examination of the new-looking hull breach, which changes what the viewer understands twice. Possible loss at 10 minutes is documented rather than hidden.
