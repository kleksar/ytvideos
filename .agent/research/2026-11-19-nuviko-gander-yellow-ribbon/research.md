## Selection / what the audience gets

Начальная заявка: «Город с 10 000 жителей неожиданно получил 38 самолётов и около 6 600 гостей. Как он справился, если их нельзя было просто отправить дальше?» **After hook:** самолёты сели, но посадка не даёт жилья; пассажиры ждут проверки, волонтёры перестраивают школы и залы, нужны лекарства и телефоны, хозяева решают проблему доверия; воздушное сообщение возобновляется лишь через несколько дней. **Payoff:** люди благополучно продолжили путь; сохранились личные связи и благодарность. **Strong objection:** история сильно освещалась в 2026 к 25-летию, есть популярный мюзикл; зритель может счесть ответ предсказуемым, fresh demand слабый.

### Three substantial competing propositions

1. **Gander** — opening EN: “How did a town of about ten thousand people look after more than six thousand unexpected visitors?” Visual: runway packed with diverted aircraft then local public hall filled with beds. Main turn: “The airport could take the planes; the town would have to take the people.” Payoff: public buildings, prescriptions, phones, homes; later friendships. **Objection:** familiar sentimental story, 2026 CBS anniversary short modest views. **Primary:** Transport Canada and 9/11 Memorial below.
2. **2003 blackout (strongest competing)** — opening EN: “How could one afternoon turn off electricity for fifty million people?” Visual: Ohio grid control room with failed alarms then cascading regional blackout. Development: line trips and shifting loads, loss of situational awareness, recovery and recommendations. Payoff: not “one switch”; interdependent failures. **Objection:** excellent engineering explainer already exists and singular human thread harder. **Primary:** DOE, original task force.
3. **Flight 1549** — opening EN: “Both engines lost thrust less than two minutes after takeoff. Why did a river landing save everyone aboard?” Visual: aircraft after bird encounter, rescue craft on Hudson. Development: glide/ditching/evacuation/rescue as separate dependencies. Payoff: all 155 survived, five seriously injured. **Objection:** high stakes but extremely familiar result and feature film, less new perspective. **Primary:** NTSB/FAA.

**Decision:** Gander beats blackout here on the cumulative changes after the hook and on concrete nontechnical human payoff, not on invented popularity. Blackout plausibly outperforms on search volume/engineering audience, which remains UNVERIFIED. Film dramatization not a production source. No fictional passenger dialogue or thought.

## Primary / direct evidence (read, not just a headline)

- **Transport Canada, _Four Days in September_** — https://tc.canada.ca/en/11-09-2001-four-days-september (accessed 2026-11-19): sections “Operation Yellow Ribbon”, Gander account and minister/aviation inspector recollection. 224 diversions and >33,000 passengers nationwide; at Gander 38 aircraft and around 6,656 travelers/crew (narration rounds to 6,600); airport/security restrictions; mayor Claude Elliott recalls initially discouraging hosting strangers; Lufthansa Gander-Halifax naming; report of pilot who lost friends at WTC. Some details are **retrospective recollections** and are attributed as such, not omniscient scenes.
- **National September 11 Memorial & Museum collection, _Come From Away / 9/11 response_** — https://collection.911memorial.org/Detail/objects/122231/rel/1 (accessed 2026-11-19): passengers accommodated in schools, churches, legion halls; food, cots, bathing facilities, local pharmacies offering prescriptions, free telephone lines and later lasting contacts. Retrospective museum overview, no individual names for every act.
- **9/11 Memorial, _Come From Away Tells a Story of Compassion_** — https://www.911memorial.org/connect/blog/come-away-tells-story-compassion (accessed 2026-11-19): approximately 38/6,500, community hospitality and later musical; no theatrical scene imported.
- **Government of Canada / Transport Canada**, 2011 community recognition: https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2011/09/public-safety-minister-toews-transport-minister-lebel-congratulate-gander-their-9-11-community-resilience-award.html and **9 Wing Gander history** https://www.canada.ca/en/air-force/corporate/who-we-are/organizational-structure/1-canadian-air-division/9-wing/history.html (accessed 2026-11-19): civic and military logistical contribution.
- **DOE, _August 2003 Blackout_** https://www.energy.gov/oe/august-2003-blackout (accessed 2026-11-19): August 14–15, ~50 million customers across NE U.S. and southern Canada; baseline for finalist, not Gander script.
- **NTSB, Flight 1549 investigation** https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/Pages/DCA09MA026.aspx and **FAA lessons** https://www.faa.gov/lessonslearned/transportairplane/accidents/N160US (accessed 2026-11-19): both-engine thrust loss after bird strike, 150 passengers + five crew, 2009 Jan 15, ditching on Hudson, 5 serious injuries. Used only for competitor.

**Claim boundaries:** Do not portray all 33,000 diverted to Gander (only ~6,600 there). Do not say the town “cured” any 9/11 trauma or caused airspace to reopen. Airspace closure/emergency and hospitality are separate processes. Personal invitation/friendship is not universal to all travelers. Rounding and actor source context are explicit.

## Causal documentary chain used in all three scripts

US airspace closes → diverted flights land at Gander (Transport Canada) → security/customs and onward flights remain unresolved (Transport Canada) → public buildings and volunteers accommodate arrivals (9/11 Memorial) → clothes, pharmacy, phones solve needs after safe landing (9/11 Memorial) → cautious safety warnings versus locals inviting guests (mayor recollection) → flight reopening and departures over days (Transport Canada) → returning visitors, friendships, Lufthansa naming (Transport Canada/Memorial).
