## Audience promise and first events

- Audience: English general-history audience unfamiliar with military prisoner camps and the 1963 film; no reliance on celebrity recognition.
- **Proposed title A:** `The Great Escape Worked. Then 50 Prisoners Were Murdered.`
- **Title B:** `76 Escaped the Camp. Only 3 Reached Safety.`
- **Title C:** `The Great Escape: How a Tunnel Victory Became a Tragedy`
- Thumbnail **concept**, not a sourced photo or finished asset: historical barracks with raised floor on sandy ground, a visually honest cross-section concept showing a physically low tunnel reaching just short of forest, one small group silhouetted at exposed outlet. Contemporary illustrative composite MUST NOT be labelled archival. Avoid Nazi emblems used purely for sensation, invented firing scenes, misleading portraits or blood. No unowned photos directly pasted in.
- First VO of 05m: `Why would prisoners build three tunnels beneath a camp designed to stop tunnelling? Why did their escape work for seventy-six men, but save only three? And what happened after the guards found the hole?`
- Early confirmation: `In March 1944, two hundred Allied airmen were waiting inside Hut 104` plus physical hidden passage beneath stove; factual specifics grounded in RAF.
- **Progression promised:** preparations, Tom/Dick/Harry, exposed exit, rope signals and 76 out; *then* the captured/murdered aftermath. Keep human costs respectful and causal.
- **Payoff:** 73 recaptured, only 3 successfully escaped, Gestapo murdered 50 after capture following Hitler's orders. Do not imply 50 were shot while escaping, and do not treat a forced return to POW camp as reaching safety.
- Packaging honesty: do not show an imminent rescue or bullet wound that is not in the record. Do not claim the tunnel ended beneath trees or that all 76 reached freedom.
- Competing angle in October 2026 publisher's 114-second video: `The Real Great Escape: 76 Out, 3 Home`; literal **76/3 alone is not a novel title**. Differentiate by real mechanics + deliberate after-capture killing, not unverified “never told” claims.
