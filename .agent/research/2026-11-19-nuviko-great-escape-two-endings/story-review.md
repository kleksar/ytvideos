### 02m EN — why each number, principal weakness

- **I9:** “Seventy-six ... only three” plus 50 murdered, substantial human history rather than merely a clever tunnel.
- **H8:** the exact opening asks two understandable numerical questions; second one has implied stakes.
- **D8:** “The tunnel ended short of the trees” changes the practical problem, then the guard's discovery changes the plan.
- **P9:** “two endings: the tunnel worked, and fifty prisoners were murdered after it did” resolves precisely the opening paradox.
- **A6:** 1963 film and another creator's 2026-10-21 114-second *76 out/3 home* version already use central angle; not fresh packaging.
- **V8:** distinctive narrow shaft, rope, guard tower, 76 out / 73 recaptured shown through photographic historical reconstruction.
- **C8:** mostly natural sentences, but “a manhunt” to 50 murders happens quickly.
- **S UNVERIFIED:** public examples show both indexed 30k and 29-view results; no creator-matched baseline/retention.
- **Main weakness:** sudden jump from tunnel to postwar trials, insufficient individual people. Confidence medium and author-only.
