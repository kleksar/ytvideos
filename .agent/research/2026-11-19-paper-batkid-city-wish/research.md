## Three real finalist pitches, without weak strawmen

**A — Batkid (selected):** Opening “A five-year-old became Batman for a day. San Francisco turned itself into Gotham City.” The city produces genuine staged actions in sequence: police chief call → cable-car rescue → Riddler bank heist → Penguin takes baseball mascot Lou Seal → stadium rescue → mayor gives city key. Why stop: a real city's scale applied to a universally understood childhood wish. Why stay: the challenges escalate from small private wish to thousands of participants and then become a question of what remains after the day. Payoff: 2023 Make-A-Wish reported Miles cancer-free ten years after treatment and Natalie granting other children’s wishes. **Strongest objection:** existing 2015 Warner documentary, major original news saturation, lower actual life stakes than alternative B, and possible 10m charity-report second half. Distinction is real organization/choices and long-term reciprocity, not “rare history.”

**B — Chilean miners (strongest competitor):** “A drill emerged with a message: all 33 were alive.” 2010 San José mine collapse, discovery after 17 days, supplies to isolated men, drilled shaft and capsule, all 33 rescued after 69 days. Cold opening, major middle obstacle and full survival payoff are stronger than A for pure suspense and longer technical Long. Primary NASA history https://www.nasa.gov/history/history-publications-and-resources/oral-histories/chilean-miners-rescue/ and satellite/technical overview https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/san-jose-mine-chile-46018/ ; no invented miner conversations. Counterargument: global adaptations and more technical exposition; Batkid fits Paper's tactile miniature city and humane after-story better. Audience advantage *not measured*.

**C — Thai cave rescue:** “The team entered the cave; floods closed the way out.” The team was trapped, rescue required complex divers and evacuation; full outcome is rescue, not invented new rules. Strong human suspense, potentially superior to A for some audiences. Counter: primary operational chronology, detailed diver accounting, rights and casualty context were not gathered deeply enough to safely write our 10-minute documentary here. BLOCKER for this candidate: missing verified primary operation log. No false “all details verified” claim.

Decision A, not because of easier drawing or novel rarity, but because its first action, escalating mission chain and post-event change have confirmed sources. B might win an actual YouTube A/B; not tested.
