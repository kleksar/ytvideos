## Comparison of fully written scripts

**2 → 5 minutes:** 2m covers injury, failed negotiation, jury's allocation, judge reduction and confidentiality. 5m earns the extra time with a meaningful *opposing argument*: McDonald's many cups sold vs about 700 complaints; responsibility for an everyday mistake vs the chain's chosen policy; jurors do not simply award money, they apportion fault. It strengthens the viewer's ability to judge the case rather than merely debunk it. Possible loss around “The company also argued...” if visual interest is low.

**5 → 10 minutes:** the complete 10m text develops skin-graft severity, the mechanics of the temperature risk, rarity denominator, legal difference between compensation and punishment, judicial reduction and its public-memory afterlife in greater detail. Some of this is useful. However, after “The amount they agreed on was not publicly disclosed,” the story repeatedly revisits fault/corporate policy and slows while the audience already knows the answer; it remains **REWORK**, not a fictitious stronger ten-minute choice.
