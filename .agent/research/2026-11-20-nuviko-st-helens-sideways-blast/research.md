## Three competing full pitches

### A. Selected: Mount St. Helens

**Exact proposed 05m opening:** “How can a volcano destroy a forest before the towering eruption cloud appears?” First intended image: real-shape reconstruction of the north bulge before the collapse (not an archive claim).

**Stop:** recognizable volcano apparently violates expectation of an upward explosion. **Stay:** March tremors, measured bulge 140m, May 18 quake, landslide removes pressure, blast travels north at least 480 km/h, then mudflows and a lake with no drainage. **Payoff:** pressure unloading explains sideways blast; after eruption engineers had to control Spirit Lake water, with tunnel opening in 1985. **Strong objection:** many already know 1980 eruption, popular 57s video does not imply a 5m retelling will work; biology and engineering after climax may become a textbook.

### B. Air France Flight 358 — strongest competitor, not a weak straw man

**Opening:** “The airplane ran off the runway and caught fire. All 309 people got out.” **Stay:** warnings of poor braking, glideslope deviation, touchdown roughly 3,800 feet into 9,000-foot runway, runway overrun, evacuation before fire reached escape routes. **Payoff:** no fatalities, although TSB final report records 12 seriously injured, and causes explain why landing distance mattered. **Strong objection:** multiple existing documentary reconstructions and much longer niche aviation coverage; competing for same audience without differentiated angle. **Source:** TSB final [A05H0002](https://www.tsb.gc.ca/eng/rapports-reports/aviation/2005/a05h0002/a05h0002.html), Summary; event August 2, 2005; final report December 12, 2007. It is independently a compelling survival story, kept as future alternative.

### C. British Airways Flight 009 — stronger immediate mystery hook

**Opening:** “All four engines failed above Indonesia, but the airliner kept flying.” **Stay:** volcanic ash, powerless glide, cooling and restarting engines, landing in Jakarta. **Payoff:** 263 survived; the hazard was volcanic ash, not ordinary weather. **Strong objection:** official original investigation not obtained this pass; the accessible [Code7700 technical account](https://www.code7700.com/case_study_british_airways_9.htm) explicitly cites a later pilot book rather than an official report. Full causal 10m treatment would risk unsupported details. This is an evidentiary blocker, **not a declaration that the audience would dislike BA009**.

**Choice:** A beats B for **additional second-phase payoff** and coherent 5m escalation from fast eruption to years-long hydrological danger, not because a volcano is inherently more popular. B may beat A on personal stakes, which remains the main editorial risk. A beats C on source depth. If independent review finds A unengaging, reject rather than inflate scores.

## Verified documentary basis (actual URLs opened; accessed 2026-11-20)

- [USGS: 1980 Cataclysmic Eruption](https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/mount-st.-helens/science/1980-cataclysmic-eruption), sections “Precursory Activity”, “Debris Avalanche”, “Lateral Blast”, “Plinian Eruption”, “Lahars”: March 16 first quake; March 27 steam eruption; >10,000 quakes by May 17; bulge ~140 metres outward, ~2 metres per day; 8:32 a.m. May 18 M5.1; 2.5 km³ debris avalanche; pressure release; blast ≥480 km/h; devastated ~600 km²; plume above 24km within 15 minutes; main phase nine hours; lahars travelled the Toutle/Cowlitz network. “Swollen wall caused disaster” is causal simplification; hazard onset timing was not perfectly predictable.
- [USGS: Landslide and Spirit Lake](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/landslide-may-18-1980-eruption-mount-st-helens): outlet blocked, lake raised by the slide; USGS public-domain page for factual evidence, not a source image downloaded for this production.
- [USGS Volcano Watch: Spirit Lake](https://www.usgs.gov/news/volcano-watch-legacy-mount-st-helens-1980-eruption-still-defies-perfect-solution): temporary water pumping installed in 1982, controlled tunnel in service from 1985; flood breach was a projected danger, **not a flood that happened**.
- [USGS: lakes and drainages](https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/mount-st.-helens/science/lakes-and-drainages-around-mount-st-helens): other waterways altered and risk management.
- [USGS: Explosions and dome growth](https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/mount-st.-helens/science/explosions-and-dome-growth): eruptions and lava dome after May 1980. No claim of immediate dormancy.
- [USGS: David Johnston legacy](https://www.usgs.gov/observatories/cascades-volcano-observatory/legacy-david-johnston): volcanologist at north observation post among killed; no unsupported speech/last words.
- USGS disaster summaries: 57 people killed or missing; avoid assigning 57 solely to the lateral blast if count covers eruption.
- [TSB Canada Air France 358 final](https://www.tsb.gc.ca/eng/rapports-reports/aviation/2005/a05h0002/a05h0002.html): alternative verified via original investigation. BA009 only second-hand source as above.
