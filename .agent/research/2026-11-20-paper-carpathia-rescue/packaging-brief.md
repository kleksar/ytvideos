## Recommended: 05m Long, EN SHA-256 `0c89028a4b98ab0cdff51c770b2ca8c7d064e34c8dea27bdef340fef2487e4f7`

**Primary title:** `Titanic Was Already Gone. This Ship Still Had 700 People to Save.`

**Alternative title A:** `The Ship That Came Too Late for Titanic — But Saved 700 People`

**Alternative title B:** `Carpathia: The Rescue Began Before the Ship Arrived`

These are honest: Carpathia recovered more than 700 people but could not rescue everyone; title `700` is conservative shorthand for >700. The title should not imply it arrived before Titanic sank or that all victims survived. No expected CTR or view uplift asserted.

**Thumbnail promise:** One massive lifeboat seen close to the high paper side of Carpathia; much smaller human paper figures with physically believable proportions, warm light from prepared dining room windows above, dark sea and ice in background. Empty horizon where viewers expect Titanic. 2–3 large chunky paper letters if used: `TOO LATE?` (question about liner, not all survivors). Actual detailed thumbnail design needs independent facts/rights and channel typography checks, not generated here.

**Start payoff chain:**
- First spoken questions ask why a captain drove into ice and what he could do before seeing passengers.
- First verifiable action: Cottam runs with the distress signal; Rostron turns toward the coordinates.
- Main hook after meaningful context: Rostron cannot park a tall ship beside a tiny lifeboat and simply open a door.
- New physical steps: doctors in three dining rooms, ropes, canvas lifting bags, own boats ready, repeated pickup.
- Full payoff: preparations made receiving 700+ survivors possible; he returns west to New York.
- Counter-promise: does **not** claim faster sailing would have saved every person, quote private conversations, or show an actual moment of Titanic sinking.
