## Comparable three finalists

**A. Tetris (chosen):** “Why did a man bring a Tetris cartridge into a Soviet office and come out questioning the rights to his own product?” Visual: physical paper cartridge, Elorg paper and two different device silhouettes; no fake photo. After hook: existing Japanese cartridge production → Moscow officials question console rights → 'computer' not necessarily Famicom or Game Boy → Rogers negotiates directly → Nintendo obtains separate permissions → Tengen's console cartridge restrained in June 1989. Payoff: courtroom distinguishes Atari/Tengen from licensed Nintendo versions, while Tetris succeeds on Game Boy. Strongest objection: major 2018 documentary and 2023 Apple film have saturated broad historical tale; legal explanation could sound like a contract lecture. Selected narrow **one existing cartridge and legal trap** angle for 05m; 10m has weaker payoff ratio.

**B. 2003 blackout (strongest alternative):** “How could one electrical failure grow into darkness for 50 million people?” Visible physical cardstock grid cutaway, staged line trips and alarms; after hook several regional failures and incomplete diagnosis → grid cascade → 50m disruption and later reforms. [US Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov/oe/august-2003-blackout), [Canada–US task force 2004](https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2004/04/canada-task-force-presents-final-report-blackout-august-2003.html), [Ontario system operator](https://www.ieso.ca/en/Corporate-IESO/Media/Also-of-Interest/Blackout-2003). Serious objection: possibly more universal than Tetris but harder to tell a precise 5m causal chain without oversimplifying multiple infrastructure failures. **Still viable for future consideration**, not owner-rejected.

**C. Mont Blanc fire (strong finalist):** “Why could motorists not escape a fire in a tunnel with exits at both ends?” Physical road-tunnel cross section, visibility and ventilation issue, later 39 deaths and safety changes. [French parliamentary inquiry](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/rap-oecst/tunnels/r2388.asp), [2026 Italian firefighter historical memorial](https://www.vigilfuoco.it/chi-siamo/memoria-storica/notizie-storiche/linferno-sotto-la-montagna). Objection: details of individual responders and cause of death require deeper inquiry checks; easily becomes distressing chronology and 10m repeats causes. Any suggestion one simple decision alone caused all 39 fatalities is unsupported.

The selected A has recognizable object, distinct obstacle, reversal and competing commercial outcomes. B is arguably stronger on universal relevance and loss of everyday services; jury remains an editorial hypothesis, not a proved ranking.

## Actual opened evidence and precise limitations

- [Tetris Company history](https://tetris.com/news/the-history-of-tetris), accessed 2026-11-20, 1984/1989/1996 chronology, Game Boy association. Official brand source and interested party.
- [Tetris Company bios](https://tetris.com/corporate-bios), accessed 2026-11-20, Alexey creator, Henk business role and later partnership. No independent factory inventory.
- [2023 interview, Henk Rogers as firsthand source](https://www.timeextension.com/features/i-never-did-that-but-i-did-something-just-as-stupid-henk-rogers-on-apples-tetris-movie), accessed 2026-11-20, opening and “The 'Tetris' Story”: 1988 CES, Japanese BPS Famicom, 1989 Moscow, his claimed ~200,000 cartridges and ~$2m financial exposure, relatives' property collateral, Elorg console authorization issue, rivals. **Attribution important**: these are Rogers's retrospective recollections; do not present an official factory audit or verbatim government records.
- [2025 Rogers interview, The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2025/may/12/henk-rogers-interview-tetris-kgb), accessed 2026-11-20, direct recollection of meeting Alexey and the film inventing car-chase scenes. This article inconsistently mentions a 1988 Moscow visit; the official history and 2023 Time Extension place licensing trip in 1989, which is what scripts use. Do not portray scripted Hollywood chase/KGB danger as verified history.
- [BBC 2012 documentary transcript](https://subsaga.com/bbc/documentaries/factual/2012/tetris-from-russia-with-love.html), accessed 2026-11-20, near 47–50m section describes Nintendo versus Atari licensing dispute and court. Available TEXT transcript, documentary not watched. Does not replace original court document.
- [Tetris for Atari Games, chronological overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetris_%28Atari_Games%29), accessed 2026-11-20, May 1989 Tengen release and June injunction. Secondary support; actual 1989 court case primary filing UNVERIFIED. Never invent judge quotations or legal damages.
- [Tetris Company About](https://tetris.com/about), accessed 2026-11-20, game mechanics and 1984 origin.

**Fact-sensitive assertions:** Nintendo's console rights distinct from Henk's handheld deal. Tengen/Atari was restrained by court; DO NOT claim Rogers's Japanese Famicom cartridge was court-banned. Computer/console is a legal interpretation, not an assertion that consoles have no processors. Original rights controlled by Elorg, not privately sold by Pajitnov in 1989. Rogers's numerical cartridge and collateral account qualified every time. 1996 partnership after later emigration, not instant 1989 royalties. Media film's chase fictionalized. Script does not copy competing transcripts or use fabricated speeches.
