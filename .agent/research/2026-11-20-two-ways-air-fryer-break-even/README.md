## One shared factual basis / precise scope
Published Which? laboratory test for 320 g frozen chips: air fryer 0.287 kWh/23 min; existing electric oven 0.863 kWh/33 min. Model: 26p/kWh and £30 hypothetical purchase for a fryer **assumed to achieve the tested energy use**. Per meal saving 14.976p; break-even on 201st use (£100: 668th; £285: 1904th). These are mathematical proposals, not verified performance of a £30, £100 or £285 appliance.
