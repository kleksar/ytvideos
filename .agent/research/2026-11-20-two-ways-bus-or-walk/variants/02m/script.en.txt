Should I wait for this bus, or walk the rest of the way?

I'm standing at a bus stop in a made-up city. My destination is twenty minutes away on foot. Once I get on the bus, the ride takes ten minutes. Both choices start here and finish at the same door. I'm comparing arrival time, not comfort or ticket price.

Which would you pick: A, wait for the bus, or B, start walking?

The display says five minutes. I give A its five-minute wait, then ten minutes on board. Fifteen minutes altogether. B takes twenty. Waiting wins by five.

Now I reset the same scene. Same walking route, same bus ride, but the display says fifteen minutes. If I choose A, I reach the door in twenty-five minutes. With B, I get there in twenty. Walking wins by five.

So neither method always wins. There is one number that changes everything. The bus saves ten minutes of travel once I'm aboard. If the remaining wait is shorter than ten minutes, A wins. Longer than ten, B wins. At exactly ten, they tie.

These are calculated times in a model, not two trips I actually took. A late bus or a slower walk would move the answer.

I still have to make the choice before I know the result. And whenever I'm back at a bus stop, the whole decision begins with one question.
