# Packaging brief — Two Ways Lab gas detour

**Source variant:** recommended `variants/02m/script.en.txt`; exact SHA-256 `3477cfab2ce6dbddf37bfd7a0c62bc4878a66088871c020856c29abc82105616`. Not a storyboard, thumbnail asset, prompt batch or approval. Two Ways illustrated cyan A / amber B, not paper or historic documentary style.

**Audience:** motorists making a small/large refill decision; comprehension without prior expertise. The title must not imply observed local fuel prices or a real filmed trial.

- Proposed honest title: **I Drove for Cheaper Gas. Would It Actually Save Me Money?** **Caveat:** since no physical journey was filmed, prefer **Is the Cheaper Gas Station Really Cheaper? (A vs B)**. The second title is approved only as a candidate, not release metadata.
- Alternate: **A Cheaper Pump, a More Expensive Trip?**
- Thumbnail/first graphic concept: schematic home at the fork of A (cyan, $1.80/L, 4km return) and B (amber, $1.65/L, 24km return); both start at same house. No real station logo or fabricated map screenshot. Exact graphic typography only via the channel's allowed generated-image method; no media generated in this PR.
- First spoken question: "Which gas station is cheaper by the time I get home?" Immediate proof: two signs; then same-fuel-at-home metric before choice.
- Spoiler-safe promise: one low price might lose when I need less fuel.
- Complete payoff: B saves **$3.41** with 40L net at home, A saves **$1.09** with 10L; crossover **17.28L**. Not just a general disclaimer about detours.
- Short loop: final "Before I turn the wheel toward a bargain, I need to ask the question again." → first question. Author conceptual seam only, independent listener and real AV seam **UNVERIFIED**.
- Risk: highlighting prices in isolation makes viewers assume real world prices; on screen mark **MODEL / EXAMPLE PRICES**, and explain same net fuel level, not same pump purchase.
- Scene planning/prompts deliberately absent: under editorial, only recommended exact master after textual checks and independent review; asset references/timings cannot be fabricated.
