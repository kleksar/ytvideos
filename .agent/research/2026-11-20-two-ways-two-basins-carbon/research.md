## Finalist A — two-basin manual versus optimized dishwasher
**Who stops:** a kitchen task everyone recognizes, with a counterintuitive published result challenging the assumption that a machine always wins. **Who stays:** first reveal typical hand wash loses badly; the comparison changes when BOTH methods follow stated best practices; later time creates a second but clearly distinct finish line. **Payoff:** 1610 vs 1960 kgCO2e in ten-year 2020 US LCA, manual two-basin lower by 350kg (~18%), with uncertainty and non-generalization.

First EN line (02m): “I'm comparing two ways to clean the same dirty dishes. One uses two basins in my sink. The other uses a dishwasher.”
Main objection: behaviors are not experimentally identical across all homes; Whirlpool funding, small behavior study, region-specific model; broad dishwasher content is not evidence of channel-level interest.

**Primary evidence (read 2026-11-20):** [University of Michigan Center for Sustainable Systems, Porras et al., published Feb 12 2020](https://css.umich.edu/publications/research-publications/guide-household-manual-and-machine-dishwashing-through-life), DOI https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/ab716b . Institutional abstract directly reports all 4 GHG totals: **typical** manual 5620, **typical** machine 2090, **recommended** manual 1610, **recommended** machine 1960 kgCO2e, 4 loads × 8 place settings per week × 10 years, recommended manual 18% lower. Also typical lifecycle water: 34200 vs 16300 US gallons; **do not attach these to best-practice pair**. Costs without assigned time value favour manual; time-value-inclusive assessment changes picture. Corrigendum: text's “249%” reduction was wrong; corrected to 71%; neither number repeated in scripts.
**Publisher/institution account:** https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/682229 (University of Michigan release, Feb 12 2020): two basin = hot wash/cool rinse; running tap worst, dishwasher tips no pre rinse and no heated dry; observation 38 Whirlpool staff at manufacturer; funding/collaboration disclose in research.
**Efficiency context, different scope:** https://www.energystar.gov/products/dishwashers (ENERGY STAR; current page observed 2026-11-20) covers full-load efficiency relative to typical manual use, not best-practice lifecycle carbon pair. Do not merge estimates from different baselines.

**Math checked:** 4×52×10 = 2080 loads. 1960−1610 = 350 kg. 350/1960=17.857% ≈18%. Approx per-load 1610/2080=0.774, 1960/2080=0.942, difference 0.168. Illustrative 35-minute time difference ×2080/60=1213.3 hours; NOT a measured study result of the exact two recommended methods.

## Finalist B — coffee, cold milk now vs later
Hook: “I can make my coffee hotter ten minutes later by putting cold milk in sooner.” Development: both cups remain on desk, later-milk cup appears hotter until final pour; Newton model vs evaporation. Payoff: under explicit model early milk remains warmer; [Rees & Viney, 1988 abstract](https://openalex.org/W2157893741), DOI 10.1119/1.15572; [2013 Mathematics Magazine article](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.4169/math.mag.86.3.204); [public calculator](https://www.videophysics.com/newtons-law-of-cooling) estimates only 0.9°C difference in a typical illustrative case, which limits emotional payoff. Real cooling depends on cup, evaporation and milk storage.
Objection: weak video comparator evidence and tiny change in typical illustration. Strong alternative; **not rejected globally**.

## Finalist C — cold vs hot laundry
Hook: “The part of a washing machine that costs most energy isn't the spinning drum.” Development: compare one normal load and how water heating dominates, then investigate stains and sanitation exceptions. Payoff: [ENERGY STAR Clothes Washers](https://www.energystar.gov/products/clothes_washers) says heating water can account for ~90% of laundry energy and cold wash is generally adequate except some cases. [Peer-reviewed 2006 comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16646382/) shows 80–85% heating in conventional top-loads, not necessarily modern all-in-one machines. Objection: no verified identical-cleaning outcome for a specific load, 10m becomes general laundry advice.
