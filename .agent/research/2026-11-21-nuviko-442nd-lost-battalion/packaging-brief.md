## Promise, first seconds, and genuine payoff

- Viewer: general EN-language history audience, not presumed to know 100th Battalion, 442nd or 141st.
- Factual promise: a cut-off U.S. unit was saved, but their Japanese American rescuers had just been taken off combat duty, had to overcome successive defended ridges, and took heavy casualties. The race to reach the pocket is the narrative.
- The 05m opening starts with three distinct factual questions: how two American units reached each other, why the rescuer regiment was exhausted, why saved headcount isn't the entire story. In the first meaningful segment, show rain/forest and the 141st advancing beyond support; do not wait a minute to demonstrate the trap.
- New main hook after immersion: the 100th/442nd is ordered back before it can recover, while the isolated unit receives too few supplies.
- On-screen payoff: October 30 Company I patrol under Takashi Senzaki reaches trapped Americans, then Company B platoon from the other direction. **211 saved**. Later scene clarifies casualties are killed + wounded with source-window caveat and shows continued service/Truman postwar context.
- Do not reveal a fabricated named individual's private thoughts, infer what all commanders believed, or inflate casualty figures into deaths.
