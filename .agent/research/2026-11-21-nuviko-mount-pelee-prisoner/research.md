## 4. Promise / causality / payoff

- Audience: EN worldwide viewers who have never heard of Martinique or Cyparis; don't need to recognize scientific vocabulary.
- Headline promise: "**The Prison Cell That Saved a Man When His City Burned**." Not "sole survivor."
- Revised cold opening 02m (HEAD after this update): “Two minutes after Mount Pelée erupted, a burning cloud struck Saint-Pierre.” The factual May 8 outcome opens; documented movements **toward** the city move to the ending, which leads causally into the opening on replay. An earlier candidate asserted an unsupported overnight timing of a prison door closing; this date/door action is not in S1–S7, so it was removed. Author text seam provisionally PASS; independent double cycle and AV UNVERIFIED.
- Long opening 05m: “Why did a prisoner survive a volcanic disaster that destroyed almost an entire city?” Main hook after content: first deadly mudflow → second, entirely different cloud. Answers city shelter / prisoners' stone cell / false sole-survivor tale.
- Long opening 10m: “How could a prison cell become the safest room in a city of thousands?” Wider contrast: maritime escape of Roddam, grounded prison confinement, city-margin survival and modern science. Includes dated contemporary maritime account.
- Core proof path: warnings → people entering Saint-Pierre → May 5 fatalities → new kind of explosion → May 8 ground-hugging lethal current → confined man shielded by stone → rescued May 11 → second survivor refutes simplified billing.
- Payoff: “That locked stone room had kept him from leaving. It also kept most of the disaster from reaching him.” Qualify "most," not absolute invulnerability.
- Objection: an extremely tragic event risks spectacle; no images of historical victims used or generated without permissions. Source narrative does not assume guilt or emotional states.
