## Three genuinely competing pitches

**LEGO (chosen, experimental demand):** Open with the physical octopus in a boy's hand and its 27-year journey. The cargo ship lost 62 containers in 1997; many themed pieces dispersed. The story moves from tracing shoreline finds to two 2024 rare discoveries and to the documented plastic persistence study. The payoff is why the boy could find an octopus decades later, why the shark emerged from the seabed, and what this says about the material's survival. Why stop: a recognizable toy in an impossible place. Why stay: first one family, then another kind of find, then the mechanism. Strongest objection: delightful curiosity risks becoming a slow environmental explainer, and no reliable recent audience comparison for Paper was found.

**Pepsi 349 (strongest alternative):** Open with a family watching a winning number printed on its cap; then reveal the second security code, failed claims, a company offer of P500, litigation and violence. Payoff is the documented distinction between printed number and valid security code, plus the human consequences. Court rulings and contemporaneous reporting support multiple reversals, so its 10m may be stronger than LEGO's. Strongest objection: the deaths in 1992 demand a serious tone and could make the paper-toy style insensitive; audience comparison differs. Current English demand: 2021 Qxir video near 988k cached views, 2026 small Short near 644 cached views. It is not rejected as a future story.

**Pudding Prince:** Open with a man purchasing thousands of cheap pudding cups for frequent-flyer miles; follow the arithmetic and distribution. Payoff is how he converted an overlooked promotion into travel benefits. TIME 2000 first-person reporting supports the key turns. Main objection: after revealing the arithmetic and real reward, a ten-minute documentary would need independent supporting material not yet collected. Not discarded permanently.

Decision: LEGO has the clearest Paper first-frame object, physical journey with two human payoffs and a 2024 follow-up plus a scientific third act. Pepsi has stronger raw stakes; LEGO wins this channel fit and tone, NOT proven demand. No claim of viral probability.
