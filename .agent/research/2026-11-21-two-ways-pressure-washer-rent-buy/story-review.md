## Story maps, by actual text

### 02m
- Cold opening "I have one dirty patio and two ways to clean it"; audience knows who, what and two methods. A/B metric: total dollars to perform N equal cleanups.
- State progression: advertised day quote → hypothetical owned tool + $5/use → N=5 rental advantage → N=7 tie → N=8 buy advantage → repeated $8 trip shifts crossover to 6.
- Result: "At six cleanups, rent costs two hundred and fifty-eight, but own costs two hundred and forty-eight." The rest accurately disclaims model inputs and storage/performance.
- Last→first: ending "...one dirty patio, and two ways to clean it" → same opening. This **repeats the object/theme but has not demonstrated a semantic new question**. **Loop self-check: FAIL / REWORK.** Do not assert continuous listening, AV seam, or native Short gate. Keep full 02m as a candidate for a *specific* revision (new hash/review), not as approved master.
- Main defect: the loop does not advance meaning. Longer than normal 35–50s production range; author has not silently compressed it.

### 05m
- Promise at first line: rent or buy? how many weekends until rental gets dearer? First visible action proposed: same soiled patio and two tools; early true pricing from supplier versus explicitly hypothetical purchase. Because this is an **everyday comparative model, not a documentary Long**, do not force a minute of documentary historical exposition.
- Progression: rented first Saturday ($35) vs owned ($215); same task repeated; tie $245 at seven; ownership $250 vs rental $280 at eight; recurring collection at $8/trip changes win by six ($248 vs $258); annual frequency changes time to break even; alternative real rental length/unequal cleaning performance bound applicability.
- Payoff: "For a job I might do once, the rental wins my example. For repeat jobs, travel and frequency can push ownership ahead." Three inputs to decide (quotes, jobs, travel).
- Main defect: the last two practical caveat sections can slow the emotional payoff; no empirical cleaning speed match.

### 10m
- Questions: (1) break-even (2) repeat pickup costs (3) comparable machine? Clear, if longer than necessary.
- Development: exactly the same two cost crossovers, four-year N=4/N=8 frequency, noncash $15/y storage valuation, 48h supplier pricing, real-equipment differences and safety.
- Payoff complete and *self-contained*: seven cleans tie, eight own; with travel six own; repair/storage can alter; no universal winner.
- Main defect: noncash storage arithmetic and maintenance caveats repeat conditional logic after audience already knows the result. All additional claims have source or explicit scenario status, but this is not enough to earn 10m. Author **did one meaningful density edit** reducing EN from 1,842 to 1,374 words, matching RU revision in scope, before SHA above; still REWORK editorially. Do not expand further with fluff.
