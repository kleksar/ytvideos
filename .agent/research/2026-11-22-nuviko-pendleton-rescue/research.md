## Three genuinely competitive proposals

**A — Pendleton: double rescue challenge.** Specific opening: “A lifeboat just thirty-six feet long returned with thirty-six people aboard.” Image: an overloaded wood lifeboat close beside a huge divided tanker, without implying an actual shot exists. Middle: the storm destroys compass/windshield → approach via rolling ladder with 33 trapped → 32 rescued/one fatality → the 36 occupants still have to navigate home. Payoff: small red buoy inside Chatham Bar and all 36 reaching Chatham. Strong objection: audience may have already seen *The Finest Hours*; documentary competition is not evidence of fresh Nuviko demand. Distinction is **two different kinds of navigation/decision**, not a generic “miracle”.

**B — Lake Peigneur: the lake becomes a drain.** Opening: a lake drains into a salt mine and temporarily reverses the flow of the canal. Middle potential: unexpected water movement, machinery and emergency evacuations, a changed waterway. Payoff: mechanism and lasting shape of the lake. Strong objection: popular video versions assert a single culpability mechanism more confidently than the consulted evidence permits; strong factual story still needs drilling / mine contemporary-source validation and fresh angle. This is **strongest alternative** for immediately visual transformation, not a strawman.

**C — Gimli Glider: the measurement error behind a gliding airliner.** Opening: a passenger jet glides toward a former airfield after running out of fuel. Middle: inoperative fuel measurement, incorrect conversion, simultaneous engine power loss, controlled glide and landing. Payoff: error chain plus human recovery. Strong objection: adjacent to already-open Nuviko aviation stories #352 TACA 110 and #349 Apollo 12; ample aviation explainers and adaptations. A potentially more broadly recognizable question than Pendleton, but less portfolio diversity now.

**Selection:** A beats B on source-level minute-by-minute human action and beats C on distinctiveness from *existing in-queue* aviation stories. B could outperform on curiosity or visual transformation. Neither is declared owner-rejected. Source coverage does not measure desirability.
