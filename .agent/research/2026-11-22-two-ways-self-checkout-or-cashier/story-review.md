All scenario switches are named and counterfactual, never made into video of an actual store.

**Was → now (10m)**: old text enumerated 14/15/16/24-item values, announced an uninterrupted series of tweaks, then restarted the conclusion three times; after six-item reversal the main question had effectively been answered and the middle drifted into an abstract lecture. New text replaces repeated sweeps with a second consequential question, **can I trust a twelve-second lead if queue time is uncertain?** A70/A100 yields opposite winners, while six items remain safe over the same assumed range until help is added. It gives the long version a distinct experiment about *confidence in a decision* rather than merely more numerical examples. The cost is still considerable quantitative narration and unsupported Long runtime.

**Added value and attention risk**: 02m gives the complete paradox; likely attention risk is the slow per-item parameter explanation. 05m buys concrete help/wait reversal; probable weakest point is the algebra of the 15-item crossover. 10m buys robustness versus uncertain input (and the 89s counterexample); probable weak point is the second group of scenario numbers after the main eighteen-item revelation. None of those probabilities is measured retention. Avoid simply adding material to hit ten minutes.

It conveys the full answer most efficiently and beats 05/10 for channel's short, visual idea despite being longer than the usual 35–50-second orientation. 05m is a defensible Long exploration, but the channel currently has no configured Long recipe.
