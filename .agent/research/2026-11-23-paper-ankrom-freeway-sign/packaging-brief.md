## Viewer promise and honest first confirmation

Audience: English-speaking viewers interested in unusual real-world choices, urban design and human ingenuity; no LA-freeway specialist knowledge assumed. Genre: grounded surprising art/public-service story with an ethical boundary, **not instructions for illegally altering highways**.

- Verified **physical subject**: two altered physical overhead sign panels (I-5 shield and NORTH plaque), not a forged government order that changed official records.
- Opening questions: why officials let a fake survive; how it looked legitimate; why the artist lost the object but state kept the idea.
- Earliest confirmation: questions and 110 freeway left-exit/tunnel problem; then artist plans two plates. Actual timestamp requires TTS/MP4.
- Verified payoff: original removed/crushed in 2009, official new sign shows same directional information.
- Strongest false reading: California approved unauthorised installation, or the project demonstrably saved millions of lives. **Explicitly avoid both**. Caltrans left completed signs but warned against unsafe procedure; no safety effect measured in our evidence.
