The promise is **not** "some diaries were fake." The reason to remain after the forgery is exposed: **what the fabricated text was trying to make readers believe about the Holocaust, made legible by a 2023 investigation**. 6. **NDR index / critical background**: https://www.ndr.de/geschichte/tagebuecher and https://www.ndr.de/geschichte/tagebuecher/Die-gefaelschten-Hitler-Tagebuecher-und-die-reale-Geschichte-des-NS,hitlertagebuecher126.html — contextual correction to fabrications, identifies true historical setting.
