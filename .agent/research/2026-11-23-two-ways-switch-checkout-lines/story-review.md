### Adding time vs actually adding value

02m already gives viewer a choice, the surprising first winner, a declared condition that flips the winner, and a real take-away: watch remaining work rather than completed customers. 05m additionally makes hidden overlapping movement and the exact changeover threshold understandable; useful for an audience willing to pause and inspect the picture. 10m adds a truly new question—risk/deadline, uncertain arrivals, what probability would reverse an expected-time choice—but narrows appeal and could feel like an unsolicited math class. Thus **02m wins on payoff/attention density despite its Short production mismatch**; a production Short around 35–50s would need a new substantial editorial revision and new hash/review, not silent speed-up.
