## Source ledger and claim boundaries
All public pages below accessed 2026-11-24. Original prose; no archival media copied. Distinguish retrospective participant testimony, contemporary-to-award reporting, museum technical history, and government history.

- S1 [Rijkswaterstaat: Watersnoodramp 1953](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/water/waterbeheer/bescherming-tegen-het-water/watersnoodramp-1953), section on disaster + official video transcript: northwest storm + spring tide, 31 Jan–1 Feb, >150 inundation points, second high tide afternoon, 1,836 Dutch deaths. Use national figures as context, never as local casualty count.
- S2 [Reformatorisch Dagblad, 31 Jan 2013](https://www.rd.nl/artikel/redders-nieuwerkerkse-watersnood-bijna-vergeten), paragraphs on harbour and manoeuvre: Heuvelman/Van Vliet retrospective interview; 3 boats, 18m vessel chosen over larger two, Heuvelman age 22, rowboat, bow fixed by people on bank, stern rotated and current seated the hull. Attribute recollections in narration, no invented dialogue, mental state or exact visual sequence.
- S3 [Scheepspost, 23 Jun 2018](https://scheepspost.info/schipper-arie-evegroen-redde-zuidholland-in-1953/), article paragraphs 1–6, relaying Evegroen testimony: water overtopped early, breach around 05:30, about 15m, swept sandbags, vessel 18m, propeller, rowboat exit/return to turn engine off. The primary recording was not obtained. Treat reported details as retrospective, not an eyewitness camera.
- S4 [Reformatorisch Dagblad, 8 Jan 2009](https://www.rd.nl/artikel/heldenfonds-kent-medaille-toe-aan-redders-tijdens-watersnood-1953), paragraphs on event and award: reported breach width 14m vs 18m vessel, mayor requisitioned it, rope, second boat Onderneming II supplied bags because trucks couldn't use dike, warm cabin, two silver medals on previous day and earlier recognition of Evegroen. Width differs from S3: use approximate 14–15m, not exact fit/margin precision.
- S5 [Memorial documentation, Roel Bendijk](https://vanberkelbeelden.pictures/beeldhouwers/a-i/roel-bendijk/herdenkingsmonument-watersnoodramp-1953/), sections monument and boatmen: 1983-10-12 unveiling by Evegroen, sand added to boat and gaps filled with bags, repeated supply journeys and family assistance, 1977 award, absent two other boatmen, 2009 reprint and recognition. Later compilation citing local materials; omit uncorroborated exact trip count, motive and exact words on the boat. No use of copyrighted photos.
- S6 [Rijkswaterstaat: Hollandsche IJsselkering history](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/over-ons/onze-organisatie/onze-historie/onze-monumenten/hollandsche-ijsselkering), sections one ship and first Delta Work: local rescue with ship and sandbags, neighbouring Ouderkerk failure, construction 1954, opening 1958. Does not establish a death counterfactual.
- S7 [Rijkswaterstaat: barrier technical page](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/water/waterbeheer/bescherming-tegen-het-water/waterkeringen/deltawerken/hollandsche-ijsselkering), structure section: steel gates ~80m wide, 12m high, 480t, concrete towers, present-day flood protection. No promise of eliminating all future floods.
- S8 [Watersnoodmuseum: Hollandse IJssel Storm Surge Barrier](https://watersnoodmuseum.nl/en/water-knowledge/learn-about-water-safety/articles/hollandse-ijssel-storm-surge-barrier), Origin/Construction: shipping/drainage precluded fixed dam, navigation lock, reinforced riverbed, thresholds shaped to resist accelerated flow, diversion during construction, one gate in 1958, later additional gate and improved closure in flowing water. Museum dates second gate to 1976; Zeeland archive dates installation 1977. Use 1970s only; avoid false precision.
- S9 [Zeeuws Archief: separate Delta Works](https://www.zeeuwsarchief.nl/zeeuwse-verhalen/de-ramp-van-1953-en-de-deltawerken-2/de-deltawerken-afzonderlijk/), first barrier section: commission recommendation May 1953; wider protected dike region about 3 million residents, dredging Jan 1954, opening Oct 1958, navigation. Population is NOT estimated lives saved; the 10m text explicitly differentiates.
- S10 [Rijkswaterstaat anniversary, 20 Oct 2023](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2023/10/hollandsche-ijsselkering-bestaat-65-jaar), first of 13 Delta Works opened 22 Oct 1958.

Claims crosswalk: storm/elevation/afternoon tide/casualties S1; local situation and harbour S2,S3,S6; mayor/boat sizes/rowboat/rope/current S2–S4; sandballast/second boat/logistics S4,S5; human recognition S2,S4,S5; barrier reason/design/opening and 1970s change S6–S10. Basic flow explanation (a narrower passage accelerates flow in these conditions) supported by S8; no invented measured local hydrodynamics. The 2m omits recognition; 5m explicitly distinguishes uncertain local counterfactual; 10m adds population boundary and engineering without adding false deaths. Place distinction: Ouderkerk aan den IJssel is not Ouwerkerk in Zeeland, and Nieuwerkerk aan den IJssel is not Nieuwerkerk in Zeeland. Historical account rather than assertion that all South Holland remained dry.
