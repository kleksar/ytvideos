# Cape Hatteras — the lighthouse that had to leave its foundation

In 1999 a nearly 60-metre brick lighthouse in North Carolina was relocated intact after decades of attempts to defend its shifting shoreline. The station was reconstructed in its original layout and its light returned after two hurricanes postponed the ceremony.
