Shortlist, equally concrete:
- Heimaey: “How can you build a defense out of the lava that's coming to destroy your town?” First visible situation: harbor and fishing boats, before the fissure; first action evacuation. Development: the escape route itself comes under threat, embankments fail, early hoses cannot reach, steel pipes break, larger pumps and flexible pipes, drilling establishes cooling below the surface. Payoff: the harbor remains navigable and boats/families return; the intervention helped, natural change also mattered. Counterargument: common simplification “water stopped volcano” and uncertain causality. Address explicitly; never claim a controlled counterfactual.
- Abu Simbel: “How do you move a temple carved into a mountain?” First scene monumental facade with reservoir rising; key action wire saw cuts rock, numbered pieces, higher artificial cliff. Payoff: rebuilt temples opened in 1968, wider preservation precedent. Strongest alternative because the object and transformation are immediately recognizable; less dynamic reversals in evidence read so far. UNESCO, https://whc.unesco.org/en/activities/1296/ lines 7–26, opened; November 2022 article: Aswan Dam risk, 1960 campaign, more than 1,000 blocks, 64 m higher and 180 m inland, opening 22 Sep 1968. No claim all displaced Nubian heritage or inhabitants were saved.
- Dutch flood: “How could a ship become part of a dyke?” First scene ship against failing bank, gaps filled with sandbags. Develop local stopgap to subsequent permanent barrier with shipping passage; payoff 1958 Hollandsche IJsselkering. Objection: source uses sweeping “millions saved,” not a verifiable counterfactual; English detailed eyewitness evidence insufficient here. Rijkswaterstaat opened https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/over-ons/onze-organisatie/onze-historie/onze-monumenten/hollandsche-ijsselkering lines 148–166. Do not use claimed millions as fact.

Choice before scripting: Heimaey's two linked rescues give a human reason to follow the engineering, and the later temperature evidence answers a genuine skepticism. Abu Simbel would be equally viable if the exact construction difficulties and modern audience angle were developed; it is not rejected by owner. No topic chosen solely because easy to visualize.
