## Sources and claims

Primary: [SEC administrative order 34-70694, 2013-10-16](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/admin/2013/34-70694.pdf), opened and page images examined (pp. 4–8, 1-based). [SEC release 2013-222](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2013-222). Settlement findings, not a criminal verdict; company settled without admitting or denying findings. Paraphrased in original spoken text. Main evidence locations:

- Order §§1, 11–12: Knight's market role, routing program and splitting parent orders. Example 100 / 50 / 50 is explicitly illustrative, not measured activity.
- §§13–15: unused Power Peg since 2003; cumulative-quantity logic moved in 2005, not retested; new program reused flag; staged installation from July 27 missed one of eight; no required independent check. Not a claim that a single employee bears all responsibility.
- §§16–19: 97 pre-open error emails not designed as alerts, no action before open; old code generated children without accounting correctly for fills; parent completion information not communicated to SMARS; 212 parent orders, over four million executions, 154 stocks, more than 397 million shares, approximately 45 minutes, $3.5bn net long in 80 stocks and $3.15bn net short in 74, eventual loss >$460m. 75 stocks had Knight >20% of trading volume and price moves >5%; better/worse prices for other participants. Executions ≠ number of distinct orders; positions ≠ loss.
- §§20–27: outgoing order controls inadequate; price control 9.5% not applied to 212 opening auction parent orders; temporary account 33 mixed positions, $2m gross limit not linked to automated firm-wide controls; staff observed rapid accumulation but couldn't immediately identify source; PMON post-execution monitoring lacked automated alerts and interlock, volume degraded accuracy; removing new RLP code from seven correctly updated machines worsened incident. Exact termination method not established in sources; no fictional kill-switch action.
- §28: assessment inadequately considered risk of malfunction of routing software itself. SEC press release: $12m penalty and independent consultant, no admission/denial. Merger with GETCO July 1 2013.
- [Knight Aug 6 2012 company disclosure, exhibit 99.2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1060749/000119312512338098/d392396dex992.htm): $400m convertible preferred investment, ~73% common stock after issuance if converted, liquidity/viability, audit committee determined delay for vote seriously jeopardized viability, NYSE did not object to exception. Not a government bailout and not an ordinary loan. Use SEC ultimate >$460m, not early $440m estimate or later differently scoped accounting number.

Alternatives: [Guardian contemporary account, 2009-07-23](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2009/jul/23/united-airlines-guitar-dave-carroll), repair $1,200, refusal, song, airline compensation offer and charity request; [artist interview](https://www.guitarworld.com/features/interview-songwriter-and-author-dave-carroll-united-breaks-guitars) corroborative personal account. [McDonald's promotion summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald%27s_1984_Olympics_promotion) boycott, prizes, shortage reports, costs not disclosed, later repeated; secondary lead, not adequate primary evidence for a new script. No quoted fictional financial amount.

## Promise and objections

Core question: why did an apparently reasonable repair spread the original failure? A concrete, visible comparison of two identical instructions at differently updated machines should establish it without requiring viewers to know trading terminology. Story climax is actual reversal, payoff includes actual survival cost. No invented employee dialogue, emotion or physical danger.
