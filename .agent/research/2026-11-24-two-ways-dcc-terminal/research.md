## Sources and claims (opened 2026-11-24)

- Visa, “What you should know about Dynamic Currency Conversion”: https://caribbean.visa.com/travel-with-visa/dynamic-currency-conversion.html lines 3–22: merchant/ATM can offer home currency conversion; quote, rate and markup disclosures, choice and no forced conversion; decline conversion without preventing purchase/withdrawal. Used for mechanism and terminal decision, not exact prices.
- Visa, “Travel credit cards and foreign transactions explained”: https://www.visa.com/en-us/personal/travel/travel-cards-and-foreign-transactions lines 28–35, describes typical markup often 3–5% and separate ATM fees. Our 5% is a deliberately selected illustrative difference relative to modeled card rate, not a measured average or ECB markup.
- CFPB official commentary to US Regulation Z §1026.4(b)(1), lines 86–97: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/4/ ; foreign transaction fees can apply for purchases in USD outside US or with a foreign merchant; rate may differ by transaction date. Used for conditional “some cards” and equal-fee scenario, not a universal card rule.
- Mastercard indicative converter: https://origin-www.mastercard.us/global/en/personal/get-support/convert-currency.html ; date, bank fee input, indicative rate and caveat from indexed source that issuer may use another rate, authorization time or processing time, merchant conversion not subject to Mastercard's rates. ## Reproducible model (assumed values, not live FX)

USD account; EUR bills. A terminal rate 1.155 USD/EUR and B card rate 1.10 USD/EUR. Fees initially zero. All other conditions, purchases, tax, repayment, currency and items equal. No interest/rewards/different account; simple rounded currency charges. Payment choice changes conversion arrangement, not base euro bill. Compare final USD charged. Use decimal arithmetic, round each charged amount half up to cents.

| Purchase EUR | A USD | B USD | difference |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 200 | 231.00 | 220.00 | 11.00 |
| 80 | 92.40 | 88.00 | 4.40 |
| 20 | 23.10 | 22.00 | 1.10 |
| 40 | 46.20 | 44.00 | 2.20 |
| 340 | 392.70 | 374.00 | 18.70 |

Budget $400 → 7.30 or 26.00 remaining. Rate markup relative to B = (1.155/1.10)-1 = 0.05. Separate scenario same 3% fee on both options: round charges A 237.93, 95.17, 23.79, 47.59; B 226.60, 90.64, 22.66, 45.32. Total A 404.48, B 385.22; hotel difference 11.33. In real cards the fee definition and rates can differ; check issuer terms; not a guarantee.

Sensitivity on hotel only, no card fee, hold quote A $231 fixed: B=200*r. Break-even r=231/200=1.155 USD/EUR. If B r=1.16, B=$232, A wins by $1; this is not a forecast, and no assertion about when or how much rate changes. A quote fixes converted charge at authorization, not all card-side fees.
Separate 10m cash model (not included in four receipt totals): same debit card, €100 cash, assumed operator fee €4 included in amount converted, flat issuer withdrawal fee $3 applied both, no other card/ATM fees. A=104*1.155+3=123.12; B=104*1.10+3=117.40; difference 5.72. Operator/issuer terms may differ. No actual cash advance or transaction.
