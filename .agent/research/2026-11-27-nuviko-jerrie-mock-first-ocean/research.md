- Rosetta: a carved decree with parallel scripts gives a partial key, then a sound-based reading works for older Egyptian royal names. Public interest may be larger than Mock, not proof of fit.

Selection: Mock wins on specific, independently grounded sequential changes and underrepresented non-disaster human agency; insulin would be the better choice if recent audience evidence or medical framing outweighed variety. Main uncertainty is the demand baseline, and overlap with #79; frame Mock around competence and first ocean crossing rather than who wins a race.
