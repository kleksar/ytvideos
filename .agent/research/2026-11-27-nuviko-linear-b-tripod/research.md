### Finalists compared at same editorial level

**Linear B.** Opening: “How do you start reading a script when you don't even know the language behind it?” First image real tablet detail, not a fake translation. After hook: patterns constrain possible sounds; place name hypothesis makes a prediction; Ventris abandons his favored language; an excavator tests readings on new material. Payoff is a matched quantity and multiple vessel shapes. Strong objection: technical linguistics could lose viewers. Keep one grid crossing, Spanish analogy clearly illustrative, end on tangible objects; do not inflate a sole genius story.

**Insulin (strongest alternative).** Opening proposal: “Why did a treatment that would transform diabetes care need a second attempt on its first patient?” Initial patient Leonard Thompson in Jan 1922, early extract limited effect; Collip purified material, second administration substantially improved measures. [Historical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7773348/) and [Endocrine Reviews](https://academic.oup.com/edrv/article/42/5/503/6323380). Payoff: the therapy succeeds after purification, real human consequences. Objection: a close thematic neighbor of #450; a familiar miracle framing can hide collaborative work and early limitations. Not rejected by owner, eligible future selection.

**John Snow.** Opening proposal: “Why did one London pump become a suspect in a deadly outbreak?” Snow tests water hypothesis with addresses and exceptions; pump handle removed; comparison across suppliers strengthens the inference. [Primary text](https://epi-snow.ph.ucla.edu/Stream2_BSPoutbreak_a.html), [review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7150208/). Payoff is the inference, not magical cessation. Objection: common viral framing that handle stopped epidemic is misleading; already subsiding. The best alternative narrative needs more context.

Linear B wins on the distinctive observable test (prediction on unseen record) and topical diversity, not simply ease of drawing. Insulin has stronger immediate human stakes. John Snow has comparable inference but less clean causal finale. Experimental evergreen hypothesis only.

## Promise and chronology

Question: how can a proposed reading of an unknown language be checked? Opening physical tablet; new constraints (phonetic signs, Kober endings, Bennett sign identities) → testable grid → place names → surprising Greek → Chadwick collaboration → Blegen applies prior readings to newly cleaned tablet → pictorial and quantity checks → historical payoff. All versions answer the question without requiring another episode.

Long opening questions answered: unknown language by relational analysis; change in theory by recurring Greek readings; pots by independent records. In 10m, corpus access and handwritten model add context to what counts as a test. No current claim of complete understanding of Linear A.
