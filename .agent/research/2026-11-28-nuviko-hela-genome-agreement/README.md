# Nuviko History — Henrietta Lacks: The Genome They Took Offline

In 1951, a tumor sample was used without Henrietta Lacks's consent. In 2013, publishing the DNA sequence of the resulting HeLa cells raised privacy concerns for her descendants. Two research releases were interrupted, and a controlled-access agreement gave the family two seats in a six-member review group.
