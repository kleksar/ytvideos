# Real-world visual references

Applies to recognizable real-world subjects in historical and contemporary imagery across channels. The channel profile owns the rendering medium; this procedure owns visual identity and construction references. It does not reopen fact-checking or rewriting an accepted narration.

## Select and inspect sources before generation

For a recognizable real person, organization or product name/logo, interface, building, vehicle, machine, artifact or historical image that matters to a shot, establish the intended identity, place, date and relevant version before prompting. Search for actual visual sources rather than asking the generator to recall the appearance. Wikipedia/Wikimedia may be useful discovery points; prefer available original company, product, museum, archive, institutional, manufacturer or mission material and inspect the source page and actual image. Search snippets and thumbnails alone do not establish a reference. No fixed source count: obtain the views needed for the scene's important features.

Record the source page, image URL, credited author/institution, depicted identity/date/version, local file/hash, reference role, rights/conditions and limitations in the existing episode reference log. Credibility, permission to use and visual acceptance are separate. Do not assume an encyclopedia image is free of conditions or an institution owns all images it hosts. Supply the actual inspected files to generation/editing when they define a visible identity or construction; mentioning a URL or institution in prose is insufficient.

## Transform the staging, preserve the subject

A source photograph anchors what the subject looks like, not the scene's required camera, pose, background, lighting or crop. Generate the planned situation at the desired supported angle while retaining recognizable proportions, silhouette, structural connections and defining details. Distinguish the roles of identity, construction, layout and medium references so a rendering-style anchor does not override mechanical geometry. For a scoped edit also supply the actual edit target under the core procedure.

Changing viewpoint is not license to invent hidden machinery. Find an additional view, drawing or reliable model when the new angle reveals consequential unknown structure. If the source does not support it, choose a supported angle, hide the unknown naturally or record the reconstruction limits and resolve them before presenting the detail as established. Do not duplicate struts, invent impossible joints or replace a known object with a vaguely similar one.

For historical people distinguish a verified photograph/life portrait, later artistic likeness and generated reconstruction. With no supported likeness, choose a treatment that does not imply a known face; do not turn a generic generated face into an authenticated portrait. For names/logos verify spelling, symbol, lettering, colors and version appropriate to the depicted date. Avoid generic substitutes when the real identity is the point, and avoid decorative branding unrelated to the story. Cross-check significant generated lettering and identity marks against the actual source.

For products and interfaces, check the relevant generation and visible identity features against sources from the depicted period; distinguish a faithful reference from an illustrative reconstruction. Use a verified graphic original where appropriate and permitted when a critical mark cannot be reproduced accurately, integrated according to the channel profile. Do not invent an interface state, document or exact design and present it as an authentic record. If an essential identity cannot be established or reproduced, resolve the affected staging before acceptance; do not fabricate certainty.

## Review and provenance

Inspect the generated result against the source for identity, proportions, attachment points, mechanical support, orientation, markings and era, then against the channel medium and shot purpose. New staging must still show the same identifiable subject. A photograph-based generated image remains a reconstruction, not archival footage; a recognizable face or image on a physical carrier does not certify authenticity. Record original source, transformation and observed checks internally; mandatory credits/disclosures follow their actual conditions. Public source lists remain governed by root §11.

An unchanged source reference can support several scenes; check each new viewpoint and object state separately. Save exact prompt/reference roles and bind accepted output to its own file/hash. Do not infer visual fidelity from a successful generator call or source citation.
