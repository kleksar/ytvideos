## Visual contract and reference choice

Read and apply [shared bold visual staging standard](../../../../.agent/VISUAL_STAGING.md) before prompting or reviewing images. Carry the storyboard's first-glance focal idea and expressive device into the prompt; do not dilute them into a neutral prop illustration. Its accepted cover examples demonstrate visual force, not mandatory scene lettering or a poster layout for every shot.

When the storyboard chooses recognizable company or product details, make them part of the paper world and its action: for example, a verified product interface on a paper screen or a period-appropriate logo on a relevant object. Preserve their actual identifying features under the [visual-reference procedure](historical-visual-references.md). Select details for this story rather than repeating generic screens with new labels; do not force branding into every frame or add a decorative outer border.

The [channel contract](../../../../channels/paper_detour/AGENTS.md#изображение-и-текст) requires a complete static opaque full-frame handmade paper/card diorama source per shot. Apply the core's composition/framing procedure and the channel's authorized motion boundary. Select actual available accepted references through the [Paper scene catalog](../../../../channels/paper_detour/brand/SCENE_IMAGE_REFERENCES.md) and its availability record; the meaningful-staging pack illustrates the approved expressive direction without prescribing its ships, palette or compositions for other stories. A catalog entry does not replace the PNG: inspect and supply the relevant actual files under the core reference procedure. Obtain missing historical identity/construction anchors through [historical visual references](historical-visual-references.md), using supplied media or credible online sources; do not invent a substitute and call it verified. For a continuing episode also use its specific accepted anchor and current visual bible. New reuse takes the catalog's shared source; preserve imported paths.

Create full-bleed scene images without a decorative outer video border. This does not ban a story object such as a framed photograph. YouTube thumbnail lettering follows its separate thumbnail owner.

Compare handmade paper/card fibers, cut/layered edges, folds, volume and physical light/shadows. A photographic human substituted for a paper character, flat vector or smooth digital render does not pass as the paper style. This does not forbid a photograph, painting, document or other real-world image carried by a physical object inside the diorama, as specified below. Match real files, not a verbal approximation. A particular hero, street, palette or glasslike droplet remains episode-specific. Promote an accepted image into the channel catalog only when the user's instruction covers that promotion; record provenance and scope.

Build a rich, tactile miniature world: formed paper, card thickness, precise cut construction, folds, joints, convincing small props and varied surfaces under coherent light. Use fibers and torn edges where the object calls for them; do not turn every material into the same rough strips, every hand into a mitten or every face into a featureless silhouette. Preserve focal hierarchy and intentional depth of field. Richness is judged in the actual scene, not by a resolution label or the number of objects. [Paper staging](../../ytv-visual-direction/references/paper-storyboarding.md#предметная-постановка-и-сценовый-текст) owns the choice of details and explanatory devices; the core image review owns acceptance.

Maps, scales, cutaways, comparison models and readable lettering belong to this material world too: supported card, layered relief, printed or cut-paper elements with thickness, perspective and contact shadows. Render the assigned scene text into the complete opaque source image; it is distinct from the later narration-subtitle layer. A physical typographic composition can be the focus of a shot when assigned by the storyboard. The scene source remains one finished static diorama.

### Real-world images inside the paper world

When an actual person's appearance, artwork, document or object matters to the story, it may remain recognizable as an image on a physical carrier inside the complete paper/card composition: a print, page, framed picture, display panel or stone-like layered paper slab. Preserve the carrier's thickness, edges, support, perspective, contact shadows and common lighting. Choose the carrier for the story, rather than putting every source on the same decorative rock. The image is part of the scene's material world, with a clear boundary from surrounding paper characters and environment.

The image within that carrier may be fully realistic; the surrounding diorama remains handmade paper/card. Choose the carrier and image treatment for the depicted time, place, technology and narrative function: a television is appropriate only where that technology belongs, while a portrait, manuscript image, relief or other supported device may serve another era. These are examples, not a template list or a requirement to insert a carrier into every scene. An image on a stone-like object is still a depiction, not a person physically present. Do not fabricate an ancient photographic record or imply that a newly generated portrait is a surviving historical artifact. A realistic image also may guide a paper reconstruction directly when the purpose is to show the actual object/person acting in the diorama; preserve identifying features while translating materials.

Use the actual authorized source when identity or documentary detail matters; record provenance, rights and any alteration. A generated likeness or recreated painting does not become an authentic photo, original artwork or archival evidence because it looks realistic. Do not replace a needed historical source with an invented image while implying authenticity. Approved episode-specific visual references are supplied with the episode bundle and do not certify provenance beyond their documented sources.

## Canvas and text

Before scene work load the actual output geometry from the [shared 2K MP4 delivery profile](../../ytv-video-export/references/delivery.md). It defines the final video canvas and frame rate for Shorts and long films. An explicit accepted aspect overrides the default. Record actual native source size, then fit proportionally without distortion; a smaller generated source does not become native delivery resolution through scaling. Design the camera/zoom crop envelope for the target canvas before generation. The complete PNG is opaque and full-bleed, with no empty bars or transparent fields. Verify the visible image reaches all four edges; matching pixel dimensions alone does not prove full-bleed framing. If needed, use proportional fill with minimal crop or repair the composition; do not stretch the image or add an artificial background to conceal bars.

### Generation quality and requested resolution

For every scene generation/edit request the maximum available image quality, native resolution and fine detail, preserving the accepted composition and handmade paper style. Use 4K UHD as the requested source PNG size for the accepted orientation.

| Scene format | Requested generation target |
|---|---|
| Landscape, 16:9 | 3840×2160 px |
| Portrait, 9:16 | 2160×3840 px |

For another explicitly accepted aspect, request the highest available native resolution at that aspect; do not force it into one of these canvases. Emphasize visible paper fibers, layered cut edges, folds, physical light and shadows, and clear meaningful details such as faces, hands and action objects. A resolution label alone does not establish visual quality. Preserve intentional focus/depth and avoid adding sharpening or effects merely to suggest detail.

Reusable prompt wording, followed by the specific scene and its constraints:

> Maximum available image quality, native resolution and fine detail. Target 4K UHD: [3840×2160, landscape 16:9 / 2160×3840, portrait 9:16 — select the accepted format]. Preserve the accepted handmade paper/card diorama style, visible fibers, layered cut edges, folds, physical lighting and clear focal details.

The [core procedure](../SKILL.md#produce-only-the-needed-change) records the requested target, actual controls and measured native dimensions separately. If the tool returns a smaller PNG, judge its suitability at the planned delivery crop and zoom before deciding whether to regenerate. Use the applicable image-generation skill for tool and mode choice.

### Scene text and storage

For both Shorts and long films, consume the storyboard's [text-area plan](../../ytv-visual-direction/references/paper-storyboarding.md#безопасные-области-текста) before generation and preserve only the crop/camera envelope and other explicit constraints it assigns. Subtitle geometry belongs to the separate subtitle layer; it does not reserve a lower portion of the source still or limit where scene subjects may appear. Compose the complete image naturally across the full frame; do not infer an empty lower band, move objects away from the bottom, or reduce detail based on a universal percentage. A written prompt does not prove compliance. If an actual later subtitle composite creates a specific conflict, route that exact frame for correction and inspect the relevant composite.

[Subtitles](../../ytv-paper-subtitles/SKILL.md#общая-геометрия-и-защита-композиции) owns narration-line geometry. Build the image from the assigned visual idea, which may come from any part of the accepted story under [Paper storyboard](../../ytv-visual-direction/references/paper-storyboarding.md). A replacement or changed framing invalidates affected subtitle-fit checks until rechecked.

If a scene becomes a Shorts cover, invoke [thumbnails](../../ytv-youtube-thumbnails/SKILL.md) with its [Paper safe-area procedure](../../ytv-youtube-thumbnails/references/paper-detour.md#shorts-cover-format-and-safe-area). Check master, specified crop and actual target surface separately. An observed card size is not a platform specification. Text below the card is a title field handled by [titles](../../ytv-youtube-video-titles/SKILL.md), not by repainting the scene.

Save scene versions in the episode's `Source/Images/`, with prompts/references and existing generation journal. Do not move imported source paths. [Storyboard](../../ytv-visual-direction/references/paper-storyboarding.md) owns compositions/sequence; [production flow](../../ytv-video-production/SKILL.md) owns FFmpeg montage.

## Paper-specific acceptance

Check the actual reduced image against the assigned focal idea under the [staging acceptance criteria](../../../../.agent/VISUAL_STAGING.md#применение-и-проверка). Material fidelity alone does not accept an inert image. Record whether the chosen scale, gesture, contrast or metaphor actually reads; return a weak idea to storyboard rather than merely increasing texture or resolution.

Apply [Paper's scene-text standard](../../ytv-visual-direction/references/paper-storyboarding.md#предметная-постановка-и-сценовый-текст) in the actual composition. Check the wording the story relies on. Keep a strong scene with minor incidental lettering errors or intentionally sparse space when the visual idea and material facts still read. An image from another beat is assessed in the sequence by what it contributes to the story.

Apply the [core's actual-file checks](../SKILL.md#review-the-exact-files) to every final PNG before import, comparing it with the exact approved reference. Inspect paper material/edges, volume and physical lighting both in the full source and its framed phone-size reduction. Photographic humans replacing paper characters, flat vector art or smooth digital renders fail this profile even when the pixel dimensions match the request; an image on a physical carrier follows the integration contract above.

Apply the [scene-image uniqueness rule](../../ytv-visual-direction/references/paper-storyboarding.md#уникальность-сценовых-изображений) to the full selection before montage, including nonadjacent shots. Compare source paths and hashes for exact reuse and inspect the images in edit order for visually repeated staging, including crops or zooms saved as separate files. Record affected scene IDs and the approved loop exception, if present, in the existing image log. Return any other repeat for correction before accepting the sequence.

For water or another semitransparent overlap, trace every person/animal and crossing object from visible into obscured sections: torso, pelvis, limbs, scale, pose and support remain connected. If background is visible through water, the body must occlude it; tint, refraction and turbidity can lower contrast but cannot erase the figure. Check proportions above/below the surface and one coherent perspective/optical treatment for body, surroundings and background. Do not excuse an undersized submerged body with selective refraction. Distinguish real occlusion by an object/frame edge from generative disappearance. Inspect full source and final framing, not just a contact sheet. If split-level geometry cannot be made coherent, rebuild the complete frame with camera above opaque water that consistently hides body and background while retaining accepted meaning; a substantive story change returns to storyboard.

Record actual `ACCEPTED/REJECTED` decisions, their authors, inspected details, exact reference and limitations. A passed scene set does not accept future subtitle timing, final export, audio or publication. The [Paper entry](../../ytv-paper-images/SKILL.md) routes to this core/profile without a second procedure.
