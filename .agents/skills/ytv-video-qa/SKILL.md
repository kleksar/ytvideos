# Video and audio QA

Read current project instructions, episode state and the applicable channel profile. In ytvideos start with [bootstrap](../../../.agent/CHANNEL_BOOTSTRAP.md); for Paper Detour read the [Paper profile](references/paper-detour.md) before freezing required checks or recording readiness. Its applicable checks are mandatory even when absent from a helper's defaults. The reusable procedure does not prescribe aspect, FPS, pacing, LUFS, language, player playback or a sidecar for every channel.

## Establish the target and freshness

For Paper and Nuviko follow the [shared profile](references/export-review.md): immediately deliver a completed review MP4 without extra post-render checks unless requested. Bind acceptance to that file/hash. The additional frame-sampling audit below applies to a separately requested audit or demonstrated defect. Keep technical checks, user acceptance and actual listening/independent coverage separate.

Identify actual media path/hash, accepted script and spoken recording, saved project/timeline revision, scene intervals, motion plan, cue sheet, mix and expected export parameters. Reuse existing handoffs; do not create a parallel episode registry. Partial audits retain their stated scope.

Consume the frozen pre-render contract from [ytv-video-export](../ytv-video-export/SKILL.md), including the audio owner's block. Compare actual values to that contract; do not generate a different preset after seeing the output. For historical audits without a passport, record the missing provenance and established applicable requirements. The [contract handoff](../ytv-video-export/references/export-contract.md#existing-qa-helper) lists checks not automated by the current helper; inspect them explicitly instead of assuming all ffprobe fields were compared.

Compare export provenance with the current accepted edit and dependencies. Matching a historical SHA, a finished render or an unchanged filename cannot prove freshness. A later relevant edit makes the export `STALE`; unresolved source/target makes freshness `UNVERIFIED`. Preserve previous acceptance with its author and object. An accepted preview is not approval of a later encode/effect.

## Run and interpret checks

- **Streams and decode:** measure container and each stream, counted frames, timing, resolution/aspect, FPS, colour, codec/profile and audio channels/rate/bitrate against the accepted contract. Decode all video/audio streams completely with errors surfaced. Distinguish encoder target from measured VBR. Check unexpected gaps, black frames and truncated endings against the story; intentional darkness/silence is not automatically a defect. The [offline helper](references/helper-contract.md) performs technical checks and exact frame extraction without changing media.
- **Image and edit:** inspect final-file frames at every scene's beginning/middle/end, both sides of cuts, cue entry/hold/exit and identified risk positions. Check full frame and phone reduction, clue visibility, text, continuity, anatomy, physical support and unintended crop/borders. Record actual inspected indices, sizes and scene/cue coverage. Contact sheets are samples; extraction is not inspection and samples do not prove every intervening frame.
- **Motion:** use [motion checks](references/motion-checks.md) when applicable. For every scene verify requested camera coverage, actual screen direction and combined scene/camera/depth/overlay framing. Review intermediate extrema and uncertain intervals. Static acceptance, parameter signs and a valid render do not prove containment or visible direction.
- **Subtitles and sync:** consume the accepted subtitle cue sheet and retained recording/alignment. Check first spoken word, continuous group hold, last word and adjacent cut against final frames, including optical bounds and every actual scene×cue pair. Distinguish word speech timing from subtitle-group hold. Subtitle authoring/fit stays with its owner; a separate SRT follows [ytv-srt](../ytv-srt/SKILL.md). For applicable new YouTube releases with speech, a separate source-language SRT is required even if captions are burned in; `N_A` needs an evidenced reason, and missing or unverified required evidence is not a pass. Machine timing does not prove audible sync or comfortable reading.
  For word-highlighted subtitles, verify the implemented word timings against the retained map and speech, then inspect states before/after word changes, group changes and pauses under the [subtitle owner's procedure](../ytv-paper-subtitles/SKILL.md#пословная-подсветка-в-монтаже) and channel treatment. Record `word_highlighting` (colour-state behaviour) separately from `cue_timing` (speech sync), with actual coverage; readability is a separate check. In full production with word highlighting these are required caption checks; a scoped technical-only audit keeps its narrower verdict. Unresolved required coverage remains `UNVERIFIED`.
- **Audio:** verify the entire accepted mix: speech, music and effects with actual trims, speed, gaps, fades, balance and channel mapping. For intended stream copy compare extracted stream hashes; after lossy re-encoding use timing/signal correspondence with stated tolerance rather than requiring identical bytes. Measure supported sample/true peaks, levels, clipping candidates, unexpected silence, starts/joins/end and planned tails. Compare suspected gaps with source/edit. No universal loudness threshold replaces accepted balance. ASR, waveform and player volume do not prove pronunciation, naturalness, audibility or heard sync. Listen to existing audio when the available modality supports it, recording actual coverage; otherwise those checks remain `UNVERIFIED`.

## Record defects and recheck

Keep one episode receipt with file/hash, edit/dependency versions, methods, evidence paths, inspected indices/ranges, author and limitations. Separate `stream_metadata`, `full_decode`, `frame_sampling`, `motion_framing`, `cue_timing`, `audio_signal`, `subjective_audio_listening`, `srt` and `independent_review`; the profile may add checks.

Checks use `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNVERIFIED` or `N_A` with reason/coverage. Freshness uses `CURRENT`, `STALE`, `UNVERIFIED`. Applicability may use `NOT_REQUESTED`/`NOT_REQUIRED` with its basis. Final verdict: `FAIL` for a blocking defect/stale delivery; `UNVERIFIED` for unresolved required coverage; `PASS` only when the profile's required checks are evidenced for this file. Label a technical PASS as technical. Non-required but unverified subjective/independent checks stay visible, without silently becoming PASS or new blockers.

Each defect needs frame/time, observed symptom, severity, owner and affected dependencies. Recheck corrections and relevant neighboring scenes/cues. Every new final file needs fresh identity/stream/decode checks; changed content invalidates dependent reviews. Keep useful unchanged-source checks and prior provenance. Self-review remains self-review; do not invent independent review or mandate subagents.

## Boundaries

QA owns final verification, coverage and defect/recheck records. [Images](../ytv-scene-images/SKILL.md#motion-safe-composition) owns early motion-safe composition; [image-motion](../ytv-image-motion/SKILL.md) owns presets/render/cache and structural preservation; [audio delivery](../ytv-audio-delivery/SKILL.md) owns sound permissions, creation/mix and audio delivery settings. [Video export](../ytv-video-export/SKILL.md) owns the complete pre-render contract and engine mapping. A separate audit is performed when requested or needed to investigate a reported defect. QA grants no new sound, timeline write, export replacement, upload or publication authority. Route repairs to their owner within existing authorization.

После проверки передать точные file/hash/edit, coverage и ограничения в [ytv-media-lifecycle](../ytv-media-lifecycle/SKILL.md) для обновления назначения выдач и плана очистки. Для stream identity сохранять hashes/команды; объёмные извлечённые H.264/PCM не нужны как постоянный QA output, если отдельная диагностическая задача их не требует. Назначение/очистку точных disposable файлов ведёт storage owner в действующем разрешении.


Новые прогоны запускать в managed-режиме `--episode --run-id --owner --output-dir Edit/QA/<run>`; см. [контракт](references/helper-contract.md). Helper создаёт отдельный QA-run, пакетно учитывает кадры в Work/scratch и завершает синхронный writer. После фактического профильного PASS автоматически передать exact review в `workspace finalize-qa --execute` по [storage contract](../ytv-media-lifecycle/references/qa-finalization.md) в действующем разрешении. Выбранные проверочные кадры/контакт-листы и точный frame map сохраняются как evidence; до завершения review выборки удерживаются. При новой версии — новый run, свежие identity/streams/decode и зависимые rechecks; прежний PASS не наследуется.

В отдельно порученном final-file аудите Nuviko включать в required checks `scene_duration`, `semantic_grouping` и `word_highlighting` по [pipeline](../../../channels/nuviko_history/docs/HISTORY_STORY_PIPELINE.md#длительность-изображений) и [subtitle standard](../../../channels/nuviko_history/brand/VISUAL_PROFILE.md#субтитры-речи--обязательный-стандарт). Проверка одной строки и закрытие gaps не подтверждают эти проверки. Подтверждённый пользователем дефект оформлять новым superseding receipt; исторический PASS сохранять как запись, но исключить ошибочную версию из готовых к выпуску.
