# YouTube thumbnails

Own the generated image, mandatory lettering and their promise together with the current title. Read the active project's instructions and relevant channel profile; [Paper Detour](references/paper-detour.md) supplies its visual standards. Video-title writing, scene illustration, upload mechanics and experiment management keep their own owners. A thumbnail is a separate artifact; accepting a scene does not accept a finished cover.

During new story development, supply a provisional visual concept under [storycraft’s opening procedure](../ytv-storycraft/references/appeal-and-opening.md). A text sketch tests the idea, not actual thumbnail legibility. Carry its viewer promise into final packaging and verify what the film’s opening delivers. Preserve accepted scripts.

## Establish the target

Identify channel/video/language, accepted film version and factual limits, current title, intended surfaces and formats, real accepted visual references, existing masters and uploaded cover, requested variants and scope. Read fresh external state for an existing release, including active experiments. Do not infer the displayed Shorts cover from a landscape videos.list thumbnail URL; inspect the actual target card. Distinguish missing source, uninspected file and a demonstrated defect.

For a catalog enumerate every scoped video and exclusions. Record `keep`, `revise`, `defer` or `needs_asset` with reason and a separate artifact/verification state. Keep useful work; neither a new title nor a style audit automatically calls for regeneration. Develop the thumbnail directly from accepted material; consult an external session when the user explicitly requests it.

## Develop the visual promise

For every ytvideos channel, apply the [shared bold visual staging standard](../../../.agent/VISUAL_STAGING.md) to concept development, prompting and actual reduced-size review. Choose a forceful focal idea and expressive device from the first proposal; preserve the channel's own visual medium. A neutral prop illustration fails the intended visual promise even when its material or photorealism is correct.

Before writing the image prompt, use the [standalone visual promise procedure](references/standalone-promise.md). It owns the stranger's reading, image–lettering relationship and semantic rejection criteria. Apply it again to the actual reduced file; a strong written concept or readable glyphs do not prove that the generated cover communicates it. Then restore the title and verify what it adds and where the film pays off the promise.

For weak feed appeal or competitor research, use the packaging owner’s [feed comparison](../ytv-video-packaging/references/feed-research.md).

For a new cover or substantive visual revision, use a focused vidIQ generation or refinement pass under the [shared packaging procedure](../ytv-video-packaging/SKILL.md#vidiq) after the concept check. Prefer MCP `vidiq_generate_thumbnail` for a fresh concept, with the exact lettering, composition, channel medium, factual limits and explicit orientation. Supply the accepted brief and permitted references when supported. Passing an existing video ID may import its current thumbnail, title and description. Supply explicit grounding when those inputs would pull the result away from the concept. Poll the returned job ID and inspect the actual image. Studio Suggestions or Optimize can supply candidates when MCP is unavailable and the target is supported there. Continue with the authorized image generator when vidIQ cannot use essential references, meet the visual brief or produce the required format.

In addition to visual appeal, inspect whether the generated props, mechanism, setting and screen imagery depict a supported event or result. Use scorer feedback only to identify a defect you can see in the file. Compare the cover by itself and with its assigned title. If using vidIQ Preview for feed context, label it a simulation and choose relevant real neighbors separately when the suggested feed is off-topic.

For long-film variants, read the concept cards and complete the A/B owner's [pre-generation distinction check](../ytv-youtube-ab-testing/SKILL.md#обязательная-проверка-различимости) before generating images. Use the assigned situation and viewer promise in each prompt; return final files for that owner's second comparison. Choose one story moment, conflict, consequence or clue that the accepted film actually develops. Make the visual idea and its lettering bold and intriguing: show the strongest supportable stakes, surprise or tension through a clear action, contrast or question that gives the viewer a reason to click. Develop a specific, punchy phrase that adds to the image and title; avoid generic hype or a phrase whose implied claim exceeds the film. Match the intensity and visual language to the channel profile. State the subject, observable action or meaningful relation, story-bearing prop, direction of attention and space for lettering. Preserve qualifiers that change the claim: legend, reported estimate, attributed result, adapted personal account. Neither a question mark nor a caveat hidden in the description excuses a misleading image.

Check title and image together: each contributes useful information, without contradiction or needless repetition. Every new ytvideos thumbnail includes lettering as part of the generated image. Before generation fix the exact English hook, its spelling and punctuation, hierarchy, placement, contrast and space around it. Include that literal string and its visual treatment in the generation prompt. A picture without lettering is an unfinished thumbnail. Inspect the actual generated glyphs; regenerate or edit the image through the authorized generator until the exact phrase is correct and readable. Do not deliver a text-free scene as the finished cover or treat a planned later text layer as completion. Apply the channel's own material, typography, composition and reference requirements. Do not impose another channel's palette, paper style or word count. Preserve rights/provenance and character continuity when relevant. Use the available authorized image-generation/editing workflow with the actual references. For a long-film release of any ytvideos channel follow the [standing A/B package](../ytv-youtube-ab-testing/SKILL.md#стандартный-пакет-длинного-фильма): prepare three meaningfully distinct cover concepts and files in parallel with the final render, using the frozen edit revision and the early story brief when available. Present the complete package under the production flow; no separate packaging approval gate is added. Upload authority remains with that flow. The user independently selects the main title and main cover, including from different concepts; check the resulting combination and retain the agreed test alternatives. Shorts use the usual package. For those long-film variants, vary the story situation and visual hierarchy, not merely a crop or color. Each must independently communicate a concrete, truthful conflict or question at phone size. A scene still is not automatically a finished cover. Reject anatomy defects, reference-pose copying and an image whose mood or subject fails to convey the film's stakes.

For a requested broad exploration, write a numbered concept index before generation. Each entry names the viewer's unanswered question, observable action or contrast, principal prop, composition and exact mandatory lettering. Spread the search across different story situations and visual hierarchies; a changed crop, palette or synonym is a refinement of its parent concept. Inspect early results at the intended preview size and use observed failures to revise the remaining prompts, especially when a reference keeps importing the same palette, cast or era. Produce the requested count with the authorized generator, retain exact files/prompts and record incomplete or failed generations honestly. Compare numbered contact sheets at useful reduced size, then inspect shortlisted masters individually. Separate the editor's shortlist from user acceptance and performance evidence. Paper's criteria for curiosity and material quality live in its visual playbook.

## Verify the actual files and surface

Inspect the real master and upload copy: meaning, spelling, subject count, faces/hands, era, object support, edges, contrast and reference fidelity. Check a reduced preview at the profile's useful size, then the actual destination card/preview where accessible. Preserve the subject, clue and entire lettering, including outlines/shadows, through the observed crop and overlays. If a crop fails, correct the composition and repeat every affected preview. Record each surface and measured dimensions; verify that a requested viewport override actually took effect. A bounded phone-width layout is a local simulation, a desktop card is not a mobile-device check, and a local crop is not proof of YouTube rendering.

Use the local [preview helper](scripts/thumbnail_previews.py) (Python + Pillow) to save reproducible reduced/cropped files and a source-hash manifest:

```sh
python3 .agents/skills/ytv-youtube-thumbnails/scripts/thumbnail_previews.py MASTER --out QA_DIR --widths 360 180 --crops 2:3 --regions REGIONS_JSON
```

Choose widths/crops from the channel profile and observed destination, rather than copying these example values into every format. Optional regions JSON is `{"regions":[{"label":"lettering including shadow","box":[0.1,0.3,0.9,0.7]}]}` with normalized source bounds. Identify and record those bounds manually; containment checks neither detect text/faces nor prove readability. Without regions the containment result is unassessed. Visually inspect the generated files and save the actual observation, including any missing device/live-surface check. Record native master resolution separately from an upscaled upload derivative.

Before upload verify current official [format and upload guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en) and the target account/interface. Limits depend on format and upload surface; do not carry a historical size limit into another adapter. A portrait upload can have a landscape API derivative. A matching URL or successful upload is not visual acceptance. For a Short, inspect the actual selected cover on the requested Shorts surface after a fresh load; standard thumbnail acceptance may differ from a separate Shorts selection. Hand a demonstrated capability gap to the release/uploader owner, rather than repeat an already accepted write because of an old cached preview. If the destination already shows the approved image, no extra Studio write is needed.

## Return and hand off

Use the [experiment owner](../ytv-youtube-ab-testing/SKILL.md) for A/B lifecycle and decisions, and [analytics](../ytv-youtube-analytics/SKILL.md) for source/period-aware performance evidence; this skill supplies verified cover variants.

Save the selected concept, exact cover text, associated title/version, film version, reference/source paths, prompt/edits, master and upload copy, actual dimensions/bytes, and full/reduced/crop/overlay/live-card evidence with remaining checks. Separate editorial decision, user acceptance, artifact readiness and external state (`not_requested`, `proposal_only`, `deferred`, `applied_verified`). A catalog has one decision per included video and a concrete change brief for each revision.

Store these beside the episode; audit indexes may link them. Recheck the pair when the title or film materially changes. Qualified performance expectations remain hypotheses until data support them. Active experiments are coordinated with their owner; do not overwrite variants or choose a winner from impressions of style. Authorized external changes use the project's release/uploader and verified saved state, preserving other metadata. Preparing covers does not grant a publication instruction.
