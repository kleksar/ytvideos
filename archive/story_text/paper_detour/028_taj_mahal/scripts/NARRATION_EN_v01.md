Of course. I'm good at it.

That answer cost Ahmad his hands.

The king had put Ahmad in charge of building the Taj Mahal. For twenty-two years, Ahmad planned every detail.

When it was finished, the king praised his magnificent work. But he didn't want anyone else to build anything comparable.

That same day, Ahmad and his assistants all lost their hands.

He made sure Ahmad could never work again.

So next time you're tempted to be too good at your job, remember Ahmad.

The king asked:

“Ahmad, will you keep working as an architect?”
