Time stops. A raindrop hangs inches from your face. You have five seconds. What do you do? At first, it sounds like the perfect power. Everything around you freezes—even light. No light reaches your eyes. The world goes black. No sound reaches you, either. Sound needs vibrations moving through air. But the air has frozen too. You can’t push through it. You can’t draw it into your lungs. You can’t even move the drop in front of your face. Like an insect caught in amber, you’re stuck. Five seconds of darkness, silence, and no way out. Then time starts again. The drop falls. So, what did you do with your five seconds? Nothing. You just became five seconds older.
