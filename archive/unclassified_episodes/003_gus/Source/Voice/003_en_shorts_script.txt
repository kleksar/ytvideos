I didn't tell my girlfriend that her pet had died. She only found out when she got back from her trip.
His name was Gus. He was a pet snake she'd had since she was eight. Yeah, a snake. Maybe not everyone's idea of a pet, but to her, he was family.
Gus was getting old. We knew he didn't have much time left. But when he finally died, I still broke down crying.
And my girlfriend was in another country, visiting her brother for his birthday.
Things with her family had been complicated for a long time. They'd only just started getting better. She really wanted to reconnect with them, and this trip meant a lot to her.
So now I had to make a decision.
Call her and tell her Gus was gone? I knew she'd be devastated, and it could ruin her whole trip.
Or let her spend a few days with her family, then tell her face to face?
I went with the second option.
When she flew home, I picked her up at the airport and told her everything. Gus had died, and I'd deliberately kept it from her while she was away.
She didn't say a word the entire drive home.
At home, we put Gus in a box we'd kept for this moment and buried him together.
Then she finally said:
"I still love you. But I don't like you right now."
I understood why she was angry, so I didn't argue.
Later, I made dinner, and we both ended up crying at the table.
Before bed, I asked if I should sleep on the couch.
She said I'd be a dead man if I slept anywhere other than our bed.
No, we didn't break up.
We're still together. And we both still miss Gus.
I don't regret waiting until she came home. But I understand why she was upset with me.
