In March 1944, dozens of Allied airmen escaped a German prisoner-of-war camp through a tunnel. Only three reached safety. Then urns began arriving back at the camp.

Follow the story of the Great Escape beyond the tunnel: the murder of fifty recaptured airmen, the false reports sent to Berlin, and the investigation that brought men involved before a British military court.
