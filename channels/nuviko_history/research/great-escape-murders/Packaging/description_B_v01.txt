After the Great Escape, police offices across Germany reported that recaptured Allied airmen had been shot while resisting or trying to escape. Their accounts matched so closely that Berlin ordered them rewritten.

This is the story of the fifty murdered prisoners, the official explanation used to conceal their deaths, and the postwar investigation into those who carried out the killings.
