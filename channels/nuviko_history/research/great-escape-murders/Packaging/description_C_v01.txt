Two drivers took part in a journey that ended with four recaptured airmen murdered. After the war, both argued that they had only driven the cars. A British military court had to decide what that role meant.

From the Great Escape at Stalag Luft III to rewritten police reports and the 1947 Hamburg trial, this film follows the killings and the search for accountability.
