## Proposed description

How could a clock help a ship find its position in the middle of the ocean? In the 1700s, sailors could compare local noon with the time back home to work out how far east or west they had travelled — but only if their clock stayed accurate at sea.

English clockmaker John Harrison spent years tackling that problem. In 1761, his son took Harrison's H4 timekeeper on a voyage to Jamaica, putting the watch to the test.
