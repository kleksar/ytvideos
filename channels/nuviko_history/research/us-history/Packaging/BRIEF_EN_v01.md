**C is the lead concept.** It matches the whole film, from the Louisiana Purchase and the political struggle over each new state to secession and war. A foregrounds the negotiating surprise. B foregrounds the mechanism that postponed a political break.

**Draft opening for C**

In 1803, American negotiators went to France to buy New Orleans. They came home with a deal for Louisiana. As the United States expanded, the fight over slavery moved west with it. Every new state could tip the balance of power in Congress. Follow that fight from the Mississippi to Fort Sumter and the Civil War.

The three thumbnail situations are distinct. A shows the offer at a negotiating table. B shows Congress drawing a boundary. C shows the outbreak of war. Keep Indigenous sovereignty and the limited meaning of transferred French claims in the description when developing A. Keep slavery central to the explanation for B and C.
