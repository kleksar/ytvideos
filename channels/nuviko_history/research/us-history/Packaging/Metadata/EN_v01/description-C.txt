In April 1861, Confederate guns opened fire on Fort Sumter. The United States had spent decades expanding west. Now it was fighting over whether it would remain one country.

This film follows how the Louisiana Purchase, the forced removal of the Cherokee, and the war with Mexico reshaped the country. As new states joined, the fight over slavery and power in Congress grew harder to contain. From the Mississippi to Charleston Harbor, follow that conflict through secession, the Civil War, and the abolition of slavery.
