In 1803, American negotiators wanted New Orleans, a port vital to trade on the Mississippi. France offered them the vast territory of Louisiana.

The purchase transformed the scale of the United States. As the country expanded, conflict over slavery, political power and Indigenous land intensified until the Union faced civil war.
