When Dutch ships reached New Zealand, people were already living there. So who gets to call a place discovered?

From the first movements of Homo sapiens beyond Africa to Pacific navigation and European voyages, this film follows how different communities came to know different parts of the world. It asks what changes when we distinguish settlement, contact and the making of maps.
