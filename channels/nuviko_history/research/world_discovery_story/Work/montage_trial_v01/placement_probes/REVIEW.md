# Static subtitle/scene probes — experimental

Six 960×540 still probes combine center-cropped full candidate scene with a caption-state PNG produced by the shared renderer (v02b), using the renderer's current canvas/geometry and first highlighted word of a nearby group. This approximates a single static position only: no actual MP4, pan/zoom, timing, colorspace conversion, small-screen playback or audio was checked. Source files and exact generation logic are in `build_probes.py`; these are not scene assets.

Author visually inspected S001, S014, S023, S024, S063, S082 probes. S001, S014 and S063 show main action above caption in this static position; does not establish historical acceptance or motion-safe placement. In S023/S024 renderer center-crop truncates the top archive title, subtitle covers lower portion of map, required ASIA/ALASKA labels absent, and 700×458 source is visibly soft; compare earlier padded mockup, which did not reflect renderer's actual fill-crop. These candidates are therefore not ready for import. S082 archive crop has extremely fine lines in the 960×540 reduction and no clear highlighted path; cannot perform the storyboard's clear route function yet. The safe technical solution must preserve geography and required meaning, not misrepresent this static test as a final frame.
