## Description

In AD 79, a rain of pumice fell on Pompeii. Some residents fled; others sheltered indoors as the stones piled up. By the following morning, a new danger was moving toward the city.

Follow the eruption of Mount Vesuvius through eyewitness letters and archaeological evidence: from everyday life in Pompeii to the falling stones, earthquakes and pyroclastic flows that buried it. We also visit Herculaneum, follow Pliny the Elder’s voyage across the Bay of Naples, and explore what the excavations and plaster casts can—and cannot—tell us about the people who lived there.
