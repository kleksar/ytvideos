Was Pompeii destroyed by a flood of lava? The film traces a different sequence: a towering eruption column sent pumice toward the city, earthquakes shook buildings, and later pyroclastic flows swept in. It follows the eruption from the first falling stones to the morning after, while distinguishing Pompeii from nearby Herculaneum and Stabiae.
