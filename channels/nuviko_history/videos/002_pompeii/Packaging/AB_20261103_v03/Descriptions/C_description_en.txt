Pompeii was a working city of bakeries, workshops, shops, gardens and homes before the eruption. Volcanic deposits left traces of that world: streets, rooms, everyday objects and the spaces later used to make plaster casts. This film moves from ordinary life to the eruption and the excavations that still reveal its people, while staying clear about what the surviving evidence cannot tell us.
