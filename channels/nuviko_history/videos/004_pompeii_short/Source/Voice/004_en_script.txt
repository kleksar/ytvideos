Pompeii wasn't destroyed by a river of lava. In 79 AD, stones began falling from the sky. At first, some people took shelter inside their homes. But as pumice piled up, roofs began to collapse. Others fled while they still could. By the next morning, a deadly cloud of hot gas and ash swept through the city. Pompeii was buried, but traces of everyday life survived beneath the debris.
