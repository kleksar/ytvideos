After the Black Death, labour shortages gave some survivors a chance to seek better wages or another employer. A 1349 ordinance and the 1351 Statute of Labourers sought to control pay and compel work under set conditions. The old order did not simply return, and survival did not mean freedom from poverty or dependence.
