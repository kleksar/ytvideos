The Black Death reached London in 1348. At East Smithfield, near the Tower of London, archaeologists found 181 coins in the grave of a woman buried during the epidemic. Why were the coins left with her? The reason is unknown. The cemetery’s carefully arranged burials show that essential work continued through the crisis.

This story follows the choices families faced, the disruption to work and trade, and a 1377 Dubrovnik rule requiring arrivals from plague-affected places to remain in isolation for 30 days.
