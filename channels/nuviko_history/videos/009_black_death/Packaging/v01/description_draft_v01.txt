At East Smithfield, near the Tower of London, archaeologists found 181 coins in the grave of a woman buried during the Black Death. Why did the money remain with her? The grave’s careful arrangement offers another detail: even during the epidemic, people continued the work of caring for and burying the dead.

This story follows the plague’s arrival in London, the choices families and communities faced, the disruption to trade and work, and later attempts to limit the spread of disease. The reason the coins stayed with the woman is unknown.

The historical scenes are visual reconstructions.
