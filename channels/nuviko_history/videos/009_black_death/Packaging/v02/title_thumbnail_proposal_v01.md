# Black Death — title and thumbnail proposal v01

Status: proposal for review. Not approved, not uploaded, and not an active YouTube experiment.

## Title options

1. **Recommended:** `The Black Death in London: 181 Coins in One Woman's Grave` (57 characters). Names the subject, place, and the distinctive discovery without suggesting the reason for the coins is known.
2. `London, 1348: The Black Death and a Grave With 181 Coins` (56 characters). Leads with place and date, then explains the historical subject.
3. `The Black Death Reached London. One Grave Held 181 Coins` (56 characters). A more narrative version that still makes the subject clear.

## Thumbnail concepts

All three generated candidates are 1672 × 941 PNG image bases without lettering. Any short thumbnail lettering should be composed as a separate design pass after the user selects a direction.

- `thumb_concept_01_coins_tower.png` — coins in a grave foreground, medieval London and the Tower behind; strongest match for the 181-coin hook.
- `thumb_concept_02_london_street.png` — medieval London street, Tower, coins in foreground; communicates the city and period at a glance, though coins appear on a counter rather than in a grave.
- `thumb_concept_03_traveler_cemetery.png` — cemetery scene with the Nuviko traveler standing in the midground; connects the channel character to the story, but the coins and burial ground carry less visual scale than concept 1.

These are illustrative historical reconstructions, not literal depictions of the exact grave or an exact count of coins.
