# The Black Death — title and thumbnail proposal v02

Status: proposal for review. Not approved, not uploaded, and not an active YouTube experiment. This version responds to feedback that the coin discovery should not define the whole film.

## Editorial read

The narration opens with 181 coins in a woman's grave and returns to the cemetery in its closing passage. That makes the discovery a frame for the story, not its main subject. Most of the film follows how plague moved through connected regions, how people handled fear and care, how outbreaks changed work and labor relations, and how port authorities developed isolation rules. A coin-led title or thumbnail would overstate the archaeological hook and understate the film's broader story. The reason the coins remained with the woman is explicitly unknown.

## Title options

1. **Recommended:** `The Black Death: How Europe Faced a Plague It Could Not Explain` (63 characters). Names the subject directly and reflects the film's focus on human responses under uncertainty.
2. `The Black Death in Medieval Europe: Fear, Survival, and Change` (62 characters). Broad, clear framing with the story's main themes.
3. `How the Black Death Changed Medieval Europe` (43 characters). Shortest and clearest, but less specific about the film's human stories.

## Thumbnail concepts

Both are 1672 × 941 PNG image bases without lettering. They emphasize people and city life rather than the coin find; short lettering can be added after a direction is selected.

- `thumb_theme_concept_01_london_care.png` — people cautiously passing food at a London doorway, with the Tower in the distance. Focuses on care and ordinary life during the outbreak.
- `thumb_theme_concept_02_city_gate.png` — a medieval port gate and a negotiation over supplies. Focuses on the tension between limiting contact and keeping a city fed.

These are illustrative historical reconstructions, not literal depictions of documented individuals or a single documented event.
