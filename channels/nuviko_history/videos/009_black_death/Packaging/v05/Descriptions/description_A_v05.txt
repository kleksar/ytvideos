The Black Death spread across connected medieval trade routes, reaching ports and cities far from its point of arrival. This film follows how the plague moved through Europe, how communities tried to limit its spread, and how trade and daily life continued under pressure.

From busy ports to Dubrovnik’s 30-day isolation rule, see how medieval towns tried to manage danger while depending on the movement of people and goods.
