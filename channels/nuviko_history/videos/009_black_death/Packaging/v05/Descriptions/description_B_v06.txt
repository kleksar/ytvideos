When the Black Death reached medieval communities, people faced illness without knowing its cause. Families had to decide whether to stay close and care for the sick or keep their distance. This film follows those human choices alongside the efforts to keep ordinary life going as the epidemic moved across Europe.

From households and crowded burial grounds to the people who maintained the cemeteries, the story looks at care, fear and the work that continued.
