The Black Death disrupted fields, workshops and trade, changing the choices available to workers. As labor became scarce, workers could bargain for higher pay while rulers and councils tried to restrain wages. This film follows the shortages, disputes and local rules that reshaped work across medieval Europe.

It also traces how the epidemic spread, how communities responded, and how isolation became one tool used by a major trading port.
