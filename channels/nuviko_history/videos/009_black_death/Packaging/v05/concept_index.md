## Pairwise distinction check (concept stage)

- A↔B titles: A promises the spread route; B promises the human response. Covers: A shows a cargo ship entering a port; B shows care at a household threshold.
- A↔C titles: A promises geographic spread; C promises changes to work and wages. Covers: A is a maritime arrival; C is a wage negotiation in a field/workplace.
- B↔C titles: B promises household care; C promises labor bargaining and policy. Covers: B is an interior caregiving moment; C is an outdoor negotiation with an official.

The title and thumbnail text complement each pair. The film supports the historical claims; title variants are proposals, not performance-tested winners.
