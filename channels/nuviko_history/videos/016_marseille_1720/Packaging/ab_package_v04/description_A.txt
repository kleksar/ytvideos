In 1720, the Grand Saint-Antoine reached Marseille with valuable cargo after deaths had occurred aboard. Port officials faced quarantine decisions as trade and the danger of plague came close to the city. This film follows the ship’s return, the decisions at the harbor, and the epidemic that followed.
