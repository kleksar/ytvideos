After a voyage through the eastern Mediterranean, the Grand Saint-Antoine reached Marseille with valuable cargo and deaths reported aboard. This film traces the ship’s arrival, the port’s quarantine decisions, the epidemic that spread through the city, and the vessel’s fate near Jarre.
