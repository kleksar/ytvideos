The Grand Saint-Antoine carried a fortune in silk and cotton—and a growing list of deaths aboard. When the ship reached Marseille, people and valuable goods were sent to a quarantine station on the mainland. Why was this decision made despite the warnings?

Follow the voyage, the pressure of trade and the quarantine choices that preceded the epidemic. The exact route of transmission remains uncertain, but the sequence of warnings, delays and decisions reveals how much was at stake.
