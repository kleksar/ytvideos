What happens when plague cuts off a great trading city? In Marseille, hospitals filled, markets fell silent and the streets became part of an overwhelming emergency. Some residents tried to escape; others remained amid illness, fear and restrictions.

Enter Marseille during the epidemic of 1720 and follow the crisis from the first suspicious deaths to the collapse of ordinary services. This is the story of a city whose quarantine system failed to keep the danger outside—and the people who struggled through the consequences.
