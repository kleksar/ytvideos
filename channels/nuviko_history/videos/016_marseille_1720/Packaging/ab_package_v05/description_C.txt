The ship was burned. Its captain spent nearly three years in custody. Then the case against him was dismissed. Why did the man who brought the Grand Saint-Antoine home walk free?

Trace Jean-Baptiste Chataud’s voyage, the deaths aboard, the decisions at Marseille’s quarantine station and the catastrophe that followed. From imprisonment at the Château d’If to his release in 1723, his fate raises a question that reaches beyond one captain: how should responsibility be understood when warnings pass through an entire system?
