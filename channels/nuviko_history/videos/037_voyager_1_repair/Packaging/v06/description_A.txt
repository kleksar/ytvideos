Voyager 1 was still reaching Earth by radio, but the information coming back could no longer be read. The spacecraft was about 24 billion kilometers away, so every repair attempt meant waiting almost two days for a reply. Engineers traced the problem to damaged memory, moved the needed program into working space, and rewrote its references. First, the spacecraft's systems report became readable. Then science data returned from all four working instruments.
