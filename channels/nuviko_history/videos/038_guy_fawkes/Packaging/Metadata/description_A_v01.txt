Guy Fawkes became the famous face of the Gunpowder Plot, but he wasn't the man who led it. That was Robert Catesby, who organized a small group of conspirators planning to kill King James I and members of Parliament. While Catesby led the conspiracy, Fawkes guarded the gunpowder beneath the House of Lords, where he was discovered and arrested before the attack could take place.
