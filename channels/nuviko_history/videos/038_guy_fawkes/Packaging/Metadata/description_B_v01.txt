An anonymous letter set a search in motion before the Gunpowder Plot could be carried out. On 26 October 1605, Lord Monteagle received a warning not to attend Parliament; the letter reached royal authorities, but its author remains unknown. When they searched beneath the House of Lords, they found Guy Fawkes guarding the gunpowder and arrested him before the planned attack.
