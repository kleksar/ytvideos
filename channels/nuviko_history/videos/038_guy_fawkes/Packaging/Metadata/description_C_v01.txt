Why is a failed plot from 1605 remembered with bonfires and fireworks? The Gunpowder Plot was stopped before the planned attack on King James I and Parliament could happen, and the conspirators’ attempt to raise an uprising failed as well. November 5 became a day of thanksgiving; over time, bonfires, fireworks and effigies of Guy Fawkes became part of Bonfire Night.
