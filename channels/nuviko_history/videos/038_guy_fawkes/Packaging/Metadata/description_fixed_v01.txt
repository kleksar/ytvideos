On 5 November 1605, Guy Fawkes was found guarding gunpowder beneath the House of Lords, on the morning King James I was due to open Parliament. Robert Catesby led the plot, but an anonymous warning reached royal authorities and a search uncovered the gunpowder before the planned attack. This film follows the failed conspiracy and uprising, the fate of the plotters, and how November 5 gradually became linked with Bonfire Night.
