On 5 November 1605, Guy Fawkes was found guarding gunpowder beneath the House of Lords as King James I prepared to open Parliament. An anonymous warning helped set a search in motion, and authorities found the explosives before the plotters could act. This film follows the conspiracy, the failed uprising, and how November 5 became tied to the tradition of Bonfire Night.
