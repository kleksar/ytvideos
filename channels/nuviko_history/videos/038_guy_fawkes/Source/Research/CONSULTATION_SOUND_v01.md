# Sound direction consultation — Nuviko History 038

- Consultation: visible ChatGPT project `YT Видео`, model label `Очень высокий` (Extra High), 2026-11-04.
- Narrow brief: restrained instrumental score, no vocals or trailer-style build; guide the mix over the accepted English Guy Fawkes narration, avoid implying that the plot's planned explosion happened.
- Response title: “The Plot That Never Exploded”. This is a creative direction, not a factual review.

## Sound palette

Use a single restrained bed with bass viol / viola da gamba-like low strings, occasional dry lute-like plucks, sustained viol harmonies and only a barely audible chamber-organ support in selected passages. A short recorder motif may appear only near the ending. The palette may suggest early seventeenth-century music but must not claim to reconstruct a specific historical performance. Approximate tempo: 58–66 BPM. No drums, choir, vocals, strong orchestral hits, synthetic risers or epic climax.

## Sparse effects and factual boundaries

At most three or four noticeable effects: a short paper rustle at the letter; one very soft wooden knock around the search; a brief reduction of the music at arrest; and faint distant bonfire crackle, with at most one muted firework only during the remembrance passage. Keep effects away from key words. Do not use an explosion, burning fuse implying an actual blast, building collapse, torture cries or execution impacts. Do not invent a historically specific door or action.

## Mix starting points

Keep narration as the stable foreground. Start music roughly 15–20 LU below short-term speech loudness; add 3–5 dB of speech ducking only if needed, with about 100–200 ms attack and 500–900 ms release and no audible pumping. If needed, reduce score 2–4 dB in the 1.5–4 kHz range. Keep effects quiet. A provisional integrated target is −16 to −14 LUFS and true peak at or below −1 dBTP; adjust by listening to the actual mix, since these are starting points rather than proof of quality.

## Editing principles

Use one continuous musical foundation and change it mainly by adding or removing parts. The main dramatic shifts are the motif stop at arrest and near-silence at the torture order. The November 5 sequence signifies historical memory rather than approval of persecution or executions. Do not accent every image cut; musical changes follow narration blocks.
