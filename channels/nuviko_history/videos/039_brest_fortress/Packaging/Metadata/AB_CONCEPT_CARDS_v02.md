## Pairwise distinction

- A is about the adversary's report and the cost of the assault.
- B is about the changing form and duration of resistance.
- C is about an unidentified author's inscription.

The thumbnails use three different situations (report/map, divided fortress, wall inscription), not color variations of one scene. Pair B is the editorially preferred starting hypothesis for clarity of the film's central distinction; this is not a CTR prediction. Each title is below YouTube's 100-character limit. Exact PNG hashes and local small-preview review are recorded in `THUMBNAIL_QA_v01.md`.

Native YouTube testing supports title and thumbnail variants, including up to three alternatives; description text is a single field and remains fixed during such a test. See the official help: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400 . The experiment remains unstarted pending the user's later release decision and approval of the MP4 and package.
