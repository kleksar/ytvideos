Water lay close to Brest Fortress, but reaching it could be deadly. Inside the battered positions, defenders faced thirst, dwindling supplies and an enemy that had broken into the complex.

The German assault began on June 22, 1941. With communications disrupted and units separated, soldiers fought in scattered pockets rather than as a single garrison.

Follow the struggle through the end of organized defense in June and the isolated resistance that continued into July, including the story of Major Pyotr Gavrilov.
