“I am dying, but I will not surrender! Farewell, Motherland.”

After the war, these words were found inside the ruins of Brest Fortress, dated July 20, 1941. The identity of the person who left them remains unknown.

To understand the world behind that message, we return to the German assault of June 22 and follow the defenders through disrupted communications, thirst and isolated resistance. The film brings the anonymous inscription together with a report left by the commander of the attacking German division.
