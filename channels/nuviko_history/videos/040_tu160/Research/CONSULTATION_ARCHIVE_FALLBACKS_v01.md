However, two specific historical depictions remain prohibited until licensed and appropriately verified evidence is available: an image asserted to show the actual first flight (092), and an image asserted to show the specific 1984 production-aircraft test (099). Governing distinction: archival footage documents an event; an explanatory construction illustrates what the narration says about it. These five substitutions must remain visibly on the explanatory side of that boundary.
