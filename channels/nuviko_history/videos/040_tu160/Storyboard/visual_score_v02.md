## Visual concept

Each frame should reveal a specific thought from the narration through a physically convincing situation, not simply illustrate nouns. The image drama moves through seven acts: impossible requirements; Cold War necessity; wing and structure; engines and range; missiles and strategic role; prototype to service; Soviet collapse and the aircraft's legacy.

## Shared staging rules

- The historical world is ultraphotorealistic, with natural optics, material surfaces, credible scale, light and atmospheric perspective. The default historical world uses photorealistic material scenes. The five approved non-archival fallback shots (031, 092, 096, 099 and 108) use visibly contemporary tactile explanatory constructions; keep their clean modern styling, materials and any required status captions clearly distinct from genuine archival photographs. Do not use faux archive styling for them.
- The recurring host is the channel's only drawn 2D character, matching both accepted traveler references. He appears only as a present-day guide outside historical action, never as an engineer, crew member or witness. Omit him during historical events, engineering work, testing, military service, disarmament and archival scenes.
- Cuts follow meaning, matching geometry, direction or material. Motion is limited to smooth movement of the whole still; separate object animation is not implied. Movement endpoints are set after image review and audio alignment.
- Statuses: **Р** historical reconstruction; **О** explanatory physical model/metaphor; **А** authentic archival image required. `REF` means a real visual anchor must be inspected before generating that view. Reconstructions are never presented as archival footage.
- Every selected image still needs construction, count, continuity, caption-safe framing and motion-envelope review. Source imagery, rights and actual visual proof are logged separately.
