When Endurance sank in the Weddell Sea, 28 men were left on drifting ice with three lifeboats and no way to call for help. Getting off the ice was only the first step.

Follow Ernest Shackleton's party from the wreck to Elephant Island, then across the open ocean in the James Caird. On the island, Frank Wild kept the rest of the crew going while rescue attempts stalled. It took a small Chilean tug and a way through the ice to bring them aboard.
