Six men set out from Elephant Island in a wooden lifeboat barely seven metres long. Their destination was South Georgia, more than 800 miles away. If Frank Worsley missed the island, the 22 men waiting behind might never learn what happened.

Follow the James Caird through the Southern Ocean, where Worsley had only four usable observations of the sun. Reaching shore put the crew within reach of help, but an overland crossing and a separate rescue voyage still lay ahead.
