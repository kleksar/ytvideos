# Endurance — Nuviko History review packaging

Frozen English edit v01. Three different reasons to open the same film.

| Variant | Title | Cover lettering | What a new viewer sees and asks | Film payoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | The Endurance Sank. How Did All 28 Make It Home? | 28 STRANDED | A wreck disappearing beside a crowded ice camp. How can a whole crew get off the drifting ice? | The opening presents the loss and group stakes. The film follows both groups through the final rescue. |
| B | Shackleton’s 800-Mile Gamble in a Lifeboat | ONE SMALL BOAT | A cramped boat tilting into a large wave. Why take it into open ocean, and how could it find help? | The opening sets up the six men's voyage. The crossing shows navigation, the landing and the journey to Stromness. |
| C | Three Rescues Failed. Could a Small Tug Get Through? | FOURTH ATTEMPT | A lone steam tug enters the only open lead in a wall of pack ice. Can this ship get through where the others failed? | The last act distinguishes the three failed attempts and shows Pardo bringing Yelcho to the island. |

- A vs B titles: survival of the whole party after the wreck vs the six men's open-ocean gamble. Images: survivors watching the ship sink vs men navigating a rough sea in the lifeboat.
- A vs C titles: getting everyone off the ice vs making a fourth rescue work. Images: stranded people and wreck vs a rescue ship threading the ice.
- B vs C titles: reaching help in a lifeboat vs a powered tug reaching the stranded crew. Images: close low view of an exposed boat in waves vs aerial view of a tug and a narrow ice channel.

A uses S004 as ice-camp realism reference, B uses S007 as James Caird/wave reference, C uses S107 for Yelcho's silhouette. Separate generated covers and integrated lettering. No expectation of measured audience performance.
