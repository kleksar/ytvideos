Three ships had failed to reach the men stranded on Elephant Island. The fourth was Yelcho, a small Chilean steam tug under Luis Pardo. The tug couldn't force a path through solid pack ice. It had to find a way around it.

After Endurance sank, Shackleton's party had already endured months on drifting ice and split up to seek help. This is the story of how they reached Elephant Island, crossed the ocean in a lifeboat, and finally got the waiting men aboard.
