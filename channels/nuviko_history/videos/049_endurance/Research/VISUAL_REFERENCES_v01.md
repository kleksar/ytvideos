## Stromness reconstruction limitation

For the 20 May 1916 arrival, do not use the modern Manager’s Villa as the period building. Cambridge University Press’s abstract for Basberg and Burton, “New evidence on the Manager’s Villa in Stromness Harbour, South Georgia” (Polar Record 42(2), 2006), states that the villa commonly assumed to be the one Shackleton reached was moved from Ocean Harbour to Stromness in 1925 and replaced the original manager’s dwelling: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/polar-record/article/abs/new-evidence-on-the-managers-villa-in-stromness-harbour-south-georgia/E7030E4AF3841CCB0C0768DC842A2DB5

South Georgia Heritage Trust confirms the 20 May arrival and provides a post-arrival group image, probably taken at Leith Harbour, which is not an architectural reference for Stromness in 1916: https://sght.org/sir-ernest-shackleton/. Shetland Museum and Archives identifies its Stromness villa photograph as February/March 1998, so it is also not a 1916 visual anchor: https://photos.shetlandmuseumandarchives.org.uk/view-item?i=53846. Depict the arrival at a distance with non-identifiable station silhouettes; avoid a recognizable villa or unsupported architectural details.

## South Georgia, rebuild v02

For S071–S075 keep King Haakon Bay at the southwestern side of South Georgia and the whaling stations toward the opposite inhabited coast. The current British Antarctic Survey overview describes the landfall on the south side; the wider expedition's accepted voice describes the western coast. The schematic can show the southwestern sector while preserving the accepted story. For the mountain crossing, distinguish the west-side landing from Stromness across the mountainous interior. Approximate relationship only. Use these pages as written geographic reference, with no direct map reproduction or plotted itinerary. [BAS map announcement](https://www.bas.ac.uk/news/new-map-of-south-georgia-unveiled/) and [map edition, extent and rights](https://data.bas.ac.uk/items/c6003587-141d-4683-957e-56429d2e583e/).
