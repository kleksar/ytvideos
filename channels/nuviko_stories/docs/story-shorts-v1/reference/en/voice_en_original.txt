My brother proposed to his ex-girlfriend's best friend. And then I saw his text messages.

He and his ex had been together for twelve years. She wanted to get married and start a family, but he kept saying he didn't believe in marriage. Eventually, she left him.

Then, out of nowhere, he announced he was engaged. I didn't even know he was dating anyone.

And his ex was left wondering: why was he willing to marry her best friend, but not her?

She'd been like an older sister to me for years. So, when one of my brother's friends showed me the messages, I couldn't just stay quiet.

Turns out, my brother wanted revenge. He wanted to give her best friend exactly what his ex had been waiting for all those years.

My mom and sister told me to stay out of it. But his friend and I went to see her anyway and told her everything.

At first, she didn't believe us. So his friend showed her the messages.

The next day, she texted me saying that at least now she knew she wasn't the problem. She'd already found a new job and was planning to move away.

I told my brother. He barely said anything.

Then he asked if he could stay with me for a while.

He just sat around playing video games and drinking beer. He kept saying he had everything under control, that he'd just taken a week off work.

Then he got drunk and told me he'd gone to see his ex. He'd apologized. And he'd tried to propose to her.

To her. After already getting engaged to her best friend.

I have no idea what's going to happen with that engagement now. My brother won't really explain anything. All he said about his ex was that she really was planning to move away.

So now he's staying at my place, drinking beer and playing video games. He keeps promising he'll pull himself together in a week.

I feel bad for him. He's still my brother. But I don't regret telling her the truth.
