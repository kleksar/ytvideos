My sister decided to show up to my engagement party in a wedding dress. But she had no idea what surprise I had planned for her.

Lera and I share a dad, but we have different moms. And for as long as I can remember, she's always tried to one-up me.

When my boyfriend proposed, we decided to have a small celebration. Nothing huge. Just get our family and friends together, dress up, and have a good evening.

A few days before the party, my cousin messaged me. She sent me a photo of the dress Lera was planning to wear.

I opened the picture.

A long, strapless white dress covered in sparkling stones, with gold trim.

Basically, a wedding dress.

To my engagement party.

At first, I was furious. But then I thought, why cause a scene? I could do something much more interesting.

So I started messaging the guests to say the plans had changed.

Instead of a regular party, we were having a costume party.

My mom and aunts agreed right away. They love that kind of thing.

I messaged my dad separately and asked him to let Lera and her mom know.

Knowing my dad, I was pretty sure he'd forget to tell them.

The day of the party arrived.

The guests showed up in all kinds of costumes. My mom and aunts dressed up as ABBA. My fiancé and I dressed up as cartoon characters too.

And anyone who hadn't had time to prepare got a funny wig or hat.

Then, about an hour late, Lera, her mom, and our dad arrived.

Lera walked in.

In that very same white dress.

And saw everyone around her in costume.

Some people were wearing wigs, some were dressed as movie characters, and some had shown up in completely ridiculous outfits.

And suddenly, her glamorous dress wasn't anything special.

She started getting angry.

Then my fiancé walked up to her and said, perfectly calmly:
Oh, what a beautiful bride costume! For a cheap costume, it's gorgeous!
Lera burst into tears and left the party.

And her mom and my dad started telling me off for being childish. Apparently, I should have warned my sister myself.

But you know what?

After she left, we just carried on celebrating. We had so much fun that we even threw another costume party later.

But I didn't invite my dad to the actual wedding.
