I spent almost my entire budget on a gift for my boyfriend. But when I opened it at home, I cried. I thought I'd thrown my money away.

My boyfriend loves The Lord of the Rings. He has a whole collection of books that he takes great care of.

I don't know much about it myself, but I really wanted to get him something special for Christmas.

Then one day, I saw an old set of Tolkien books in a small antique shop.

Beautiful covers, an unusual box. I thought of him right away.

The set cost fifty dollars. For an ordinary barista like me, that was a lot of money.

But I decided to buy it anyway.

The problem was, the books were sealed in plastic. They wouldn't let me open them in the store, and I couldn't get an internet connection there at all.

At home, I finally took off the wrapping.

And I almost cried.

The pages were a horrible shade of yellow. And when I looked up the set online, I found out it was supposed to have another book.

The Hobbit.

It was missing.

The shop refused to take it back, and I didn't have enough money left for another gift.

I posted in an online collectors' community and asked what I should do with the books.

And that's when things got interesting.

First, people explained that the yellow pages weren't damaged. Apparently, they'd been dyed that color when they were made!

But The Hobbit really was missing from the set.

Then a man I didn't know wrote to me. He had an extra copy and offered to send it to me for free.

I said yes.

He wouldn't even let me pay for shipping.

A while later, a package arrived.

Actually, several packages.

I opened the first one and saw the missing Hobbit.

Then I started pulling out the other things.

There were rare Tolkien editions I didn't even know existed.

And a poster signed by Andy Serkis, the actor who played Gollum!

It turned out this stranger had decided not just to help me complete the set, but to give us a real surprise.

I sat there surrounded by books, crying again.

Only this time, they were tears of happiness.

Christmas arrived.

My boyfriend started opening his presents. With each new book, he grew more and more amazed.

At one point, he was even worried I'd taken out loans to buy it all.

So I told him about the stranger.

About the gift I'd thought was ruined. About the missing book. And about the person who had sent us all these treasures completely free of charge.

My boyfriend was so touched that he teared up too.

And I saved the old fifty-dollar set for his very last gift.

I put The Hobbit underneath it.

The very book that started it all.
