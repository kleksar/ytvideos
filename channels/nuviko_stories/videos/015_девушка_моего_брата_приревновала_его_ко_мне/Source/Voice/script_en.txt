My brother's girlfriend decided I was trying to humiliate her on purpose. All because of a birthday gift. And the strangest part? She seemed jealous of me because of my brother.

Even though we're twins.

We've loved building LEGO since we were kids. Our family didn't have much money, so every new set was a big event.

And even now, at twenty-five, we still give each other LEGO sets.

For our birthday, I gave him a huge castle, and he gave me Notre-Dame Cathedral. We were both thrilled.

His girlfriend got him a gift too. A beautiful watch.

My brother thanked her. He liked it. But he was noticeably more excited about the LEGO. He was already talking about building it the next weekend.

A little while later, I got a message from his girlfriend.

Apparently, I'd bought an expensive gift on purpose to make her look bad. And I should have warned her in advance that I was planning to give him something expensive.

I explained that my brother and I had been exchanging LEGO sets our whole lives, and there was nothing unusual about it.

But she kept going.

She said LEGO sets were children's toys. She was trying to turn my brother into a grown man, and I was ruining everything.

Then she started writing as if I were some kind of rival trying to steal her boyfriend.

Let me remind you. I'm his sister. His twin.

At first, I didn't even know what to do with that.

But a few days later, I went over to help my brother build the castle. And I decided to show him the messages.

He took my phone.

Read them in silence.

And suddenly said:
Oh, hell no.
Then he took out his phone and texted his girlfriend, telling her to return the keys to his apartment by eight that evening.

And immediately blocked her.

I was stunned. I asked if he was rushing into this.

But my brother said he could live with her not liking LEGO. What he wasn't going to tolerate was what she'd said to his sister.

That evening, his girlfriend did show up.

But instead of just returning the keys, she spent an hour knocking on the door and shouting, demanding to talk.

And my brother didn't even come out.

He sat at home building that same castle, deliberately turning up the music so she'd know he was home but wasn't going to open the door.

Eventually, she dropped the keys in the mailbox and left.

Later, my brother admitted he'd been acting childish on purpose.

Since she wanted so badly to make him grow up.

And the funniest part was that the whole time she was outside demanding a serious conversation, he was calmly building LEGO.

The very set that caused their breakup.
