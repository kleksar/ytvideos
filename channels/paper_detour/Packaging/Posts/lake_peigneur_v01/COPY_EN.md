# Lake Peigneur — five-card draft

1. A LAKE BEGAN TO DISAPPEAR
On November 20, 1980, drilling beneath Lake Peigneur, Louisiana, triggered a disaster no one expected.

2. A MINE BENEATH THE WATER
Deep below the lake lay a salt mine. Suddenly, lake water began pouring into the underground workings.

3. THE WHIRLPOOL GREW
A massive whirlpool formed, pulling in drilling equipment, a tugboat, and barges as the lake drained away.

4. THE WATER CHANGED DIRECTION
The nearby canal reversed its flow. Instead of carrying water away, it began feeding the enormous sinkhole.

5. THE LAKE CAME BACK
Everyone escaped alive. Over the next two days, water flowed back in, refilling the lake and transforming its landscape.
