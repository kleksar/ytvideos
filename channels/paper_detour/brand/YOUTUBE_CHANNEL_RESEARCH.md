## Editorial approach

YouTube describes discovery in terms of appeal, engagement and satisfaction; search separately considers relevance, engagement and quality. Focus on topic, truthful title and thumbnail, opening, viewer comprehension and the actual content. Tags play a minor role and are useful for common misspellings; keyword stuffing and changing channel country are not a reliable growth strategy. See [recommendations](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16533387), [search](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438), and [tags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402).

After any separately authorized public launch, assess impressions/CTR together, retention and drop-offs, average view duration and traffic sources; private videos cannot provide representative public discovery data. The [retention report](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415) supports analysis of introductions and key moments. YouTube's [A/B testing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400) has feature and visibility restrictions; do not change privacy just to run it.

The intended original language is English, with PT-BR and ES translations/dubs when ready and reviewable. See [video language](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6304325), [automatic dubbing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15569972), [multi-language audio](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13338784), and [translated metadata](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6289575). Do not claim these tracks are present until verified. YouTube states [related videos for Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157) must be public or unlisted; do not change a private film's visibility solely to link it.
