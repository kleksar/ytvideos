A journalist told readers not to panic about AI taking tech jobs. A young graduate wrote back that telling people not to worry was bad advice.

Her name is Kiran Maya Sheikh. After graduating from UC Irvine in 2025, she struggled to find her first full-time software engineering job.

Kiran worried that companies wanted experience while AI could take over some junior work. As she searched, she kept learning, volunteering and meeting people.

By late October 2026, she told the journalist she had started her first full-time job as a software engineer at Virginia Digital Service.

Her story doesn't prove AI caused any rejection. But it leaves a question: if entry-level opportunities shrink, where will tomorrow's experts learn?
