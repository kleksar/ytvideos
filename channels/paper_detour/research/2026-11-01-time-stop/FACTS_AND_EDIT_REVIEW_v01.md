Supports the ordinary mechanism behind «No light could reach your eyes» in the expressly fictional setup. 2. [NIDCD — How Do We Hear?](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/how-do-we-hear), accessed 2026-11-01. Official NIDCD explanation: sound waves in air reach the eardrum, producing vibrations and subsequently neural signals. Supports the need for transmitted vibrations to hear external sound. It does **not** establish complete subjective silence in a person whose body continues functioning.

No claims about absolute zero, infinite material hardness, death or thermodynamic catastrophe have been added.
