Stopping time for five seconds sounds like the perfect superpower.

Until you realize what else stops.

Suppose everything outside your body freezes—even light. You wouldn't see a world on pause. No light could reach your eyes. Just darkness.

You wouldn't hear the outside world either. Sound needs vibrations, and air frozen in time couldn't carry them.

But that air is the real trap.

If every molecule is locked in place, you couldn't push through it. You couldn't even draw it into your lungs.

You'd be stuck like an insect in amber. Even a raindrop in front of your face wouldn't budge.

So much for unlimited power.

Five seconds in the dark. Cut off from the world. Unable to move.

Then everything starts again.

And the only thing you've accomplished?

Making yourself five seconds older.
