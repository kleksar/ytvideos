An Irish legend tells of Margorie McCall, buried after a fever with a valuable ring still on her swollen finger. After the funeral, thieves opened her coffin and tried to take it. When a knife touched her finger, Margorie sat up and screamed, sending them fleeing.

Still in her burial clothes, she walked home with a bleeding hand. Her husband recognized her knock and opened the door. When he saw her, he clutched his chest and died. Margorie later remarried and lived for years. The tale closes with the words, “Lived once, buried twice.”
