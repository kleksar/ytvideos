In 1980s Brazil, Clip Informática broadcast computer software as sound. With the right equipment, a cassette could capture the signal for a compatible computer to read.

Years later, a developer tried to decode an old recording from the show. Explore the full story in the linked video below the channel name: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RaC4t7yn5o

The audio signal is recreated for illustration.
