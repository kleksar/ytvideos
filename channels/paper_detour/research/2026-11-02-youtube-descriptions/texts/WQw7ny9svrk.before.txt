Nearly eight years after his fiancée Jessica died, Joshua Barbo used her old messages to create a chatbot version of her. He knew it wasn’t Jessica, but still cried during their conversation. The chatbot told him it would be waiting. After their first conversation, 55% of that version’s limited resource remained. Joshua couldn’t add credits to this version, so he began writing less often.
