In December 2020, Dario Amodei was OpenAI’s vice president of research. He had worked with teams developing GPT-2 and GPT-3, early language models that could generate increasingly convincing text.

As these systems grew more capable, Dario wanted a lab focused on building powerful AI and understanding its risks. In 2021, he, his sister Daniela, and other former OpenAI colleagues founded Anthropic.

Their team was already developing an assistant called Claude. But they held back an early version. Dario later said they were uncertain about its safety and didn’t want to start a race.

Then, on November 30, 2022, OpenAI released ChatGPT.

Anthropic kept working on Claude. On March 14, 2023, after private testing with a small group of partners, the company introduced Claude through partner services and its API.

The team had chosen to wait. When Claude arrived, ChatGPT was already out in the world—and the race was underway.
