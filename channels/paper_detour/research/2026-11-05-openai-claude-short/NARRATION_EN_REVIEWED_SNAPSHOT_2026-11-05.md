The people building Claude once helped build OpenAI’s models.

In 2020, Dario Amodei led research at OpenAI. He had worked with teams behind GPT-2 and GPT-3. Their language models could write increasingly convincing text.

But as the systems grew more capable, one question became harder to ignore: how could people keep powerful AI safe?

Dario wanted an organization built to develop advanced AI while focusing on safety. In 2021, he, his sister Daniela, and several former OpenAI colleagues founded Anthropic.

Before ChatGPT launched, Anthropic had already built an early version of its own assistant. The team waited. They were still uncertain about its safety and did not want to start a race.

Then OpenAI released ChatGPT on November 30, 2022.

Anthropic kept working. Its team trained Claude to check and revise answers against a written set of principles.

On March 14, 2023, after private testing with partners, Anthropic introduced Claude.

Dario had left OpenAI almost two years before ChatGPT appeared. Now, the company he helped build was offering a competing assistant. Former colleagues were building alternatives to one another’s AI.
