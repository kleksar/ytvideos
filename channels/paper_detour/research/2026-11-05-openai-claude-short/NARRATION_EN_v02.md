Dario Amodei helped build OpenAI’s early language models. Then he left—and helped create Claude.

It was December 2020. ChatGPT hadn’t launched yet. Dario was OpenAI’s vice president of research. He had worked with the teams behind GPT-2 and GPT-3, systems that could generate increasingly convincing text.

But making AI more capable raised another problem: how could people understand it, control it, and rely on its answers?

Dario wanted an entire organization focused on developing powerful AI alongside its safety. In 2021, he, his sister Daniela, and other former OpenAI colleagues founded Anthropic.

Before ChatGPT launched, Anthropic already had an early version of its own assistant. But the team held it back. They were still uncertain about its safety and didn’t want to kick off a race.

Then, on November 30, 2022, OpenAI released ChatGPT.

Anthropic continued its work. The team trained Claude to check and revise its answers against a written set of principles.

On March 14, 2023, after private testing with partners, Anthropic introduced Claude.

Dario had left OpenAI almost two years before ChatGPT’s launch. Now, the new company he had helped build was offering a competing assistant. People who once worked together were building alternatives to each other’s AI.
