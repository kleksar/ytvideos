Dario Amodei helped build OpenAI’s early language models. Then he left—and helped create Claude.

In December 2020, Dario was OpenAI’s vice president of research. ChatGPT hadn’t launched yet. He had worked with the teams behind GPT-2 and GPT-3, systems that could generate increasingly convincing text.

But making AI more capable raised another problem: how could people understand it, control it, and rely on its answers?

Dario wanted an entire organization focused on developing powerful AI alongside its safety. In 2021, he, his sister Daniela, and other former OpenAI colleagues founded Anthropic.

Before ChatGPT launched, Anthropic already had an early version of its own assistant, Claude. But the team held it back. They were still uncertain about its safety and didn’t want to kick off a race.

Then, on November 30, 2022, OpenAI released ChatGPT.

On March 14, 2023, after private testing with partners, Anthropic introduced Claude and offered businesses access to use it in their own products.

Dario had left OpenAI almost two years before ChatGPT’s launch. OpenAI now faced a competing assistant built by people from its own research team.
