Anthropic held back its AI assistant, Claude, because the team wasn’t sure it was safe. While they waited, OpenAI released ChatGPT.

In December 2020, Dario Amodei led research at OpenAI, where teams were building early language models. In 2021, he, his sister Daniela, and several former OpenAI colleagues founded Anthropic, a lab focused on powerful AI and its risks.

The team was already developing Claude. Dario later said they delayed an early version because they were uncertain about its safety and didn’t want to start a race.

OpenAI released ChatGPT on November 30, 2022. On March 14, 2023, after private tests with partners, Anthropic introduced Claude.

ChatGPT was already in users’ hands; Claude’s first access came through partner services and an API.
