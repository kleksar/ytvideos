The people building an AI chatbot were also the ones holding it back. For about six months, they delayed an early release over safety doubts—and because they didn't want to start an AI race.

One of them was Dario Amodei. He had been OpenAI's vice president of research, working alongside the teams behind GPT-2 and GPT-3. In late 2020, he left. With former colleagues, including his sister Daniela, he founded Anthropic in 2021. The goal: AI that could be powerful, but also more reliable and easier to control.

Their chatbot was called Claude.

OpenAI released ChatGPT in November 2022. Anthropic introduced Claude in March 2023, following private tests with partners. At first, people accessed it through partner chat services and an API.

Claude did come out. But its creators had faced an unusual choice: building the technology didn't mean they had to rush its release.
