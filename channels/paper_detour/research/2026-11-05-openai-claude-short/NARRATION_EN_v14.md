Before ChatGPT became a hit, another company already had an early AI chatbot. But the team held back its release for roughly six months—even though that meant missing a commercial opening.

That company was Anthropic, co-founded by Dario Amodei. Its chatbot was Claude. The team wasn't sure about its safety properties and didn't want to start an AI race.

ChatGPT launched in November 2022. Anthropic could have capitalized on that moment, but chose to release later. After closed tests with partners, it introduced Claude in March 2023.

The delay had real commercial consequences. It also shaped Anthropic's culture: the team had chosen to miss an opportunity rather than rush a release it wasn't confident about.
