Before ChatGPT became a hit, another AI company had already held back an early chatbot. When ChatGPT took off, it had a chance to ride that wave—but chose to release later.

The company was Anthropic. Its chatbot was Claude. The team wasn't sure about the model's safety properties and didn't want to start an AI race.

OpenAI launched ChatGPT in November 2022. People could open a website and try it. Claude wouldn't be introduced until March 2023.

In 2026, Anthropic cofounder Dario Amodei said the company had probably ceded the lead in AI for everyday users to companies that launched sooner.
