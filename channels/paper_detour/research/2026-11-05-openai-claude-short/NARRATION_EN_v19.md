In November 2022, anyone could open a website and try ChatGPT. Another company already had an early AI chatbot—but had chosen to delay its release.

It was Anthropic, and the chatbot was Claude. The team wasn't sure about the model's safety properties and didn't want to start an AI race.

ChatGPT became a hit. Claude wouldn't be introduced until March 2023.

In 2026, Anthropic cofounder Dario Amodei said the company had probably ceded the lead in AI for everyday users to companies that launched sooner.
