# EN narration — v20 (draft)

Anthropic didn't want to be the company that started an AI race. So it delayed an early version of Claude, its AI chatbot, just before ChatGPT took off.

The team also wasn't sure about Claude's safety properties. In November 2022, anyone could open a website and try ChatGPT. Anthropic chose to introduce Claude later, in March 2023.

In 2026, Anthropic cofounder Dario Amodei looked back at what that timing might have meant. He said Anthropic had probably given companies that launched sooner the lead in AI products for ordinary people.
