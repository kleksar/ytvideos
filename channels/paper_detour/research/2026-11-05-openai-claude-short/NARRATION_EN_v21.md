In 2026, Anthropic cofounder Dario Amodei said his company had probably let AI companies that launched sooner take the lead with everyday users. The surprising part: Anthropic already had an early version of its own chatbot, Claude.

But the team chose to delay its release. They weren't sure how safe the model was, and didn't want to set off a race to release AI.

Then came November 2022. ChatGPT opened on a public website anyone could use. By the time Anthropic introduced Claude in March 2023, people had already had months to try ChatGPT themselves.
