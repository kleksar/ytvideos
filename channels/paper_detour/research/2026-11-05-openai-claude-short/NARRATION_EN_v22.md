In November 2022, anyone could open a website and try ChatGPT. Anthropic already had an early version of its own chatbot, Claude, but chose to delay its release.

The team wasn't sure how safe Claude was, and didn't want to be the company that started an AI race.

By the time Anthropic introduced Claude in March 2023, ChatGPT had already become a hit.

In 2026, Anthropic cofounder Dario Amodei said the company had probably let companies that launched sooner take the lead in AI for everyday users.
