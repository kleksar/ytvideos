Why Roofs Collapsed Miles From Mount Pinatubo #Pinatubo #History

## Описание

When Mount Pinatubo erupted in the Philippines in 1991, volcanic ash fell far beyond the mountain. A typhoon brought heavy rain, soaking the ash and adding so much weight that roofs collapsed.
