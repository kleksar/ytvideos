## Птицы и Луна — UqWIwpbTMik

Название (предлагаемое): The Scholar Who Thought Birds Spent Winter on the Moon

Текущее название: Did People Really Think Birds Flew to the Moon?

Описание:

In the 17th century, English scholar Charles Morton proposed that swallows and storks spent the winter on the Moon. More than a century later, a remarkable stork offered a different clue.

Shot near Klütz, Germany, in 1822, the bird carried an arrow from Central Africa in its neck. It had crossed a great distance, providing physical evidence of long-distance bird migration. The specimen is preserved at the University of Rostock.
