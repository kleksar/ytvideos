Inside a fossilized burrow about 250 million years old, two very different animals were found together: Thrinaxodon, a small predator, and Broomistega, a young amphibian with injured ribs.

This short follows a possible explanation for how they came to share the shelter and were buried together. The fossil preserves their remains; the animals' final moments are a reconstruction.
