One legend about the Taj Mahal tells of Ahmad, the architect placed in charge of its construction—and the terrible price he and his assistants paid after the monument was finished.

This short retells a story about skill, power and a ruler who feared that its creator might build something just as magnificent again. The tale is presented as a legend, not an established account of what happened to the builders.
