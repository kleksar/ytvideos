Two planes
collided in midair.
One
ended up stuck
on top
of the other.
They were
still flying.
It was 1940,
over a small
town in Australia.
Three men
parachuted out.
The pilot
in the top
plane,
Leonard Fuller,
stayed.
He was afraid
both planes
would crash
into the town.
His engines
had stopped,
but his controls
still worked.
The plane
underneath pulled
them forward.
Fuller steered
them both.
Then
the lower plane
started
losing power.
Fuller aimed
for a field
and hauled back
on the controls.
Both planes slid
across the grass
and stopped,
still locked
together.
All four men
survived.
