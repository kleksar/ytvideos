**His Engines Died. He Landed Two Planes. #AviationHistory #Shorts**

Two training planes collided over a small Australian town in 1940. They stayed locked together, and pilot Leonard Fuller had to find a way to land both. A paper diorama retells the Brocklesby midair collision.
