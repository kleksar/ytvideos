## Общая часть

Maximum available image quality, native resolution and fine detail. Target 4K UHD 2160×3840, portrait 9:16. Make one finished opaque full-bleed handmade paper and cardstock diorama. Visible paper fibers, folds, cut edges, layered shadows, believable physical light and depth. Fill all edges naturally without a decorative border, black bars, subtitles, stamps, or a forced blank strip. Keep the key object comfortably visible throughout a modest horizontal pan or zoom. Use the supplied Paper Detour image for material and staging only, without copying its story, object, pose or words.

## Сцены

**S001, сцепка над полями.** Dramatic low left three-quarter side angle. Two Avro Anson Mk I trainers already touching, stacked one on top of the other and facing the same direction. Dark olive upper aircraft N4876, pale ochre lower L9162. Two distinct fuselages, separate tailfins, each a low-wing twin-engine aircraft. Make the contact readable with one hard shadow. Tiny golden fields far below and layers of cool blue-grey paper cloud. No crash fire, smoke or third plane. No typography. References AWM 042590 and P00410.001 for the joined airframes, P03 for material and perspective.

**S002, городок.** Straight overhead view of a thick sculptural paper relief map of the 1940 agricultural district, a compact rural settlement surrounded by ochre paddocks and a few dark green tree lines. One small clearly symbolic model of the joined aircraft pair above it. No literal route, numerical markers, directional claims or modern roads. One centered supported cartographic tab with exact raised letters AUSTRALIA on line one and 1940 on line two. Keep the tab large enough for a phone. Use P02 for dimensional relief and visual hierarchy and AWM 042590 for silhouette only.

**S003, трое покидают самолёты.** Very wide lateral view with exactly three separated cream round parachute canopies, staggered vertically, each visibly connected by paper cords to its own tiny airman silhouette. The interlocked olive and ochre aircraft remain together higher and farther right. Low fields below. No fourth parachute, no fire, no typography. Show different depth and scale from the opener. AWM 042590 and P00410.001 for the joined airframes; P03 for layered depth and material.

**S004, Фуллер остаётся.** Inside the 1940 Anson upper cockpit, a young paper figure with the features of Fuller, right-hand figure in AWM SUK10426, wearing a period flying helmet and muted dark wool instead of that portrait's parade cap. Low three-quarter camera near the instruments. His right hand grips the control column, left hand braced on the seat edge. His eyes look forward and slightly down. Patch of farmland and a small cluster of roofs beyond the windscreen. One readable ivory label LEONARD FULLER on a separate layered paper name tab at the upper left, no readable fake gauge numbers. Use AWM SUK10426 for facial features and P04 for formed paper human/material.

**S005, управление и тяга.** Broadside explanatory cardstock cutaway with two touching stacked aircraft profiles, distinct olive upper and ochre lower airframes. Open a clear section of the top cabin so one control column is visible; show a crisp stationary upper propeller. A sculpted graphic paper airflow ribbon at the lower engine level means thrust from the lower aircraft as a system. It does not count engines or show smoke. Two plain short labels only, CONTROL in line with upper cockpit and POWER at the lower engine level, on physically supported labels with shadows. AWM aircraft references for geometry, P01 for material and cutaway clarity.

**S006, поле в окне.** Interior pilot-eye angle looking forward across one paper hand on the control column through the Anson's faceted windscreen. Field edge comes into line with the view ahead; a field fills much of the window and town roofs remain distant and off to the side. Same period paper sleeve as S004, no second hand required in view, no face. A view of the goal through the glazing rather than an instrument close-up. No typography. Use the accepted S004 image for cockpit and sleeve continuity when available.

**S007, касание.** Camera in the grass at the landing field edge. The joined aircraft pair descends toward the grass, with each fuselage nose distinct and both airframes still touching. Green and ochre grass folded into sculptural foreground strips gives the tiny vertical clearance scale. Quiet air and no flames, explosion, sparks, violent debris or definitive gear deployment. No typography. AWM 042590 and P00410.001 for geometry and P03 for physical material depth.

**S008, остановились.** Calm reverse three-quarter angle on the two paper aircraft resting joined in a broad grassy paddock. Model the relative positions from AWM 042590 and P00410.001. Soft late-day-looking highlight as artistic lighting, without implying actual hour. Subtle flattened grass traces their slide. Raised creamy lettering ALL FOUR SURVIVED on a supported short paper tab beside the picture, clear of both fuselages and the future subtitle baseline. No new people or additional aircraft. Use the historical photos for arrangement and P06 for consequence-led paper staging, without copying its object or setting.
