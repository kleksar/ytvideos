## Current YouTube specification

The official [Create a post — computer help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7124474?hl=en-GB) says one image post can contain up to 10 images; supported types are JPG, PNG, GIF and WEBP; each file can be up to 16 MB; 1:1 is recommended because images appear square in the feed. The page does not specify a mandatory pixel resolution. It also documents scheduling an image post by choosing **Schedule post** and setting a date, time and time zone.

The [TeamYouTube carousel announcement](https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/446107479/new-ways-to-connect-with-your-audience-using-image-posts?hl=en-GB) explicitly says viewers swipe through a carousel of up to 10 images. It describes image-post carousels appearing in the Shorts feed as a rollout to eligible creators, so the feed placement is not guaranteed for every channel or post.

YouTube's [Posts help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9409631) says image posts may also appear in the Shorts feed. That is a possible surface, not a reach or view guarantee.
