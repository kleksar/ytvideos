## Editorial distinction and risk

Our proposed angle combines a simple explanation of turning sound into program data with a modern investigator's reported recovery of a signal preserved in a specific broadcast recording. It is not a claim of a unique topic: the general premise and the Brazilian case already have direct competitors. Do not claim that any competitor omitted the recovery without reviewing that video in full. Titles, views and outcomes may change; there is no proven low-competition niche.
