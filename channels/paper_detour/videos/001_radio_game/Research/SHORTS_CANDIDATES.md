## 3. The radio show you could read

**Hook:** One radio broadcast carried Portuguese speech and an encoded Japanese text at the same time.

**Story:** A contemporaneous article reports that on 10 July 1988 Rádio USP used two FM stereo channels: one carried Portuguese speech, the other audio encoding of the corresponding Japanese text, which a computer connected to the radio could display. It is a separate broadcast from the 1987-catalogued programme above.

**Source:** [Superinteressante, 31 August 1988](https://super.abril.com.br/comportamento/computador-fm/).

**Boundaries:** Do not call this real-time machine translation, assume that the game broadcast used the same stereo scheme, or invent individual listeners.
