## English title

**Could This Radio Signal Contain a Computer Game?**

The question makes the signal and the game concrete without claiming the game was successfully launched. It leaves the title of the game for the story. Alternative if the final cut emphasizes explanation over investigation: **The Radio Broadcast That Carried Computer Software**. These are editorial choices, not tested click-through results.

## English description

An archived copy of Clip Informática episode 102, dated to 1987, is catalogued with MSX software audio near the end.

Years later, developer Weber Kai tried to decode a separate online recording he identified as episode 102 and reported a match between the recovered data and a computer game.

How could software travel through a radio signal — and what can the surviving evidence actually tell us?

#RetroComputing #ComputerHistory #TechnologyHistory
