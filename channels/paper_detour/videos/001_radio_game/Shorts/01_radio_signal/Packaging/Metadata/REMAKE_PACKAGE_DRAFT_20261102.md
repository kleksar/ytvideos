**Title:** When Radio Sent Software as Sound #RetroComputing #Shorts

**Description:**

In 1980s Brazil, Clip Informática sent computer programs over the radio as sound. With the right equipment, listeners could record the signal onto cassette tape, then use a compatible computer to try to read its patterns as data.

Years later, a developer tried to decode an old recording from the show.

Watch the full film: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RaC4t7yn5o
