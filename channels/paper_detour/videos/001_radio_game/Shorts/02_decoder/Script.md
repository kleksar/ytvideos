# The Game in an Old Radio Recording

Rarovista · Short 02 · English · preliminary draft for comparison with ChatGPT Pro. Vertical 9:16; self-contained investigation. Voice and video have not been produced. Working estimate: 35–55 seconds; measure after recording.

## Narration

An old radio recording was quiet and running fast. A developer tried to read computer data from it.

Weber Kai examined an online recording he identified as an episode of the Brazilian show Clip Informática. He extracted the relevant sound and published instructions for reading it with a decoder.

Those instructions describe six output files, each representing a block of tape data.

What was in the signal?

Kai reported that the recovered data matched an MSX game called Avenger.

There's a limit to that discovery: we haven't independently repeated the comparison, and the public notes don't show the game running.

Still, a strange sound can hold a message that only the right machine knows how to read.
