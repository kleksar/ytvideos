In 1988, a Rádio USP broadcast carried Portuguese speech on one stereo channel and encoded Japanese text on the other. A computer connected to the radio could decode the signal and display the text.

The computer did not translate the speech: both kinds of information travelled in the same broadcast.

This visual retelling is based on a report in Superinteressante about the July 10, 1988 broadcast. The images are a paper-diorama reconstruction; they are not original broadcast audio or a verified recreation of the historical computer screen.

Source: https://super.abril.com.br/comportamento/computador-fm/
