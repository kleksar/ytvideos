# The Radio Show You Could Read

Rarovista · Short 03 · English · preliminary draft for comparison with ChatGPT Pro. Vertical 9:16; a different 1988 broadcast from the first Short's archived programme. Voice and video have not been produced. Working estimate: 35–55 seconds; measure after recording.

## Narration

In nineteen eighty-eight, a Brazilian radio broadcast carried two versions of a message at once.

One was spoken in Portuguese. The other sounded like noise.

The second signal wasn't another voice. It encoded the message as Japanese text.

According to a report published that year, Rádio USP used the two channels of an FM stereo broadcast: Portuguese speech on one, and the encoded text as sound on the other.

A listener could connect a radio to a computer, which decoded the signal and displayed the Japanese text on screen.

One broadcast. Two kinds of information. And a radio message you could read.
