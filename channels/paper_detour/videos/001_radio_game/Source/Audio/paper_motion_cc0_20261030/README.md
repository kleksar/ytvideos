These are *candidates*, not approved effects: audition each in context and omit anything that competes with speech or sounds cartoonish. Place the sound on the visible onset of physical movement, without forcing an effect on every animated element. Set starting gain around -18 to -24 dB and adjust by ear to sit unobtrusively below narration and the continuous music bed. Original-file gain and masking vary, so these are not measured targets; verify the final mix, peaks and synchronization in context. No rejected cassette button click or siren is included. No clips have been placed on the timeline here, no listening verification has been performed, and no claims are made about integration.
