Images inspected individually for legibility, spelling, brand consistency and ambiguity. English lettering is embedded, so separate localized versions are required if the same picture track is used with non-English dubs. Generated artwork remains a reconstruction rather than documentary evidence. The discarded alternate match image showed six pairs of shapes and was not copied into the project, since it could imply that each of six files was independently confirmed to match.
