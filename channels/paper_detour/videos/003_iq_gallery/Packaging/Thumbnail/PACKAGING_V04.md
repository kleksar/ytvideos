## YouTube guidance checked

- Video titles allow up to 100 characters; descriptions allow 5,000. This title is 45 characters and the description is 399. [Edit video settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404?hl=en-uk).
- Up to three hashtags from a video's description may be shown by the title; avoid hashtag stuffing. [YouTube hashtag guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en).
- Tags are secondary for discovery and mainly help with common spelling variants, so the prepared tags are supplementary. [YouTube recommendation-system guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650?hl=en).
- YouTube says realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered content requires disclosure; non-realistic AI work does not. [GenAI disclosure guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en).
