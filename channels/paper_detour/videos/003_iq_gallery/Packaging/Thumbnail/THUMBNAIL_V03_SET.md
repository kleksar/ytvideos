## Direction and copy

The user rejected the single-person `IQ 228?` cover because it revealed the answer immediately. The new direction puts all 13 characters from the finished gallery together, gives them equal status, and asks a question without showing scores or implying a verified ranking.

Exact copy on all ten images: **THINK YOU'RE SMARTER THAN THEM?**

This asks viewers to compare themselves with the group while withholding every score and the eventual order. It is an invitation to guess, not a claim that the images establish a scientific comparison.

## Extra High consultation record

Both creative questions were sent in Google Chrome to the YT Видео project chat after the visible model label was checked as **Extra High**. Each request covered one narrow decision.

1. Copy recommendation: ask for a curiosity-led thumbnail question for the IQ Gallery that would not reveal the results. The recommendation was **“Think You're Smarter Than Them?”**, because it invites self-comparison without promising an objective IQ test. [ChatGPT project chat](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-p-6ae147d3975481919b5bfda6935777d0-yt-video/c/6ae7954a-4308-83eb-b1ee-d8a6abc5041f)
2. Composition options: ask for ten distinct ways to arrange a collage of the 13 people without a ranked winner. The response proposed orbit, balanced rows, split panels, a diagonal, tiers, a fan, mosaic, a ring, a zigzag, and a three-column gallery. The final ten layouts adapt those suggestions to the existing 9:16 Paper Detour art.
