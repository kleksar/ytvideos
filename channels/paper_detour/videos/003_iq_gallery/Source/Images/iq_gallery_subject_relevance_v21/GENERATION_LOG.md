## Second Extra High type review

Before the follow-up request, the coordinator again confirmed the visible ChatGPT.com mode in Chrome was **Extra High**. The response recommended replacing the serif I/Q pair with broad rounded, non-serif paper I/Q glyphs, using a single physical paper set for the names, IQ, and numbers, and retaining v13 glyphs only when they match that family. Fixed geometry: 96 px names; IQ and digits all 108 px; x=540; row centers y=1304/1426/1560; 10 px between I and Q, 26 px between IQ and first digit, 10 px between digits; maximum row width 820 px. It also specified cream #F1E3C9, terracotta #C47D45, 6–9 px paper edge, and a consistent deep-blue shadow offset 7–10 px with 10–14 px softness. Decorative stickers, digital outlines, and serif glyphs are excluded.

The title block uses the common optical center x=540, row centers y=1304/1426/1560, visible heights 96/96/108 px, and maximum width 820 px. The score line is assembled from the separately cropped, broad I/Q pair and individual digits; visible gaps are 10 px between I/Q, 26 px between IQ and the first digit, and 10 px between digits. All four use the same cream paper face, warm orange edge, depth, and soft shadow. The initial scene motifs (pen stroke, chess cells, music rhythm, page-edge strips) were removed after full-size review because they read as separate decorative stickers and disrupted the typography.
