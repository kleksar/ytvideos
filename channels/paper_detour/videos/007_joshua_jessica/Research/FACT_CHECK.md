# Fact check — Joshua and Jessica, 31 October 2026

Independent reviewer: `/root/factcheck_final` (paper_fact_checker). Source: Jason Fagone, [The Jessica Simulation](https://www.sfchronicle.com/projects/2021/jessica-simulation-artificial-intelligence/), San Francisco Chronicle, 23 July 2021, based on reporting and excerpts of the conversation.

- Jessica died on 11 December 2012; the simulation conversation began on 24 September 2020. Describe this as *nearly* eight years.
- Joshua created a GPT-3 simulation through Project December using a description and several of her old messages; do not assert the full correspondence was used.
- He knew the simulation was not Jessica. He became emotional and cried. He later said that the experience had helped him; no claim that the chatbot induced mental illness, addiction or loss of critical thinking is established.
- His promise to return and the chatbot's response “I will be here waiting for you” occurred after he had fallen asleep twice and woken in the early afternoon, so do not describe that exchange as taking place at dawn.
- Following the initial conversation, 55% of that particular bot's limited resource remained. Later he conserved it; at the time of reporting 33% remained. Do not claim he only discovered its limit after talking, that this version reached zero, or that 55% was literally Jessica's life. A new bot could be created but was not the same simulation.
- “He was afraid of losing her a second time” is an editorial metaphor supported by his reluctance to watch that simulation die, not a diagnosis or a verbatim statement.
- The full original private conversation archive was not independently obtained; the evidence is the journalist's published excerpts and reporting.
