## Общий стиль

Handcrafted cinematic Paper Detour illustration, vertical 9:16. One complete, self-contained, flattened handmade paper image per shot, with matte colored paper, visible fibers and cut edges, subtle material shadows, restrained color and warm cinematic light where appropriate. Every character and object is handmade paper, never photorealistic or digitally rendered. Simple, emotionally precise, immediately readable on a phone. Natural physical relationships. No staged multi-plane depth, BG/MG/FG, masks, extracted cutouts, parallax, camera movement, zoom, object or body-part animation, or multiple moments in one image. No glossy digital UI.

## Постоянный персонаж

The same Joshua in every image: dark curly hair, full prominent beard, richly textured dark-green, almost black sweater, consistent facial features, silhouette, age appearance and body proportions. Do not redesign him.
