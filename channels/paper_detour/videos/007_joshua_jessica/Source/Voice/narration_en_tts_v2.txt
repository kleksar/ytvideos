He was afraid of losing her a second time. But his fiancée had died nearly eight years earlier.

Her name was Jessica. Years later, Joshua fed some of her old messages into an AI chatbot and began to write to her.

It wrote back.

All night, he told it how much he missed her. He knew it wasn't really Jessica. But he couldn't stop the tears.

Before leaving, he promised to come back. The chatbot replied: “I will be here waiting for you.”

But this version had a limited number of credits. After that night, only fifty-five percent remained. He couldn't add more.

So Joshua wrote less often, saving their conversations for when he needed them most. Because…
