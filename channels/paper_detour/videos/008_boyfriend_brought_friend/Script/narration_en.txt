My boyfriend brought his best friend on every single date.

Even when I decided to break up with him, he showed up with his friend again.

We’d been dating for three months, and his friend Matt was always there.

Going to the movies? Matt came with us.

Going for a walk? Matt was there too.

Even if we were just going shopping, he’d tag along.

I tried telling my boyfriend that sometimes I wanted to spend time with him alone.

But every time, he’d tell me how hard Matt’s life was.

His mom had died. His dad wouldn’t speak to him. He had no brothers or sisters, and no girlfriend.

Apparently, apart from my boyfriend, he had almost nobody.

And every time, I ended up feeling like a terrible person.

I even invited my single friend on a date with us once. I thought maybe we could go on a double date.

But that didn’t work out.

Eventually, I’d had enough. I decided to break up with my boyfriend and asked him to meet me so we could talk.

I got to the park.

And there he was, standing with Matt.

I asked if we could talk alone, but my boyfriend insisted I say whatever it was in front of him.

So I did.

I told him I didn’t want this kind of relationship anymore. I was tired of Matt coming on all our dates, and of feeling guilty just for wanting time alone with my boyfriend.

Then I mentioned how he kept making me feel sorry for Matt by telling me about his miserable life.

Matt looked at us, completely confused.

He asked what my boyfriend had been telling me.

It turned out Matt’s mom was alive.

He had a brother and a sister.

And not just a girlfriend. He had a fiancée, who was studying in Europe.

I just stood there, trying to make sense of it all.

We broke up.

About forty minutes later, Matt texted me and told me something else.

My boyfriend had told him that I didn’t like being alone with my boyfriend.

And that I was the one asking Matt to come on our dates.

For three months, my boyfriend had been telling each of us completely different stories.

We never found out why he did it.

But what annoys me most isn’t even that Matt came on our dates for three months.

It’s that the whole time, Matt thought he was doing me a favor.
