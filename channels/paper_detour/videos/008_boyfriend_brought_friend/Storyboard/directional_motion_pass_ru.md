## Motion contract and current limits

- Each shot is designed as BG / MG / FG. Any movement within a shot is horizontal and follows one direction only. No vertical drift, zoom, opposing layer motion, or mirrored characters.
- `←` / `→` is the intended screen direction. `Conditional` means direction is approved as a storyboard choice but movement must wait for canvas, alpha, contact, and registration checks.
- All source images are 941×1672 px. Scene backgrounds are full-frame RGB plates with no confirmed overscan, so **BG stays still in every scene** until its actual horizontal margin is measured. A flat BG does not justify moving grounded characters across a stationary floor; use a static shot or a crop above the contact line if registration cannot preserve contact.
- Character poses and environmental layers are DRAFT. Their pivots, ground/seat contact, hidden fills, and positions against BGs have not been verified in Palmier.
- Accents with zero or very small side margins must be revealed through a real layer overlap or remain static. Do not invent a safe travel path beyond the PNG canvas.
- Scene 04, scene 06, and scene 20 are static for now. Other directions remain conditional. No timings are assigned here; accent cues stay attached to their spoken words.

## Registration checks before enabling motion

1. Check alpha edges and canvas sizes for N-back, B/M/F neutral poses, seated N/M, and cinema seat FG. Account for the seated M image being 1 px shorter.
2. Measure usable horizontal overscan for every BG. Until that is verified, BG remains still.
3. Register pivots and contact: grounded figures must not slide over a still floor, seated figures must stay anchored to seats, and a crop must not reveal a cutout edge.
4. Confirm the single park geography in S02 and S12–18. There is no separate park FG edge file.
5. Check travel margins for A02, A04, A06, A08, A10, A18, A19, A20, A26, A34, A39, and A42. With zero side margin, use internal occlusion or static visibility rather than travel beyond the PNG.
6. Resolve S01 pair/reveal registration, S04 seat contact, S06 duplicate frame, S20 branch registration, and S23 tab travel before calling those composites ready.
