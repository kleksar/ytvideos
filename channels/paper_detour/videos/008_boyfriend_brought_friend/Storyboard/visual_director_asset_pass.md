## Visual rule for this story

Use **Matt's place in the composition** as the repeating visual idea: first an extra person at the edge of a date, then a hidden third figure interrupting the expected pair, finally the position of the friend is reinterpreted by the last sentence. The orange tab can carry that change only on `favor`. This connects to the concrete paper-action approach in the consulted social-media concept session while keeping the metaphor specific to this story.

The reveal sequence must remain gated by the words:

1. Hook: viewers first read N and boyfriend as a pair; a small lateral move reveals Matt at the breakup meeting. This reveals the *odd consequence*, not the reason or Matt's knowledge.
2. Scenes 3–11: repeated presence becomes a pattern, while the boyfriend's sad account is still presented as his account. Matt must not appear to hear it.
3. Scenes 12–18: in one consistent park geography, Matt's confusion precedes the sequential corrections: mother alive → siblings → fiancée → Europe study. The father claim stays unresolved. Do not draw family scenes, rings, a campus, or Europe before/at the reveal.
4. Scene 19: the later message is a second account, not a literal invented chat transcript. Reveal the ideas in order; do not put `ASKING MATT` on screen before its spoken marker or physically bring Matt into the room.
5. Scene 20 is the first time the two blank paths divide. Scene 21 leaves the motive empty. Scenes 22–23 return to the same trio and move the tab from “extra place” to the narrator's side only on `favor`; keep `THOUGHT` readable as attribution.

## Accent and graphic coverage

The current score already maps 45 phrase events across all 23 scenes; it does not need more phrases by default. Most beats have one or two intentional anchors, with scene 08, 14, 17, and 19 carrying small sequential groups. Once the words exist, the script has frequent visual anchors without turning the narration into continuous subtitles. The actual VO does not exist yet, so exact entry frames, readable holds, and total duration are still unverified. Current estimated scene grid totals about 3:10 and must be retimed against real VO before any Short edit is locked.

Recommended graphic build:

- Treat each accent event as an independent transparent phrase/letter layer, positioned behind the named character/object for its entrance and in front for its readable stop only where the scene supports that depth. Do not ask imagegen to place these words inside the BG/master.
- The recurring orange tab (`motifs/orange_place_tab_v01.png`) and grouped two-story fork (`motifs/two_story_fork_v01.png`) are the only dedicated 008 graphics currently present. Both have transparency confirmed but still need edge/phone-scale/composite checks. The fork cannot yet diverge by independent layer motion.
- There are 45 accent events but only about 43 distinct phrase strings because `BREAK UP` and `THREE MONTHS` repeat. Current typography examples in 001 (`A_MESSAGE`, `MATCH`, `FOLLOW_THE_SIGNAL`, `FORMAT_MATTERS`, `REPORTED`, plus `D`, `A`, `T`, `0`, `1`, `6_FILES`) are reference examples, not a complete alphabet or a ready 008 phrase library. The channel has no confirmed OTF/TTF. New phrase graphics must be visually compared to the real paper letter PNGs; do not silently replace them with system type.
- Show no new letters in the image before their spoken marker. Scene 17's family-status phrases must be staged in exact order and need physical occlusion behind Matt until their words. Scene 19's future claim needs the same concealment. For scene20, the fork and `DIFFERENT STORIES` enter together on the marker, not earlier.
- The existing score appropriately avoids mandatory SFX; this art pass adds no audio assets. Grain is a continuous final compositing layer, not a baked background treatment.
