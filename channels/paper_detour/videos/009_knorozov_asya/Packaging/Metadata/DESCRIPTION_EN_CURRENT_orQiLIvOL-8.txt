Why is a cat featured on a monument to a scholar who helped unlock the Maya script? Yuri Knorozov’s Siamese companion, Asya, came into his life years after his breakthrough. A former student recalled that he even tried to credit her as a co-author of research on signaling—and the monument recreates a famous photograph of the two together.
