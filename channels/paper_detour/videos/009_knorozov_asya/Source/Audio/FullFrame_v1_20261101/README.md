# Audio sources for Paper Detour 009 rebuild

- `music_bed_56s_synth_stereo.wav` is reused from `channels/paper_detour/videos/001_radio_game/Source/Audio/paper_sound_design/`. It was generated in-project with mathematical oscillators and noise; it uses no external musical recording or samples. Its source README notes that subjective balance and transitions were not auditioned as a finished mix.
- Roger narration and its word alignment are in `Source/Voice/roger_v1_20261101/`. The voice was explicitly authorized for this episode. Exact script: `narration_en.txt`; voice `CwhRBWXzGAHq8TQ4Fs17`, model `eleven_v4`, default speed.
- The ChatGPT.com Extra High sound plan calls for one continuous quiet music bed, speech clearly in front, no animal sounds, sparse optional paper/pencil foley. No new foley was generated; this rebuild uses only narration plus the existing music bed.
- Timeline target: 32.00 s at 30 fps. Music bed starts at frame 0 and fades out across the last second; Roger ends at 30.56 s, leaving about 1.44 s of music.

The final level, fade and pronunciation still need listening review in Palmier.
