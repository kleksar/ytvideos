In Mexico, a monument shows a scientist holding a cat. His name was Yuri Knorozov. In 1952, he helped unlock Maya writing by showing that many of its signs could represent sounds. Other researchers built on that breakthrough for decades. Years later, his Siamese cat, Asya, became his companion. He even jokingly called her a co-author of a paper about communication and signals. Today, Mérida remembers a scholar and his cat.
