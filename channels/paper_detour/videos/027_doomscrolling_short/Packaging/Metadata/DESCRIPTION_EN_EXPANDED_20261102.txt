When a Nebraska school began coming apart in the 1888 blizzard, Minnie Freeman tied thirteen pupils together with twine she had taken from a boy who was playing with it in class.

Carrying the youngest, she led the children through the storm toward a farmhouse three-quarters of a mile away. All thirteen made it to safety.
