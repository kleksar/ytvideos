## Visual basis

The visual reference is the user-approved Paper Detour parallax frame `channels/paper_detour/videos/004_last_video/Storyboard/user_reference_parallax_20261101.png` and the verified cut-paper letter example `channels/paper_detour/videos/001_radio_game/Source/Images/accent_typography_v2/A_MESSAGE.png`. The school background, table, Minnie, pupil sheet, storm plain, farmhouse interior, farmhouse exterior, storm-line group and cold-open school edge were generated with the built-in image generator; local outputs were copied into this folder, leaving the originals in the generated-image cache. The pupil sheet was visually counted; the group was visually counted as twelve walking pupils plus one carried pupil. No composite, parallax movement, thread occlusion, full-length grain, transition, sound, or runtime check has been done.

The school/building and clothing are illustrative reconstructions. History Nebraska's marker calls the school a sod schoolhouse and says Minnie was still in her teens; these images do not claim an exact school, portrait, farmhouse, or costume. Sources and the board's remaining historical limits are recorded in `../../../../Storyboard/MINNIE_SCENES_AND_STORYBOARD_v07.md`.

## Prompt / generation notes

- **01_schoolroom_background_v01:** portrait 9:16 handmade paper-diorama clean background for a generic 1888 Nebraska one-room school; warm ochre/walnut interior, cold indigo snow through two square windows, chalkboard, hinged door, open central floor, no people, desks, props, text, or watermark.
- **02_desk_twine_foreground_v01:** transparent portrait paper cutout of one rural school desk and a small ball of cream-brown twine with a long continuous strand; no people or background; matched to the generated classroom and approved Paper Detour reference.
- **03_minnie_character_cutout_v01:** transparent full-figure, generic late-teen teacher, muted charcoal-teal 1880s dress and pinned hair, profile, paper layers and fibers; no exact portrait, scene, prop, or background.
- **04_pupils_thirteen_cutout_sheet_v01:** transparent sheet with exactly 13 full-body pupils, 6 in the upper row and 7 in the lower row; matte cut-paper figures in restrained period-inspired clothes, mostly side/back views, one small twine ball; no teacher or scene. The generator added a few book/bag details despite the no-extra-props direction; check these against research before reuse.
- **05_blizzard_farmhouse_background_v01:** layered, cold-blue paper snowfield with one distant farmhouse as a single background plate; no path, people, or foreground silhouettes.
- **06_farmhouse_interior_background_v01:** empty generic farmhouse room looking toward an open doorway into the blizzard; warm interior/cold exterior. This interior view is not used as proof that the pupils entered the house.
- **07_farmhouse_exterior_threshold_v01:** cold-blue exterior farmhouse wall/door cutout with open amber doorway and visible threshold; no people or route.
- **08_storm_line_group_cutout_v01:** transparent full-body group, 12 walking pupils plus the youngest carried by Minnie, one twine strand visible through the gaps. No body knots were requested; verify the resulting waist-level strand visually before use.
- **09_school_escape_storm_background_v01:** cropped generic sod-schoolhouse edge at left and open paper storm to the right, no roof visible and no people. Designed for the cold open with group 08 moving rightward away from the building.
