The roof has just blown away. A string connects thirteen children in the whiteout.

Nebraska, 1888. Moments earlier, the schoolhouse door blew in. Then its windows. Teacher Minnie Freeman feared the building would not last.

She remembered twine she’d taken from a boy for playing in class. She tied the children together. Then the roof blew off.

Carrying the youngest, Minnie led them toward a farmhouse through the blizzard. The snow erased the path; the string kept them together.

All thirteen reached shelter.

The thing she’d taken away brought them home.
