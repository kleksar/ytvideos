You open the news for one minute. Twenty minutes later, you’re still scrolling.

But the urge to check is older than your phone.

In seventeen ten, the English satirical periodical The Tatler described a man who rose before dawn to read political news.

He was a character in a satire. But the question feels familiar: what happened while I wasn’t looking?

Today, the updates never seem to end. So the next time you open the news for one minute, choose one question you want answered. Find a reliable source. Then decide when to check again.

The world will keep making news. Your life will keep happening, too.
