## Review notes and limits

- An independent still-image review confirmed the door → windows → distant farmhouse → twine memory → tied-and-waiting pupils → missing roof → journey → arrival order. It found no definite missing or duplicated child.
- The initial S09 was difficult to count at a glance because of storm and pupil overlap. Replacement S09 v02 has twelve walking pupils arranged in two clear rows. Independent still-image review counted six in each row plus the youngest carried by Minnie; it found no blocking issue.
- S02 and S04 school crowds are partly obscured by desks and framing; no definite absent child was found. S01, S08, and S10 provide the clearest group-count views.
- The generated settings, clothing, and schoolhouse are generic historical reconstructions. The S04 view from the school toward a visible farmhouse is not documented by the cited account.
- Static image inspection and sampled Palmier frame inspections are not a full 1× playback or a spoken-word audit. No export or publication is part of this pass.
