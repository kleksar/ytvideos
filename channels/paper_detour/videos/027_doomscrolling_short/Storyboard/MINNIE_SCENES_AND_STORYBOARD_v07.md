## Image and motion rule

Every shot has its own master composition, then registered full-canvas BG, whole MG and FG PNG layers. The shot changes are regular cuts with a new composition every 3–6 seconds. Inside each moving shot, use a small, steady left-to-right drift: initial 1080×1920 proposal BG +2 px/s, MG +8 px/s, FG +16 px/s. The deltas below are setup values, not verified playback. Keep contact items on a shared parent group: desk with ball and thread; feet with their ground shadows; roof with its shadow; people with their thread. Use the faster foreground only for independent paper snow or set edges. Preserve clean fill underneath moving objects and enough overscan to keep frame edges closed. Check first, middle and last frames plus every cut at 1×. Rescue-group movement within the story is separate from its subtle parallax.

Visual reference: user-approved Paper Detour parallax image (../../004_last_video/Storyboard/user_reference_parallax_20261101.png). Build tactile matte-paper sets, visible card edges, soft contact shadows, cool indigo/teal storm shadows and restrained ochre/amber light. The classroom, school, people and farmhouse are generic historical reconstructions, not claims to reproduce exact appearances. Add continuous soft moving film grain to the eventual full video; it is not baked into these stills. Do not use ordinary subtitles or a flat zoom in place of depth.

## Loop, type and count

The spoken loop is: **“All thirteen reached the farmhouse, connected by twine taken from one boy.”** The rescue must be clear before the fold. The cut back to the earlier blizzard is an editorial time shift; never animate the twine returning to the classroom or the pupils setting off from the house. The S01 thread should be visible immediately, but the exact number need not be instantly countable until the group is revealed.

The previous motion pass proposed three possible physical-paper accents: **THIRTEEN**, optional **¾ MILE**, and **WAIT**. Treat these as candidates, not captions. Use only existing verified letter assets or newly checked paper cutouts, with real occlusion and clear space. No word may cover a face, the group line or the story object.
