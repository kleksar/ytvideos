## Драматургическая петля

Первый кадр: roof already gone; thirteen silhouettes outside a torn schoolhouse are connected by a visible thread disappearing into white. The question is why the thread is there and whether anyone is lost. The timeline then deliberately cuts to moments before the roof failed. Story answers the question inside the same view, then ends with the confiscated string physically guiding us back to the children in the opening image. Do not show the children restrained: the thread is a loose, practical connection; show them holding it / attached around coats, not bound hands or bodies.
