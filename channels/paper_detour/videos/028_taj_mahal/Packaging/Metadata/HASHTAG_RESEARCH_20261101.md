- Channel/source: Paper Detour’s YouTube Studio Analytics → Trends.
- Search language: English query terms. Geography and exact time window were not displayed in the opened Trends view, so these are directional channel-side signals, not a global or date-bounded popularity claim.

## Channel Trends observations

Studio Trends query `Taj Mahal architecture` displayed:

- `taj mahal architecture documentary` — low demand.
- `taj mahal history in english` — medium demand.
- `how taj mahal was built animation` — low demand.
- `art and architecture of mughal empire` — medium demand.
- The “What viewers watch” panel included Taj Mahal engineering, Mughal masterpiece, and monument/history videos.

Studio Trends query `paper diorama` displayed medium demand for `how to make diorama with cardboard`, `how to make miniature diorama`, and `how to make paper toys with paper`; a paper-diorama craft video appeared in the “What viewers watch” panel. This supports `#PaperDiorama` as a truthful format label, but does not establish that it will drive discovery for a story Short.

## Platform rules and source limits

- YouTube Help says hashtags are clickable/searchable links; up to three description hashtags may appear by the title. It asks creators to use directly relevant hashtags and says more hashtags reduce relevance; over 60 are ignored. [YouTube Help — Find playlists & videos using hashtags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)
- YouTube’s current hashtag suggestions can reflect prior uploads and, in some countries, entered text. This episode has no upload draft yet, so its live Studio hashtag suggestions were not available for checking. [YouTube Help — Find playlists & videos using hashtags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)
- YouTube Help recommends a unique description, putting the main terms early, and reviewing display on mobile. [YouTube Help — Tips for video descriptions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en)
- Direct hashtag-result pages fetched by web search did not provide current result counts or usable trend data. A YouTube result from 2025 used `#TajMahal` and `#HistoryShorts`, but this is an example of usage, not a current popularity signal: [Decode Gurukul video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z9Ezk06_Zj8).

## Recommendation

Use three descriptive hashtags in the draft: `#TajMahal #PaperDiorama #HistoryShorts`. They cover the specific subject, the episode’s handmade visual format, and the short historical-story category. The Studio Trends query `art and architecture of mughal empire` also makes `#Architecture` a reasonable substitute for `#PaperDiorama` if the team prefers subject-category relevance over format identity.

No hashtag reach or performance prediction is claimed. Recheck YouTube’s suggested tags in the upload draft when it exists.
