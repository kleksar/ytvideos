Stone tools survived for more than a million years. Almost all the clothes disappeared.

Follow a tiny ancient sandal into a journey through Western European clothing: Roman tunics, medieval layers, Amsterdam in 1559, extravagant court outfits, enormous crinolines, and the strikingly different dresses of the 1920s.

One riverbank connects the eras in a richly detailed paper-diorama world. And at the end, the question turns toward us: what will people make of the clothes we leave behind?

Which outfit would you wear today?

Historical scenes are illustrative recreations. The future museum is imagined.
