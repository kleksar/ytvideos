## Locked project context

- Channel: Paper Detour.
- Subject: changes in clothing silhouettes across Western European history, from early Homo to the 2020s.
- Picture-only scene sequence. No narration or sound effects. Period-matched background music is a later production step; no audio has been generated.
- Visual format: one finished, full-frame physical paper/card diorama per scene; static compositions joined by direct cuts. No separate layers, parallax, or moving scene parts.
- Recurring fictional composite location: river bend from lower-left toward center and a pale far-bank rock with a triangular notch. This is a continuity device, not a claim that every period depicts one archaeological site.
- Dates, objects, and clothing are treated cautiously; visual reconstructions are illustrative and do not imply a single universal outfit or undocumented archaeological scene.
