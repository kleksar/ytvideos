# Superseded Palmier readback — v02

These inspection frames were captured after cue timing edits but before reapplying the shared optical-position normalization. During the intermediate state, position values were incorrectly set to `x=0.5` even though Palmier interprets `position` as the PNG's normalized top-left corner; the contact sheet therefore shows cues clipped at the right edge.

The position keys were recalculated and the helper post-readback passed. Use [`timeline_reads_v03/`](../timeline_reads_v03) and [`PALMIER_STATE_en_v03.json`](../../PALMIER_STATE_en_v03.json) for the verified current state. This folder is retained only to record the superseded intermediate inspection.
