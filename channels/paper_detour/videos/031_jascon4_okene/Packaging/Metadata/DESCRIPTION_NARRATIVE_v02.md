Harrison Okene was the cook aboard the tugboat Jascon-4 when it sank off the coast of Nigeria on 26 May 2013, at a depth of about 30 metres. Trapped inside the wreck, he survived for more than 60 hours in an air pocket.

A diver sent to recover bodies reached for a hand — and it grabbed back. Okene was the only survivor among the 12 people aboard; the other 11 crew members died. Divers brought him to the surface in a diving bell before transferring him to a decompression chamber.
