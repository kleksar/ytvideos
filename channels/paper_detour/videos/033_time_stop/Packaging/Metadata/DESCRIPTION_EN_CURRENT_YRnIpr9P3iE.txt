What if time stopped for everything except you?

In this thought experiment, light, sound, air, and even a raindrop inches from your face are frozen. You can't see, hear, move through the air, or breathe.

Five seconds later, time resumes. Nothing has changed, except you're now five seconds older than everyone else.
