## Fact-check consultation

- **Chat:** [Аудит утверждений о зебрах](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-p-6ae147d3975481919b5bfda6935777d0-yt-video/c/6ae7d1b8-2920-83eb-881e-c5c7fd484203).
- **Task:** audit the exact draft against primary research only, focusing on the cow result, what photographs measured, the separate zebra observations, and the 2023 mechanism paper.
- Findings: the cow study used six Japanese Black cows across three coat conditions; photographs after 30-minute observations counted visible biting flies on the body and legs. The aggregate count for black-and-white-striped cows was almost half that of either control. This was not a count of bites or a video record of the landing.
- The zebra study is separate: horseflies approached zebras faster, and successful landings were less frequent than on horses. Brief touches and full landings are distinct measures; do not claim that every fly failed to land.
- A 2023 study supports strong contrast and small dark patches as part of the explanation. The exact mechanism remains unresolved, so the script says the full answer is not clear yet instead of saying no mechanism has been studied.
