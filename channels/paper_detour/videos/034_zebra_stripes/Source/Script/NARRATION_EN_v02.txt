Can paint stop a fly? Imagine it seconds from landing as the cow gets zebra stripes. Six cows tried three looks: zebra stripes, black stripes, or no paint. Photos showed about half as many biting flies on zebra-striped cows as on black-striped or unpainted cows. But the photos couldn’t show what happened just before landing. A separate study found horseflies rushed toward zebras, but landed less often than on horses. Maybe stripes don’t stop the approach; they change the landing. The full answer isn’t clear yet. Another fly is already coming in to land.
