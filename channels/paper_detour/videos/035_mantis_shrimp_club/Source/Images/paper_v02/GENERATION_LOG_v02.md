## Motion-safety inspection

The initial motion review showed cropped animal edges in S01/S04/S08 and over-tight limb shots in S05/S06. The early source-PNG acceptance did not include the actual camera/depth envelope, so its static acceptance did not prove motion safety. Corrective source set v02 was rendered offline before import, using the actual per-scene camera modes, whole-image camera path, `depth_zoom=1.0`, and new `framing-safe` preset. Five points (first, quarter, middle, three-quarter, last visible frame) were extracted and inspected for all nine rendered scenes. S04 v02 failed this check and was replaced by S04 v03; its five-point motion contact sheet was inspected before import. Post-import motion preview samples include five positions per scene and five actual frames per cue (entrance, hold, end and neighboring states).

- Camera modes by scene: S01 push-in; S02 push-in; S03 pan-right; S04 push-in; S05 pull-out; S06 pull-out; S07 pan-left; S08 pan-right; S09 push-in.
- `framing-safe` parameters: parallax offset 0.12, height 0.20, camera deviation factor 0.35; depth zoom is 1.0 for each scene. This is a low-amplitude option, not a substitute for scene-specific framing review.
- Selected v02/v03 PNGs were reviewed individually at full-frame. All nine rendered scene samples keep the story focal forms inside the screen at the inspected frames. The review remains sampled: it is not a frame-by-frame proof of every interpolated depth-warp frame.
- Motion export: `Edit/Previews/Paper Detour 035 Motion Safe Review v02.mp4`. H.264 1080×1920/30 fps, AAC mono 44.1 kHz, 44.166667 s; full decode passed. Five-point scene and cue sheets: `Edit/ImageMotion/79fc35ac5f5e9a97be5e/qa/export/`.
- Full 1× listening, spoken sync, final open-loop seam, Shorts UI safe-area, final delivery QA, and independent review remain unverified. This is a review preview, not a final deliverable.
