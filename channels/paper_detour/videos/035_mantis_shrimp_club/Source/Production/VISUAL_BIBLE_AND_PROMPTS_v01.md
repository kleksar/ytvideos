## Visual Bible

- Vertical 9:16 deliverables; one complete, opaque, static handmade paper/card diorama per scene; each image is a standalone composition.
- Matte paper/card with visible cut edges, layered thickness, slight handmade irregularity, paper fibers, realistic contact shadows and coherent miniature perspective. No separable animation layers.
- Consistent cinematic light across scenes: warm amber edge/key light against cool, muted blue shadows. Keep dark areas readable.
- Use neutral paper tones for animal surfaces. These are craft choices, not claims about the coloration of ancient animals or one named species.
- Render recognizable stomatopod / mantis-shrimp body organization, including stalked eyes and articulated hunting limbs connected to the body. Avoid ordinary shrimp anatomy and detached appendages.
- Early reconstructed forms show several broadly similar pairs of hunting limbs. Later scenes distinguish the front pair, then show two separate specialized forms. No image presents a modern spearer as the ancestor of a modern smasher.
- Apply only the material, depth, craftsmanship and light from the two approved references in `channels/paper_detour/brand/SCENE_IMAGE_REFERENCES.md`: `channels/paper_detour/videos/004_last_video/Storyboard/user_reference_parallax_20261101.png` and `channels/paper_detour/videos/033_time_stop/Source/Images/paper_v01/S01_v01.png`.
