# Visual Bible v02 — Paper Detour Short 035

**Status:** image direction for storyboard v02. All ten shots are new compositions in `Source/Images/paper_v03/`; no prior scene PNG was reused. S01_v03 is the opening master and must be reused byte-for-byte at the ending loop.

## Art direction

- Full-frame opaque portrait 9:16 handmade paper/card diorama for each direct-cut scene; static scene art. Visible paper fibers, hand-cut layered edges, folds, fine surface variation, miniature depth, natural contact shadows, physical directional light.
- Atmospheric underwater fantasy stage: layered stone-like paper shelves, grotto arches, distant blue-green haze, shafts of aqua/amber light, a few suspended paper motes. This is artistic staging, not a statement about the exact habitat of any particular species or ancient form.
- Use varied blue, teal, jade, indigo, violet, parchment, copper, ochre and muted rose. Maintain one cinematic cool/warm family while varying scene-specific light, camera distance and background geometry. Avoid flat vector design, smooth CGI, a single-color void or repetitive identical layouts.
- Animal tones are visibly constructed colored paper, not claims about natural coloration. Show stomatopod organization: stalked eyes, segmented body, articulated attached limbs. Generalized early forms retain several broadly similar hunting limb pairs. Only the modern spearer has the slender spined terminal section; only the modern smasher has the compact thickened striking end. The whole limb never becomes a club.
- S04/S08 present two distinct specialists, not one changing into the other. S09 depicts a plain shared early limb and returns to the exact S01 master.
- No baked text, diagrams, researchers, fossils, prey, action, extra creatures, plants or identifiable coral. Accents remain separate paper PNG overlays.

## Generation and acceptance control

Every image request used the built-in image generator, requested maximum available quality/native detail and portrait 4K UHD target 2160×3840, and named the exact references below. Actual returned files measure 941×1672 RGB, so they are **not native 4K**; the accepted video canvas remains 1080×1920 and no upscale claim is made. Each final PNG was viewed individually in the generation response and together in `Source/Images/paper_v03/CONTACT_SHEET_v03.png`. They are a new visual candidate set, not yet motion-safe or approved for Palmier import until the camera-envelope gate and actual composites pass.

## Motion-safe guard

Protect animal identity, eyes, limb/weapon tip, attachment joints, repeated limb row and separation between paired animals. Require the actual PNG to pass the accepted scene's camera and depth displacement at all path extrema before import. Scene envelope and per-scene camera motion must be measured and recorded; prompt instructions or a full-frame still alone do not establish safety. Any image failing the real envelope is regenerated or receives a scene-specific reduced motion plan before import.
