## Findings

- **Three regions — supported, Fig. 1E–F.** The study names impact, periodic, and striated regions; these are identified in transverse cross-sections.
- **CT wording — refine to micro-CT and distinguish the evidence, Fig. 1D–F.** The paper specifically says whole-body microcomputed tomography; Fig. 1D shows the dactyl and propodus within the appendage, while Fig. 1E–F identifies the three regions in cross-section. v02 separates these observations.
- **Hardness/mineral gradient — supported with a surface caveat, Fig. 2.** Nanoindentation and EDS show properties/composition varying from impact surface toward interior; the outermost impact surface is particularly hard and mineral-rich. Avoid implying the entire impact region is uniform, because it includes a transition zone with changing mechanical properties.
- **Helicoidal chitin and crack paths — supported with caveat, Fig. 4F–H and discussion.** Damaged cross-section micrographs show cracks nesting between chitin fibers; the authors model the fracture path as helicoidal. They report observed microcracks crossing the helicoid and arresting within a few layers (Fig. 4C). This is not time-lapse footage of crack motion.
- **Steel-target strike — needs explicit model label, Fig. 5A–C.** The steel target and impact are in dynamic finite-element simulations, not a filmed physical strike by these researchers.
- **Scale of the model — clarify, discussion after Fig. 5.** The paper says its finite-element simulations do not take the complex microstructural features into account (lines 409–416 of the source PDF). v02 says the model shows stresses through the club’s overall shape and explicitly states it did not model the tiny fiber layers.
- **Damage-resistance conclusion — supported with calibrated wording, Figs. 4–5 and conclusion.** The paper argues the architecture hinders catastrophic crack propagation and minimizes internal damage; “helps explain how” appropriately avoids claiming invulnerability.
- **Evolutionary origin limit — fair editorial caveat.** The paper studies current structure and mechanics of clubs from *Odontodactylus scyllarus*; it does not reconstruct the trait’s evolutionary origin. Say “helps explain” rather than implying a complete explanation.

## Versioning

v02 corrects “CT” to “micro-CT,” distinguishes overall-scale impact simulation from the tiny fiber layers, and changes “it explains” to “it helps explain.” v01 is retained as provenance.
