## Visual continuity and framing

A single handmade paper/card miniature world uses layered blue, teal, indigo, jade, ochre, copper and muted violet settings, with scene-specific light and varied scale. Environments are artistic underwater atmosphere, not claims about exact habitat or coloration. All animal bodies use visible crafted-paper construction, fibers, cut edges, folds and physical shadows. No words are baked into scene art; captions remain separate PNGs.

Before import, evaluate the actual selected PNGs against the scene-specific pan/zoom/depth envelope and all protected story details. Do not rely on a generic percentage. Scene-specific motion modes and the measured envelope belong in the image-motion manifest. Review intermediate path extrema and every cue composite; selected endpoint stills or prompt language alone do not prove fit.
