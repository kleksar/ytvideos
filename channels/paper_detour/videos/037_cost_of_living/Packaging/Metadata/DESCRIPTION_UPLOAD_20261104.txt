Could one wage cover a family's basic needs?

Follow a London labourer's pay against the cost of food, fuel, soap, light and other necessities across several centuries. When daily wages rose, the price of a year's basics did not always become easier to afford.

The film compares daily wages with a reconstructed household basket. A wage-day is an equivalent price, not a record of days actually worked; families' real incomes and expenses varied.
