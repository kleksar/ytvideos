## Coordinator calculations and guardrails

- For a separately labeled **model-implied wage-day equivalent**, the ratio can be inverted against the 250 assumed annual wage-days: 250 ÷ 0.75 ≈ 333; 250 ÷ 1.42 ≈ 176; 250 ÷ 1.16 ≈ 216; 250 ÷ 2.15 ≈ 116. This arithmetic does **not** say that an actual worker or family worked that many days. The script may omit these derived numbers if they distract from the ratio or overstate precision.
- Avoid an exact direct comparison to present-day prices or a single modern family's cost of living. The source series covers selected English/London historical wage-price estimates and its own modeled basket.
- Do not infer the cause of the 1850–1899 ratio from this comparison alone.
- Historical scenes are visual reconstructions, never documentary photographs; do not invent named workers, private thoughts, or witnessed dialogue.
