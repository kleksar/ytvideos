## Visual contract

- Long-form 16:9. Every `Sxx` is one complete, still, full-frame handmade paper/card diorama. Each shot changes by a direct cut; no layer motion, parallax, or animated diorama pieces.
- Style references to attach as real image inputs on generation: `channels/paper_detour/Shared/References/Paper/paper004_user_38.png` (primary material/light/depth reference) and `channels/paper_detour/videos/033_time_stop/Source/Images/paper_v01/S01_v01.png` (secondary character/environment reference). Do not copy their characters or locations.
- Target visible material: cut and folded card, tactile paper fibers, layered edges, real contact shadows, motivated physical light. Avoid photorealistic scenery, slick digital vector charts, floating objects, blank visible pages, and invented readable historical text.
- Palette: warm paper, ink-dark blue and restrained amber/silver light. Keep the same annual-needs basket, plain silver wage discs and four-part budget motif visually consistent; they are teaching props, not claimed surviving objects.
- Anonymous figures illustrate a model, never one person's continuous biography. No invented names, dialogue, motives or punishments. Use era-specific dress only after checking actual references; several close-up compositions deliberately avoid unsupported costume detail.
- Accent words below are candidates for a later captions decision, not baked into scene PNGs. No continuous subtitles. Historical text on the law prop remains illegible; the year `1351` is a separate editorial accent.
- Paper scene duration standard: 4–6 seconds. This storyboard does not assign exact seconds. Align each composition to the actual narration before building the timeline; do not use Palmier’s 5-second image default as an editorial decision. Accent triggers require a separate timing pass.
