## Pairwise distinction

- **A vs B:** A promises motive and sacrifice; B promises a near-failed search during the raid.
- **A vs C:** A follows the retainers’ decision; C follows the criminal judgment and later memory.
- **B vs C:** B is a single-night suspense beat; C is the story’s long-term moral and cultural afterlife.
