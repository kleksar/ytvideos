## Search and rules check

On 3 November 2026, YouTube-focused web searches checked exact or close spellings for `#47Ronin`, `#Chushingura`, `#JapaneseHistory`, `#Samurai`, and `#EdoPeriod`. The results confirmed an English YouTube description using `#Japanesehistory`; a YouTube hashtag-page fetch for `47ronin` returned only generic page chrome, and other pages were unavailable or generic. Search also surfaced a 47 Ronin fantasy-film clip, demonstrating ambiguity around the exact name. Studio Trends and Google Trends were not checked. Search demand and trend status remain unknown; absence from these search snippets is not evidence that a hashtag is unused.

Observed use: [Japanese History / YouTube High School](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aIoLl4eoamw) uses `#Japanesehistory` in its description. Content relevance for the 47 Ronin title is also apparent in [EPIC's 47 Ronin clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=peWahY37UqI), though that is the unrelated fantasy feature and does not establish exact hashtag usage.

YouTube's current help says hashtags must relate directly to the video and contain no spaces; more than 60 causes all hashtags for that video to be ignored. Seven unique tags are proposed here; this is not a claim that the count improves recommendations. See [YouTube Help — hashtags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en).
