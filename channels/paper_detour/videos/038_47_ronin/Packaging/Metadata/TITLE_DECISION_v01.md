## Why this wording

The current narration says that the shogunate sentenced the forty-six surviving participants and that later generations remembered the act as loyalty. The wording “сочли преступлением” describes the state's judgment without treating the rōnin's motive as settled or implying that all forty-seven died. It keeps the main subject at the beginning and does not claim the historical cause of Asano's attack is known.

The supplied narration's opening title, `47 ронинов. Месть, за которую пришлось умереть`, is preserved in the script. This YouTube title is a separate candidate refined to make the legal tension explicit. A factual alternative, `47 ронинов: месть, за которую погибли 46`, is more explicit but gives away a later count and foregrounds the outcome. It is not selected.
