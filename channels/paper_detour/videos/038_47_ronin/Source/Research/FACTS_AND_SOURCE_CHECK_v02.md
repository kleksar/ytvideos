# Paper Detour 038 — Akō Incident: factual basis v02

Status: DRAFT, 2 November 2026. This revision follows the user's supplied editorial review. It sharpens Ōishi's documented choices, corrects participant/sentence counts, and avoids the unsupported “one sudden pivot when hope vanished” version.

## Story promise

Ōishi leads a divided group of lordless retainers. They must decide when to act, what they still hope to preserve, and whether private revenge can be reconciled with the shogunate's law. Their revenge succeeds; 46 are sentenced to seppuku. The figure remembered as “the Forty-Seven Rōnin” includes one member whose participation in the raid is disputed.

## Claims and limits

| ID | Claim | Evidence / boundary | Planned use |
|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | In 1701, Asano Naganori attacked Kira Yoshinaka in Edo Castle as court envoys were being received. | Columbia University ExEAS gives the context and says the cause of Asano's anger is unclear. His attack lightly wounded Kira before Asano was restrained. | Hook + setup. Do not invent the popular insult or failed-bribe explanation. |
| F02 | The shogunate ordered Asano to perform seppuku and confiscated the Akō domain the same day; his retainers became rōnin. | ExEAS and Bitō. Avoid describing a modern criminal trial. | First turning point. |
| F03 | Retainers disagreed over resistance, following their lord in death, surrender, petition, and revenge. Ōishi helped resolve these choices over time. | Bitō documents the castle-siege and junshi proposals, Oishi's decision to surrender the castle, his attention to the Asano brother's situation, and efforts to restore the family. No single-minded group or sudden conversion should be implied. | Character arc through choices. |
| F04 | An account of the retainer Horibe says a planned attack was set aside after reports that the Uesugi family could protect Kira. | Bitō recounts this as Horibe's account, not a universal fact about what every retainer knew. Its narrative use is a reported risk that helps explain why a premature attack was rejected. | Specific early obstacle; attribute only in internal ledger. |
| F05 | The retainers continued to seek reconsideration/restoration while planning the vendetta; Bitō warns that revenge and restoration were not mutually exclusive options and that Oishi's private expectations are not fully recoverable. | Bitō, especially pp. 160–165. | F06 | On the fourteenth day of the twelfth month, Genroku 15, Oishi and forty-five others raided Kira's residence, took his head to Sengakuji, and surrendered. This corresponds to 30 January 1703 Gregorian. | Bitō. One member of the Forty-Seven group, Terasaka, was absent from the forty-six who were taken into custody; why he disappeared/was dismissed remains disputed. | Climax and responsibility; 46 participants in this account, no invented raid choreography. |

## Sources

- Bitō Masahide, [“The Akō Incident, 1701–1703”](https://www.columbia.edu/~hds2/pdf/2003b_Bito_Ako_Incident.pdf), translated by Henry D. Smith II, *Monumenta Nipponica* 58:2. Used for contemporary accounts, motive limits, strategic debates, Kira protection rumor as told by Horibe, revenge/house-restoration relationship, raid and count caveat.
- Columbia University ExEAS, [“Law and Society: The Story of the 47 Rōnin”](https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/special/japan_1450_47ronin.htm). Educational historical summary; confirms Asano was immediately restrained and lightly wounded Kira; 46 entered the raid/account and 46 were sentenced.
- Henry D. Smith II, [“Rethinking the Story of the 47 Rōnin”](https://www.columbia.edu/~hds2/47ronin/47ronin_rev.htm), Columbia University. Dates the debate at seven weeks and gives March 20, 1703 (Gregorian) for the sentence/execution order.
- John A. Tucker, [The Forty-Seven Rōnin: The Vendetta in History](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/fortyseven-ronin/3B692DF6B1255F55DF6A3B568DFD1B1C) (Cambridge University Press; preview). Used to explain the distinction between the 47 named in a document, 46 detained/sentenced, and disputed accounts of Terasaka.
- Akō Castle Digital Museum, [“Akō: The Birthplace of Chūshingura”](https://ako-castle.jp/en/learn/the-birthplace-of-chushingura/), maintained by Akō City. Local chronology and later dramatic tradition; its event dates use the Japanese lunisolar calendar.

## Script boundaries

- Give no invented cause for Asano's attack, dialogue beyond what the ledger quotes, hidden feelings, disguises, passwords, fight choreography, snow, exact timing of the raid, or death count among Kira's household.
- Present Ōishi's choices as sequence and result. Do not claim that every retainer shared one motive or that the restoration hope vanished in one knowable instant.
- State “Ōishi and forty-five others” at the raid and “forty-six were sentenced.” Keep “Forty-Seven Rōnin” as the later group name, with the disputed role of Terasaka explained succinctly.
- The moral dilemma is grounded in contemporary deliberation over vengeance, public order, and loyalty; do not recast it as a simple universal bushidō rule.
