# Paper Detour 038 — Akō Incident: factual basis v05

Status: DRAFT, 2 November 2026. Focused revision after the user's third story assessment. It explains the honor motive for those pressing for revenge, removes the Horibe/Uesugi branch from narration while retaining it in the research record, and gives the raid a short event sequence. No exact hiding place or combat choreography is asserted.

## Script boundaries

- Clarify the honor motive only for the retainers who pressed for revenge; do not attribute one belief to all 47.
- Keep Horibe/Uesugi details in research notes, not narration. They are accurate but not required to understand the core conflict.
- Describe the search and discovery generically. Do not specify the room, hiding place, quoted exchange, weapons, wounds, casualties, snow or movement order.
- Keep the resolution as a historical ruling amid divided judgments, not a universal statement that one side was morally correct.
