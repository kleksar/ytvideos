## Editorial consequence for the story

The verified turn is a local, Iran-born volunteer patrolling Pattaya during the shore leave of U.S. sailors returning from a war involving Iran. The reporting does **not** establish that he was helping police monitor the sailors or sex workers. Build the question around why he was on that patrol and let his residency and own words answer it; do not promise a surveillance reveal that the source does not contain.
