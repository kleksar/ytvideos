# Narration EN v01 — USS Abraham Lincoln / Pattaya

**State:** DRAFT; factual/editorial review led to a tighter version in v02.

Two hundred sixty-four days at sea. Then the USS Abraham Lincoln reached Thailand, and Pattaya expected a rush that never quite came.

With about 5,000 sailors and Marines aboard, the carrier was nearly nine months into deployment. Families reported food disruptions and broken toilets; the Navy disputed claims of poor conditions.

At Laem Chabang, the ship made its first full port visit. Shore leave began.

Before arrival, Thai police arrested 25 people in a crackdown on public prostitution. Officials denied any connection to the Americans. Yet patrols increased, and the US military imposed restrictions ashore.

Then the anticlimax: a Pattaya bar owner said not one Lincoln sailor visited her bar that first night. The hype, she said, was bigger than reality.
