At Pattaya, the USS Abraham Lincoln’s crew finally reached shore after nine months without a full port visit. About 5,000 sailors and Marines were aboard when the carrier docked at Laem Chabang, a short drive away. Families had reported food shortages and broken toilets; the Navy acknowledged supply problems but disputed broader claims of poor conditions.

Before the carrier arrived, Thai authorities detained 25 people over suspected public solicitation. Pattaya’s mayor said the operation wasn’t specifically linked to the US visit. Police separately increased patrols, while the Navy set rules for its crew ashore.

Some expected a rush. But one go-go bar owner said she saw no apparent Lincoln customers on the first night. The hype was bigger than reality, she said.
