Imagine hearing on television that you could finally travel to the other side of Berlin. Would you go to the border that night?

On November 9, 1989, people did exactly that. They gathered at the checkpoints, asked to cross, and waited as the guards tried to understand their orders. Their determination helped turn an announcement into an open passage.
