## Pairwise distinction result before generation

- A↔B: title promises a radio reply vs. an Anderson search action; image is a close microphone vs. an exterior freighter and changing water.
- A↔C: title promises known words vs. two competing explanations; image is a single microphone vs. paired conceptual cutaways.
- B↔C: title promises what the search vessel did vs. what remains unknown about the loss; image is an exterior turning freighter vs. two interior models.
