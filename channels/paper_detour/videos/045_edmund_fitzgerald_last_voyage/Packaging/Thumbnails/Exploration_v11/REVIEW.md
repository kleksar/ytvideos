# Thumbnail review — v11

Editorial review of three actual 1672×941 masters and 320×180 local previews: lettering is complete and legible in the local reduction. A shows storm, freighter and radio, with NO SOS HEARD clarifying that no distress call was heard; B shows an upright bow and overturned stern on the bed with FOUND IN TWO PIECES; C shows a ship departing a sheltered shore into a storm with BACK INTO THE STORM. B is very close in visual composition to the existing channel reference 05; C shares a ship-in-storm motif with the existing cover 03, though its safe shore and wake express the decision to return. These are new generated versions, not measured performance improvements. Small background details and period radio stylization are artistic reconstruction. No live card or physical phone inspection is claimed.

A/B title promises: A disappearance with no heard distress call; B discovery of the two-piece wreck; C the Anderson's return. Images respectively show radio/absence, found wreck and ship returning toward the storm; they are distinct without text. Promises match film. Earlier drafts 01 and NO CALL HEARD were rejected because they overstate what was heard.
