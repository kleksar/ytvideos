## Pre-generation sequence review
Cargo cutaway → geographical relief → immersive deck → human interior → deliberate exterior manoeuvre → underwater aftermath.
Different scale, camera position, focal object and use of light for every composition. Cargo repetition explains mass; storm repetition conveys exposed deck length; neither simply repeats a distant vessel. Text is present when it anchors amount, place, words, vessel or depth; P03 relies on the physical scene alone. No precise route, radio-log imitation, invented telemetry or depiction of the unknown sinking instant.
These six samples are separated narrative moments, not six consecutive 4–6s shots. Timing/subtitle alignment is unassigned. Source review is full frame; tentative later central zoom <=1.04, no pan; actual endpoints and composites must be checked after any integration. No reserved subtitle band.
