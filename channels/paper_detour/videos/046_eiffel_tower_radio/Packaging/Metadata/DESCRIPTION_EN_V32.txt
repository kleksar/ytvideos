What did the Eiffel Tower have to do with French military radio?

Built for the 1889 World's Fair, the Eiffel Tower later carried antennas used for French military radio. French forts in the east had relied on weather-sensitive tethered balloons to raise antennas. In 1909, a permanent military radio station was installed underground beneath the Champ de Mars park, while its antennas stood high on the tower.
