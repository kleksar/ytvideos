## Description

The Eiffel Tower opened for the 1889 World's Fair. The French army later found a use for its height. At eastern forts, raising antennas with tethered balloons depended on the weather. In Paris, engineers tested wireless signals from the tower. By 1909, the military had a permanent radio station beneath the Champ de Mars, with its antennas high above on the tower.
