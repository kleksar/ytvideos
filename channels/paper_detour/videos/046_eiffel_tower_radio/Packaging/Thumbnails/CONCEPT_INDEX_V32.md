# Paper Detour 046 · numbered cover concept index v32

Each uses one complete handmade-paper stage with generated lettering inside the image. The main subject and complete hook stay inside the central 2:3 crop of a 9:16 portrait.

1. **Unexpected station location** — Stranger's question: why is the radio room under the park while the antennas sit high on the tower? Observable contrast: iconic tower above ground, warmly lit separate station beneath the Champ de Mars. Principal prop: tower-top antenna paired with underground operator's room. Composition: tall cross-section, tower to the right, separate underground room lower left under park ground; no cable line and no implication that station is in the tower. Exact hook: **ANTENNAS ON TOP**. Title: **The Eiffel Tower's Radio Station Was Underground**.
2. **Balloon-lifted antenna** — Stranger's question: why is a balloon hauling an antenna above a fort? Observable action: tether pulled taut as a balloon lifts a long antenna in blustery weather. Principal prop: large tethered balloon and antenna above a small eastern French fort. Composition: balloon dominates upper half, fort anchors bottom; no second tower or invented battle. Exact hook: **THE ANTENNA LIFTS**. Title: **French Forts Once Used Balloons to Raise Antennas**.
3. **Fair landmark to military radio** — Stranger's question: how did a fairground landmark acquire a radio role? Observable contrast: 1889 fair decorations at the tower's base and an antenna mounted at its summit. Principal prop: unmistakable Eiffel Tower silhouette. Composition: a single full-height tower against a dark Paris evening sky; paper fair pennants near base; small antenna at summit and two restrained cut-paper signal arcs; no split screen, cables, or battlefield. Exact hook: **FAIR TO RADIO**. Title: **How France Used the Eiffel Tower for Military Radio**.
