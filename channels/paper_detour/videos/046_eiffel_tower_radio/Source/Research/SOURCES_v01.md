# Eiffel Tower story — evidence and limits

Collected 2026-11-05 by the coordinator. This is internal research, not a public source list. Story v01 is a draft for user review.

| Claim used | Evidence | Limit / editorial action |
|---|---|---|
| Exhibition of 1889; tower about 300m, then world's highest; artist opposition and exhibition success | [Official tower controversy history](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/news/history-and-culture/when-eiffel-tower-was-subject-controversy); [National Archives press release, 2024, p1](https://s3.fr-par.scw.cloud/an-p0-bucket/an-public/files/Footer/Espace%20presse/2024/DOC/202409_CP_Exposition_tour-Eiffel_Archives-nationales.pdf?VersionId=1759406029836009) | Opposition was not unanimous Parisian hatred. No invented exchanges. |
| Original 20-year exploitation right after the exhibition, from 1 Jan 1890; scientific rooms anticipated | [1887 convention, original Article 8 and 11 transcription with scan](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Convention_8_janvier_1887.djvu/5); [National Archives exhibition](https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/evenements/1887-la-tour-eiffel-la-convention-de-construction) | Article 11 says city ownership after the exhibition, while Eiffel retained enjoyment/exploitation. Simplified official articles that say ownership first passes in 1910 must not override the contract. No obligation to demolish was established by the inspected Article 11. No signed demolition order claimed. |
| Early science and meteorology; height as experimental resource | [Official science history](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/the-monument/eiffel-tower-and-science); [Tower as laboratory](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/news/130-years/how-eiffel-tower-was-science-lab); Archives press release p1 | Science was contemplated from the start, not invented in a last-minute rescue. |
| 5 Nov 1898, Ducretet Tower–Panthéon wireless Morse, 4km | [Official radio preservation history](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/news/130-years/why-was-eiffel-tower-kept); official science history; Archives press release (credits Ducretet and Ernest Roger) | Ducretet named as actor, no sole-inventor claim; no imaginary spoken dialogue. |
| City considered demolition in 1903 while planning Champ-de-Mars; chose to retain | Official controversy history, preservation section | Existing decision retained in the story; no fictitious countdown to an unavoidable 1910 demolition. Direct municipal deliberation not inspected. |
| Eiffel offered tower and funding to Ferrié in 1903; wooden station by south pillar; height and antenna | Official radio preservation/science histories, Archives press release p1 | No assertion that Eiffel alone invented wireless communication or that height alone provides the result. |
| Radio extended to eastern France and North Africa | Official radio preservation history, Archives press release p1 | Dates differ: tower history gives eastern forts in 1904 and Bizerte later; Archives press release gives eastern France in 1906 and Algerian coast in 1908. Story omits precise dates and the disputed 6000km milestone. Bizerte is in Tunisia; one tower laboratory article wrongly labels it Algeria, so use the dedicated radio history's location. |
| Permanent underground station in 1909; radio/science supported survival and extensions | Official radio/science histories; National Archives exhibition/press release | Dedicated tower pages say a 70-year renewal from 1910. [1908 yearbook of Société de la Tour Eiffel, DFIH primary-document OCR](https://dfih.fr/issuers/2789/yearbook-ocr?source=annuaire_cac_1908) records a 1 Dec 1906 agreement adding five years from 1910. Exact succession of legal extensions unresolved; omit the 70-year claim and any scene of a sudden 1910 rescue/signature. Archives confirms renewals, scientific/strategic role and continued operation. |
| Regular radio broadcasts from 1921 | Official radio preservation history; [Radio France collection history](https://www.radiofrance.com/de-la-tsf-aux-debuts-de-la-radiodiffusion) | Brief factual epilogue. No broad claim about radio alone winning a war or revealing spies. |
