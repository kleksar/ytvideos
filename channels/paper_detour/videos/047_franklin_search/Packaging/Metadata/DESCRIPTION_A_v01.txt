In 1845, HMS Erebus and HMS Terror sailed into the Arctic under the command of Sir John Franklin. Both ships were eventually abandoned, and for generations their final resting places remained unknown.

This is the story of the search for Franklin’s ships: the note left by the expedition, Inuit knowledge and accounts, years of underwater searches, and two discoveries made by following very different leads. From a clue on a remote shore to an unexpected detour into Terror Bay, the search finally located Erebus in 2014 and Terror in 2016.
