A rusted piece of metal on an Arctic shore helped change the direction of a search for one of history’s most elusive shipwrecks. The object did not reveal a ship’s location by itself—but it gave the search team a reason to look closer.

Follow the long search for HMS Erebus and HMS Terror, the ships of John Franklin’s lost expedition. Inuit accounts, historical records, archaeological work and sonar surveys all shaped a search that eventually found the two wrecks in different places, two years apart.
