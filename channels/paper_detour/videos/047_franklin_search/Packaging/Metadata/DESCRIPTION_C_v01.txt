A research vessel was heading north when a local man’s account prompted its crew to investigate a different bay. What they found beneath the water brought the search for HMS Terror close to its end.

Travel back through the disappearance of John Franklin’s Arctic expedition and the long search for its two ships. The story follows the Inuit accounts and modern investigations that led to HMS Erebus in 2014 and, after an unexpected detour, HMS Terror in 2016.
