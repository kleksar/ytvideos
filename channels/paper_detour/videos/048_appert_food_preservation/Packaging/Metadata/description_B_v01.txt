What do a glass vessel, a cork and a hot water bath have in common? They were central to Nicolas Appert’s search for a way to preserve food without relying only on salt, sugar or drying.

This is the story of a French pastry chef’s experiments with meat, broth, milk, vegetables and fruit—and the challenge of turning those experiments into a method other people could use.
