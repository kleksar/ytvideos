In 1810, Nicolas Appert received an offer of 12,000 francs—but there was a condition. He had to publish a description of his food preservation method and provide the French government with 200 copies, while paying for printing himself.

The story begins with experiments in sealed glass vessels and follows the inspections, questions and decisions that turned years of work into a published guide.
