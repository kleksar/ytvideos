## Open evidence boundary

Historic England's Crossness pages describe release at “high tide,” while the Crossness Engines Trust history describes storage followed by release “on the ebb tide”; the accepted English narration uses the outgoing tide. Keep the accepted narration unchanged. Visually express it as seaward/outgoing current without adding a tide label or claiming an exact internal gate arrangement. Crossness outfall geometry, reservoir internal layout, and original Crossness pump connections remain reference-limited.
