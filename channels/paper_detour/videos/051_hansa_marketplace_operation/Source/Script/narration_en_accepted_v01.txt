After a rival dark-web marketplace shut down, daily visits to Hansa grew eightfold. Hansa was an online market for illegal goods. In June, Dutch police secretly took it over to attract users from that rival. Buyers didn’t know who controlled the site. During the operation, police recorded more than 50,000 transactions.
