A geometrically matched closing still gives the accepted narration a truthful visual loop: “The plan worked: After a rival dark-web marketplace shut down, daily visits to Hansa grew eightfold.” The viewer sees the shutdown, then immediately returns to the surge whose significance is clearer after learning who controlled Hansa.

## Why the story remains understandable

The sequence gives an unfamiliar viewer three clear relationships rather than a technical explanation: a closed rival accompanies a dramatic increase at Hansa; the inviting market is secretly controlled by Dutch police; and that hidden control results in recorded transactions and transferred address information before a public shutdown.

The evidence quantities have separate visual forms, the rival remains unnamed, and the covert control is communicated through theatrical geography rather than invented interface mechanics. The final shutdown completes the story on the first viewing, while the matched opening makes the spoken loop reinforce the result.
