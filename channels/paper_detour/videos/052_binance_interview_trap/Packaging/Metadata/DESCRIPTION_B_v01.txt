Binance stopped ordinary withdrawals after suspicious transfers were detected. Yet a separate permission on Ethereum remained active, allowing assets to move without the exchange's usual withdrawal system.

The breach began with a fake job interview. Binance estimated the stolen assets at about $6.34 billion and said around $2.85 billion was frozen or intercepted — not necessarily returned.
