- Selected PNGs are 1664×936, below the 4K target requested. They preserve 16:9 geometry and are upscaled to 2560×1440; they are not native 4K.
- The series uses a readable hook, dated estimate signs, interview and task props, human actions, a transaction ledger, route models and state comparisons. End-screen backgrounds deliberately reserve negative space for YouTube elements.
- Full-frame review caught ungrounded file-size/progress numbers in `S03_v02.png`; `S03_v03.png` removes them. It caught specific asset ticker labels in `17_v02.png`; `17_v03.png` uses a generic token route. It caught the same caller appearing in the recruiter and second-interview roles; `S24_v03.png` distinguishes the second caller visually.
- Preview `subtitle_sample_25s_v03.mp4` revealed a missing transparent VP9 decode and loop in the shared renderer. Renderer was corrected to loop the canonical Paper Frame and explicitly choose `libvpx-vp9`; the current implementation sample shows the diorama beneath the frame, with readable subtitles and no top-edge title loss. The 25-second sample predates `S24_v03.png`; that replacement is full-frame reviewed but its final composite remains pending.
