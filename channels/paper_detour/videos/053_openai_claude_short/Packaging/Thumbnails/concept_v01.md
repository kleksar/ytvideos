# Cover concept v01

- Viewer sees: a hand holding back the release switch beside a computer, plus the words “WHY HOLD CLAUDE BACK?”
- Understands: Claude was held back; the decision is the subject.
- Wants to know: why hold it back, and what happened while it waited?
- Film pays off: Anthropic's AI race and safety concerns, ChatGPT's release and Amodei's later assessment of the cost.
- The lever and computer are visual metaphors, not a claim about an actual interface or event. Title adds ChatGPT and the contrast without promising an outcome the film does not prove.
