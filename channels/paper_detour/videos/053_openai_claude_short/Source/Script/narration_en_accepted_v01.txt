Before ChatGPT took the world by storm, the team behind its future rival made a surprising choice: they held their own chatbot back.

One of them was Dario Amodei. He had worked on AI models at OpenAI, then left and co-founded a new company: Anthropic.

As his team developed an early version of Claude, they worried that releasing it could spark an AI race. If companies rushed to outdo one another, would they have enough time to make powerful systems safe?

So Anthropic waited.

Then, in November 2022, OpenAI released ChatGPT to the public. Anthropic introduced Claude the following March.

Years later, Amodei said waiting had probably cost Anthropic the chance to lead in AI for everyday users.
