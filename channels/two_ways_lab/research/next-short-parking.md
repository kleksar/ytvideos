Controlled scenario: hero reaches the same parking lot entry. Compare time from entry until arrival at the shop entrance, with equal 30 seconds to park and exit at either spot. First method: immediate space, 30 seconds to park/exit plus 90 seconds walking = 120 seconds. Second: drive/search for 120 seconds before reaching a close vacant spot, then 30 seconds to park/exit and 30 seconds walking = 180 seconds. Difference is 60 seconds. These are deliberately specified fictional timings; neither method is always faster. The takeaway is to compare the whole trip, not just the walk; parking spot availability and walking constraints vary.

Evidence for the kind of tradeoff: https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/trsc.32.1.30 discusses parking strategies that consider driving and walking time to the destination; it does not support the fictional timings.
