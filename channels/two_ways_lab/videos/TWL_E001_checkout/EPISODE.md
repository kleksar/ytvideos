## Story and model

A viewer chooses between two full carts and five small baskets. Two basket shoppers leave first, confirming the choice, but payment time reverses the outcome: both carts clear while one basket shopper remains. Under the explicit simplified model, two carts clear in 74 seconds and five baskets in 88, a 14 second difference. Scan time is two seconds per item and payment is 12 seconds per shopper; 25 items versus 14. [Events](experiment/events.json). The viewer learns that the number of transactions can outweigh the number of groceries. This is a model, not a claim about every real checkout.
