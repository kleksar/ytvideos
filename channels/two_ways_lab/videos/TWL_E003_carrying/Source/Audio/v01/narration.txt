Six things in the hallway. Two go to the bedroom, two to the kitchen, and two to the bathroom. Which way means less walking?

First, you take one thing at a time. Each time, you walk to its room and return for the next. All six things are put away, but you've made six trips.

Now reset. Put all six things in a basket. Take two to the bedroom and return. Then two to the kitchen, and finally two to the bathroom, returning after each trip. That's just three trips.

In this test, each room is ten meters away. The first way adds up to one hundred twenty meters. The basket? Sixty.

But the basket has to hold everything, and the things you need must be easy to reach. With a different home or heavy items, the result can change.
