Three slices of bread. One pan that fits only two. How fast can you toast all three?

Each side takes one minute. In this test, flipping takes no time.

First, put two slices in the pan. After a minute, flip them both. One more minute, and they're done.

But the third slice still needs both sides. That's two more minutes. Four minutes in total.

Now let's try again.

Start with two slices. After one minute, flip the first. Take the second out and put the third slice in its place.

After another minute, the first slice is done. Take it out. Put the second slice back, uncooked side down, and flip the third.

One more minute, and all three slices are toasted. Just three minutes.

The trick? You don't have to finish both slices before making room for the next one.
