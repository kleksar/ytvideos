What would you do if you had... Three gifts and two rolls of ribbon. Can you wrap them all?

The big gift needs four meters. Each small one needs three. One roll has six meters, the other has four. That's ten meters altogether. Surely that's enough!

You take the big roll and cut four meters for the big gift. Two meters left. Then you cut three meters from the other roll for a small gift. One meter left.

Two gifts are ready. But the last one needs a single three-meter piece. You only have two meters and one meter left, and you can't tie the pieces together.

Did you run out of ribbon? Let's try again.

For the big gift, use the short roll. It has exactly four meters. Now split the long roll in half: two three-meter pieces, one for each small gift.

Done! All three gifts are wrapped, and there's no ribbon left.

Next time, before you cut, check which pieces fit on each roll. Having enough ribbon in total isn't always enough. It matters how you divide it.
