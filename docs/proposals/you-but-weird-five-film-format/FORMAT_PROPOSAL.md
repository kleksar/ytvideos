## Current ending rhythm

1. Complete the story without altering its outcome; preserve an emotional pause and unmistakable graphic separation.
2. Deliver the approved future-story riddle in a standalone meaningful vignette, with an invitation to guess (for example the on-screen `Leave your guess in the comments.`). Measure the natural approved recording. This is not part of the ten-second end screen.
3. Only after the riddle and invitation, show a separate full **10 seconds** reserved for the **previous** story's platform video element. Do not bake a fake clickable card into the MP4. Do not reveal the future story through this element.
4. Next opening: original hook first, then the short approved reveal, then resume the story. The approximate four-to-six-second reveal budget is checked against actual speech; do not speed up or revise approved words without scope.

Previous destinations: Everyone -> Mind; Teleportation -> Everyone; Wishes -> Teleportation; Rewind -> Wishes. A later cyclic Mind edition may point to Rewind only when verified available; the first chronological pilot has no fictitious prior episode.

The visual descriptions below specify riddle composition, not a shared ten-second slot. Any old `0–5s`/`5–10s` shorthand is superseded by this sequence. Preserve source masters; perform only authorized migrations.

## 1. Mind Reading → Everyone Wants You

**Story stays:** opponent chooses what you read; “You've lost everything” remains. Do not alter finances or reveal a new power in the poker room.

**Outro EN:**
> Strangers see your face. Suddenly, nobody wants you to leave. Why?

**По-русски:** чужое внимание превращается в притяжение. Мы не называем желание и не обещаем любовь. Это намеренно не математически однозначная загадка: гипноз или навязчивое внимание тоже разумные версии.

**Riddle visual:** poker composition closes completely. In a separate cream-background vignette, a lone adult silhouette walks past three adults; their heads turn. No heart eyes, kiss, mirror, bathroom or title of the next video yet. Hold the question for the measured riddle; the previous-video end screen follows as a separate full ten seconds. Motion remains subtle, not a literal escape or ability upgrade.

**Reveal in Everyone, after its original hook / before the birthday-bar setup (4–6s):**
> The clue was desire. They don't want you to leave. They want you.

**RU:** разгадка короткая, различает влечение и любовь; не раскрывает зеркало. On-screen `DESIRE`, not “you all guessed correctly.” If the existing hook already states the premise, this line is acknowledgement of the previous clue, not a second surprise.

## 2. Everyone Wants You → Teleportation

**Outro EN:**

**По-русски:** пропущен сам путь. Можно угадать телепортацию, мгновенное перемещение или портал; не дисквалифицировать близкую разумную догадку.

**RU:** ответ про способность, не про механизм копирования. Label `TELEPORTATION`.

**RU:** раскрываем только тему, не второе желание и не трагедию. Label `THREE WISHES`.

**По-русски:** реальность откатилась, память осталась. Это намёк на возврат времени, не на стирание чужой памяти; точный десятисекундный предел раскрывается уже в следующем ролике.

**Visual:** independent sketch of a fallen pencil returning beside a cup, with a clock hand slipping back slightly. No app power menu, underwater car, rescuer or suggestion the wife can now be saved by rewinding.

**RU:** даём понятную разгадку и предел способности, но не разжёвываем правило намеренной команды раньше его штатного места. Label `TEN SECONDS BACK`.

## Release phases and spoiler control

Before the future story is released, do not expose its answer through the riddle artwork, premature title/thumb/card/pin/playlist, or a false watch-now promise. After it is public, viewers may discover the answer elsewhere; do not pretend it remains secret or fabricate an upcoming-release competition. In both phases the final separate ten-second end screen still recommends the PREVIOUS story, not the future answer. Verify the previous video's real URL and availability before configuring Studio. Its actual card is platform state, not something this docs update or a baked placeholder creates.

Optional pinned prompt: `What power is hidden in the clue? Leave your guess and the detail that convinced you.` Actual audience acknowledgements require real contributions; no invented users, votes, winners or deadlines. When all five stories are available, the forward riddle cycle remains an editorial game, while end-screen recommendations point backward. These are deliberately different directions.

YouTube's documented end-screen timing and availability constraints remain relevant: [official YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en). Verify the actual configured element in Studio. No essential story conclusion depends on it.

## Opening-answer choices and honesty

The original hook often reveals the power already. Therefore the short `LAST CLUE` module explicitly connects clue to answer; it is not staged as a delayed shocking reveal. To avoid repetition, test the 4–6-second module against simply letting the hook answer the riddle. The owner's requested explicit answer is the approved format default here.

Do not insert names unless there are real relevant contributions and permission for their display. Author examples remain clearly labelled, with no fake accounts, likes or invented independent viewers. No useful comments means no winner panel; it does not block the next story. Keep moral dilemmas optional in a separate thread, not in the spoken riddle or reveal.
