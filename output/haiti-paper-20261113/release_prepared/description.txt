A woman posted a house in Haiti and asked Nayib Bukele to destroy it. The president of El Salvador replied asking for the exact address. Four days after his reply, he published strike footage and said his promise had been kept.

Public reports do not establish whether the commenters’ coordinates guided the operation or whether the photographed house was the one destroyed. Told through original paper-style illustrations.
